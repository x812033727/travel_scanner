Before Erniu's hoe can land, a long staff sweeps in from the side and knocks it flying. Qin Shuang stands between the two mobs: "Fight, and you hand him his excuse." One by one the Qian staves come down, and the two villages don't come to blows. The deep pool below Liuxi still holds water, but the water is low and the fields are high, so Xu Yan says just two words: dig wells. Under a blazing sun, twelve well pits line up in a row; by day three not one has hit water. Xu Yan ties on a rope and goes down himself, digs all day, and comes up caked in mud, then a second later topples straight backward. Qin Shuang hoists him onto her shoulder and strides across the threshing yard, calling him a "soft bookworm" even as her ears turn red. Affection 41. The gold reward chest clicks open: blueprints for a waterwheel. Old Shi the carpenter reads them and says only, "Three hundred catties of good timber. Where do we find that?" The village's big trees were all cut down last year; only one piece of timber that size is left: the beam of the ancestral hall. At dusk, Elder Han leads the whole village to kneel before the ancestral tablets: "We take down the hall's beam, so the children get a mouthful of water." Every head turns toward Xu Yan, standing in the doorway. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 32.
