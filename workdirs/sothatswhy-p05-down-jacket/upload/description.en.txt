A down jacket makes no heat at all. So why does it keep you warm? | So That's Why｜CC Subtitles

On a cold-snap morning you take your down jacket out of the closet, and it feels cold.
In this episode, the Chief and Little Q follow one down jacket to find the heater: where the heat comes from, why it keeps running away, why the iron railing in a park feels colder than the wooden bench in the same park, what blocks heat best, and why down stops keeping you warm when it's squashed, soaked or blown through by wind. At the end, a vacuum flask and a wrapped-up ice cube show what keeping warm really does.

It has no plug and no battery, and it makes no heat of its own. So why does it keep you warm once you put it on?

The illustrations in this episode were made with AI. The power figures and multiples are typical textbook values or rough orders of magnitude; the source of every claim is listed in the references below.
