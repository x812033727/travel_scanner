Same sunlight: why is the sky blue, but the sunset red? | So That's Why｜CC Subtitles

At noon on the rooftop, the sky is a bright, shining blue.
In this episode, the Chief and Little Q follow one beam of sunlight: which colors hide in white light, how air bounces blue light in every direction, why the sky isn't violet, why evening light travels a much longer path, how a glass of water with a little milk holds both a blue sky and a sunset, and why clouds are white. At the end, they stand on the Moon and look up.

Go back up at dusk, and the sun has turned into a big red ball. It's the same sunlight shining down, and air is clear, so where do the colors come from?

The illustrations in this episode were made with AI. The multiples and distances are rough orders of magnitude from teaching materials; the source of every claim is listed in the references below.
