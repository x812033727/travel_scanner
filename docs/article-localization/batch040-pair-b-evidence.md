## Editorial and structural review

Each target document translates the title, description, all 33 ordered blocks, headings, lists, callouts, table cells, image alt/caption/description, and source titles. The staged structural audit confirms the same block shapes, five ecommerce and four zero-click source URLs and exact `checked_on` dates, image credits, and seven remaining exact `(kind, slug)` `ArticleInline` targets. Those targets remain publication-aware; translated link text does not claim that its destination locale is already published.

The ecommerce article uses an original desktop storage box example, not a tested product or a deployment. The text keeps variant URL fragments versus query parameters, single-page versus multipage canonical choices, the `ProductGroup`/`Product` relationship, merchant listings versus product snippets, actual price/stock/image consistency, and the limit that Rich Results Test cannot guarantee factual accuracy or special display. These points were checked against Google's [product structured-data overview](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product), [product variant guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product-variants), [ecommerce URL guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/designing-a-url-structure-for-ecommerce-sites), [merchant listing guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing), and [structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies).

The zero-click article uses an original clothing-alterations shop scenario, not observed business data or a market-wide zero-click percentage. All locales preserve the illustrative one thousand impressions, fifty clicks, five-percent site CTR, and the prohibition on inferring that ninety-five percent of searches had no website click. They also distinguish Business Profile call-button clicks from completed calls or jobs, acknowledge possible Ads/organic mixing, and avoid claiming guaranteed featured snippets or attribution. These checks were compared with Google's [Search Console metrics definitions](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7042828?hl=en), [featured-snippet documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/featured-snippets), [Business Profile performance definitions](https://support.google.com/business/answer/9918094), and [generative AI controls](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16908024).
