## Execution and preservation

1. Reuse reviewed translations whose source, documents and assets still match.
2. Translate travel information and guides before the remaining life articles.
3. Work in waves of at most 20 articles, with narrow content and release tickets.
4. Review every new document and text image independently, binding exact hashes.
5. Publish only eligible missing locales after bundle guards and required release
   checks; refresh coverage from production after every wave.

The baseline reports 251 repository/database document differences. The current
database publication is authoritative. Affected articles need a recorded
preservation or source-correction decision before installation; a translation
must not silently rewrite the source or an existing locale.

Active concurrent article/source scopes, held hubs, private and expired articles
remain in the queue with their concrete dependency rather than being overwritten.
Model quota or authentication failures stop new jobs without automatic retry.
