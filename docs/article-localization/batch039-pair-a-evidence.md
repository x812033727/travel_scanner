## Editorial and link review

- Business models keeps the fictional wooden-storage brand as an illustration. B2B and B2C describe the buyer/transaction relationship; D2C is direct brand-to-consumer selling within B2C, not a separate customer type. The translation retains purchasing roles, sales cycles, delivery and service responsibility, the limits of customer data, and the distinction among inquiry, qualified demand, confirmed order, and completed delivery.
- Five Forces keeps its fictional small recording-studio example. It first defines the relevant industry and customer job, then treats existing rivals, new entrants, supplier power, buyer power, and substitutes as separate structural questions. Entry barriers are more than equipment cost; price is only one buyer term. The model helps explain competitive pressure, but positioning still needs demand evidence and delivery capability.
- An independent source review found no blocking citation or article-target defect for these two packs. All source URLs and `checked_on` dates remain exactly as in the source; titles are translated.
