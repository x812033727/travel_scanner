## 查過、沒問題的主要主張

- **Anthropic 把 temperature、top_p、top_k 標為棄用；較新模型只接受 1.0，其他值回 400**：Create a Message 參考頁 temperature 欄位標 "Deprecated"，原文 "Deprecated. Models released after [特定型號] do not support setting temperature. A value of 1.0 will be accepted for backwards compatibility, all other values will be rejected with a 400 error." 及 "Defaults to 1.0. Ranges from 0.0 to 1.0."；top_k "Deprecated. Models released after [特定型號] do not accept top_k; any value will be rejected with a 400 error."；top_p "Deprecated. … A value >= 0.99 will be accepted for backwards compatibility, all other values will be rejected with a 400 error." 正文「只接受預設值 1.0，其他值回 400 錯誤」成立；正文以「較新的模型」代替頁面上的型號，符合系列規矩。｜https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/api/messages/create
- **Anthropic：溫度 0 也不完全確定**：同頁原文 "Note that even with temperature of 0.0, the results will not be fully deterministic."；分析與創作的建議原文 "Use temperature closer to 0.0 for analytical / multiple choice, and closer to 1.0 for creative and generative tasks."｜同上
- **OpenAI：不要同時調 temperature 與 top_p**：Create chat completion 參考 temperature 欄位 "We generally recommend altering this or top_p but not both."，top_p 欄位 "We generally recommend altering this or temperature but not both."；範圍 "between 0 and 2"；top_p 說明 "An alternative to sampling with temperature, called nucleus sampling"。頁面確實沒寫原因，正文標成「本文的解讀」正確。｜https://developers.openai.com/api/reference/resources/chat/subresources/completions/methods/create
- **OpenAI seed 欄位**：原文 "Deprecated." "This feature is in Beta." "our system will make a best effort to sample deterministically … Determinism is not guaranteed"。｜同上
- **OpenAI：推理開啟時移除 temperature、top_p**：最新模型指南 Migration 區原文 "Unsupported parameters: When reasoning effort is not none, remove temperature, top_p, and top_logprobs."（這段在該頁針對當前旗艦系列的遷移說明裡；正文寫「新模型指南」、不列型號，成立。）｜https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/latest-model
- **Google：較新一代建議維持預設**：原文 "we strongly recommend keeping them at their default values for Gemini 3.x models. - **Google：低溫較確定、高溫較多樣；先 topK、再 topP、最後溫度取樣**：原文 "Lower temperatures are good for prompts that require a more deterministic or less open-ended response, while higher temperatures can lead to more diverse or creative results." 及 "the topK tokens with the highest probabilities are sampled. - **Google：溫度範圍 0–2、預設值依模型而異**：GenerationConfig 原文 "Note: The default value varies by model" "Values can range from [0.0, 2.0]."；三家範圍「0 到 1 或 0 到 2」成立（Anthropic 0.0–1.0，OpenAI 0–2，Google 0.0–2.0）。｜https://ai.google.dev/api/generate-content
- **Hinton 等人：t 通常為 1，越高越軟**：原文 "where T is a temperature that is normally set to 1. Using a higher value for T produces a softer probability distribution over classes."｜https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.02531
