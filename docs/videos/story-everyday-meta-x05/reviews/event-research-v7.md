# Meta X05 v7 — 原始年報事件補充（2026-10-20）

獨立查核 FY2022、FY2023、FY2025 SEC Form 10-K 的本地 HTML，對照現有 `source-notes.md` 與 `drafts/lines.tsv`。以下是可補進 FY22→FY23→FY25 敘事的五組事件；口播是建議文字，不是已核准的稿件。原始文件：[M22](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680123000013/meta-20221231.htm)、[M23](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000132680124000012/meta-20231231.htm)、[M25](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1326801/000162828026003942/meta-20251231.htm)。本地核對檔依序為 `/tmp/x03-meta-2022-10k.htm`、`/tmp/meta-2023.html`、`/tmp/meta-2025.html`。

1. **FY2022 重組有第三季、十一月、十二月的先後。** M22，Item 8, Note 3, *Restructuring*： “Beginning in the third quarter of 2022, as a part of our facilities consolidation strategy, we made a decision to sublease, early terminate, or abandon several office buildings under operating leases to align our real property lease arrangements with our anticipated operating needs.” 接著是 “In November 2022, we announced a layoff of approximately 11,000 of our employees across the FoA and RL segments.” 最後是 “In December 2022, we reevaluated our data center investment strategy to improve efficiency and further advance our efforts around artificial intelligence. As a result, we decided to pivot several of our data center building projects to a next generation design while also canceling multiple existing data center projects.” **口播選項：**「第三季，Meta 決定縮辦公空間：部分租下的樓準備轉租、提前解約或放棄。十一月裁員宣布前，重組先碰到辦公室。」「十二月，資料中心也轉向：為了效率和 AI，Meta 決定把部分建案改成下一代設計，另取消多個既有建案。」**口徑：**辦公室、建案是當時的決定，裁員是十一月的宣布；年報記有相關資產減損／放棄費用，但不能說所有租約退出、裁員離職或新建案都在當年完成。不要把 2025 年 AI 費用直接歸因於 2022 年這次決定。

2. **FY2023 再宣布三輪裁員，年底人數才顯著下降。** M23，Item 8, Note 3, *2023 Restructuring*： “In March 2023, we announced three rounds of planned layoffs to further reduce our company size by approximately 10,000 employees across the Family of Apps (FoA) and Reality Labs (RL) segments (the 2023 Restructuring).” 同段說 “As of December 31, 2023, we have completed these employee layoffs.” *Executive Overview of Full Year 2023 Results*：“Headcount was 67,317 as of December 31, 2023, a decrease of 22% year-over-year.” **口播選項：**「隔年三月，Meta 再宣布三輪裁員，計畫縮減約一萬人；到了年底，全公司員工比前一年少了百分之二十二。」**口徑：**22% 是全公司年末人數變化，不能全歸因於三月裁員；年報也說少數受影響員工仍計入 2024 年人數。

3. **FY2023 廣告變多的方式，解釋了平均價格仍下跌。** M23，Item 7, *Results of Operations—Revenue—Advertising*： “The increase in the ads delivered during 2023 was driven by increases in the number and frequency of ads displayed across our products and an increase in users.” “The decrease in average price per ad was driven by an increase in the number of ads delivered, especially in geographies and in products, such as Reels, that monetize at lower rates.” 同節指出展示數增加 28%、平均單價下降 9%、廣告收入增加 16%，均相對 FY2022。**口播選項：**「Meta 說，展示更頻繁、用戶也變多；Reels 等變現率較低的版位接下更多廣告，量上升，平均單價卻仍下滑。」**口徑：**Reels 是年報列出的產品例子之一，並非唯一原因或有單品價格可核。M23 的 *Marketing and sales* 另說該費用比 FY2022 少 29.6 億美元，主因包含推廣及人事費下降；人事費下降與該職能的年末人數減少有關。年報沒有說這 29.6 億全由三月裁員帶來，不能把第 2 點與此費用直接畫等號。

4. **FY2025 廣告量價同升，與 FY2023 形成清楚對照。** M25，Item 7, *Results of Operations—Revenue—Advertising*： “Advertising revenue in 2025 increased $35.54 billion, or 22%, compared to 2024 due to increases in ad impressions delivered and average price per ad.” 同節：“In 2025, ad impressions delivered increased by 12%, as compared with an increase of 11% in 2024, year-over-year.” “In 2025, the average price per ad increased by 9%, as compared with an increase of 10% in 2024, year-over-year.” **口播選項：**「二〇二五年，廣告送達數增加一成二，平均每則廣告價格也漲百分之九；這次量和價一起推高收入。」**口徑：**12%、9%、22% 都是 FY2025 對 FY2024 的廣告指標，不能寫成 FY2023→FY2025 逐年連續成長，也不是 FoA 整體收入的增幅。對價格原因，年報寫 “we believe” 廣告定位及成效衡量工具的改進提高需求；若口播此說法，須保留「公司認為」。

5. **FY2025 有具體眼鏡產品；未來費用傾向穿戴裝置。** M25，Item 1, *Business—Reality Labs Products*： “In 2025, we introduced Meta Ray-Ban Display, which combines our AI glasses with an integrated display built into the lens. It comes with the Meta Neural Band, which is a wrist-worn wearable device that uses electromyography to let people control their AI glasses using neuromuscular signals.” **口播選項：**「二〇二五年，Meta 推出 Ray-Ban Display：鏡片內有顯示器，腕帶能讀取肌肉訊號來控制眼鏡。」同段另說：“In 2026, we expect to spend approximately 70% of our Reality Labs operating expenses on our wearables initiatives, and the remaining 30% on our VR and Horizon initiatives.” **口徑：**年報沒有提供這一款眼鏡的銷量或利潤，也沒有說它造成 AI 眼鏡整體銷售成長。70/30 是 FY2026 **RL 營業費用預期分配**，不是 FY2025 已支出、收入或產品利潤；共享成本分攤需判斷，預期也可能變動。
