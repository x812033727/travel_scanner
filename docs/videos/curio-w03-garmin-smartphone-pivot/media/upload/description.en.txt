Did Phones Crush 70% of Garmin's Business? So How Did Revenue Double｜Curio Files

The story goes: free navigation on phones crushed 70% of Garmin's business. This episode opens its annual reports for fiscal 2008 through 2025 to see the day "could slow our growth" became "has caused our growth to stop," what happened to the phone it built itself, the other number it published that same day, and how many years revenue took to catch up.

"Crushed by phones" is marked as the popular version: the same reports also name built-in navigation in new cars, and 2009 also brought the recession and a price war. That phones stopped its growth is Garmin's own wording in its annual report. The Counterpoint smartwatch ranking covers only Q1 2022, as reported by 9to5Mac; the full-year figure of about $8.15 billion is the company's own forecast.
