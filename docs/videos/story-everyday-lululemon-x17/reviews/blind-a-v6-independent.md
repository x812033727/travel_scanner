## Standalone Shorts read

**Short 1 — coherent standalone, with a weaker final image.** Its eight narrated beats state the year and restricted batch (“二〇一三年，公司撤下部分黑色 Luon 褲”), give the conditional company finding (“布料可能符合原檢驗標準”), add the low-end range, incomplete tests and pattern changes, and end with a garment-level takeaway. A viewer need not know the main film. The conditional “可能” and “部分” survive in spoken text. The `payoff` uses shot `x17-b04-s26`, whose prompt shows a box facing an empty rack; it does not depict the finished-trouser check named in “看布樣之外，最後還得看做好的那條褲子.” A new side-by-side finished garment inspection image, kept in the 9:16 safe corridor, would make that takeaway visible. This is a visual repair, not a missing narrative premise.

**Short 2 — coherent standalone, with a date cue worth tightening.** Its hook asks why company revenue increased despite the withdrawal. The turn explicitly says the US$57–67m figure is a forecast, the proof identifies US$17.5m as an inventory provision, and the +21% beat names company-wide revenue; the final beat resolves how those can coexist. Its opening and “三月預估” do not say *which* March in the spoken Short; the description supplies 2013 but the film should stand on its audio. Change the setup narration to “二〇一三年三月預估，全年將少賣五千七到六千七百萬美元.” The `x17-b03-s08` rack gap under the forecast graphic is usable because the headline says “三月當時預估”; preserve that label onscreen so viewers do not mistake the figure for a realized loss.

Neither Short needs the main video to resolve its own question. Their approximately 138 and 141 CJK narration characters are compact enough to play as Shorts; no audio duration has been measured in this review.
