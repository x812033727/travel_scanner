## Listener and edit notes

The mechanism is understandable once `[x170401]`–`[x170410]` connect possibly compliant fabric at the low end of the old tolerance, incompletely tested variables, and small pattern changes. `[x170411]` finally gives the listener a physical picture of a flat swatch versus a stretched pant leg. That picture is the strongest explanatory moment, but the estimated timeline does not reach the April finding until **05:07**. The opening lasts 00:41; the pre-incident second chapter then lasts to 03:13, including several passes through suppliers, sewing, and delivery (`[x170208]`–`[x170215]`) before the 2012 financial figures. A new listener can lose the central opacity question while waiting for the event.

The chronology is recoverable: FY2012 closes in early February 2013, the implicated batch is sold from March 1, withdrawal is announced March 18, the production update is April 3, FY2013 Q1 closes May 5, June announces the Q1 result/replacement status, and the full-year result arrives the following March. Yet `[x170501] 缺貨拖了那季後七週` can sound like **seven weeks after** the quarter, not the **last seven weeks within** it. Short 2 makes the same ambiguity stronger with `店裡與網站，第一季後七週都缺這批貨。` Change both to `第一季最後七週` (with the natural surrounding sentence adjusted). This preserves the distinction between Q1's seven-week shortage and the later “within 90 days” return in `[x170510]`.

The cost account is carefully distinguished from the March forecast: `[x170317]` is a projected FY2013 sales shortfall, while `[x170505]`–`[x170506]` is the US$17.5m inventory provision booked in cost of goods sold. The Q1 +21% company revenue and lower gross-margin percentage can coexist; `[x170522]` correctly avoids assigning the whole margin decline to these pants. The annual coda, though, says in several forms that company-wide totals cannot isolate black-pants loss (`[x170604]`, `[x170607]`–`[x170608]`, `[x170611]`–`[x170613]`). One clear revenue caveat and one gross-margin caveat would keep the analytical point without a second run through the same limitation. `伸展模數`, `公差`, and `容許範圍低端` also ask more of a casual listener than their short nearby explanations provide; the stretched-leg image can do more of that work.

The file has 112 scenes, 118 spoken lines, and 16 declared loops; `narrativeProblems` with `TIGHT_CADENCE` reports no warnings or errors. Those counts explain why the structural check passes, but they do not resolve the delayed reveal or repeated information. Trim repeated setup and annual caveats before relying on a faster voice.
