## Shorts as standalone narrations

**Short 1 — YES, 8/10 standalone.** It names the affected product and event in the setup:「二〇一三年，公司撤下部分黑色 Luon 褲。」Its own hook asks「布料可能達標，做成褲子怎麼還是太透？」; the subsequent spoken beats qualify that this is the company's investigation, identify the low-end fabric and incomplete testing, add subtle pattern changes, then pay off with「看布樣之外，最後還得看做好的那條褲子。」The “可能” wording and “部分” scope survive without the long film. The middle three proof beats could be spoken more briskly, but the Short closes its own question.

**Short 2 — YES, 7/10 standalone.** Its opening asks「黑褲下架，公司營收為何還能增加？」The setup explicitly calls US$57–67m a March forecast, the turn says「那是當時的預測，不是最後量到的損失。」The cost beat records US$17.5m as inventory provision, and the revenue beat says「第一季全公司營收增兩成一，其他商品也在賣。」Its payoff makes the two simultaneous outcomes plain:「所以總收入能增，黑褲的存貨準備卻仍列成本。」The budget of eight beats spends several beats explaining forecast versus charge before directly answering the revenue hook; a tighter cut could put the “other products” answer one beat earlier. It is still understandable without a click to the long video.
