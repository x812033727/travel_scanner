## Prompt and acceptance guidance

1. The source episode concerns late Eastern Han Jiangdong. Put a **positive period anchor in each scene prompt**: one named-role historical figure with tied-up hair/topknot or cloth headwrap, wrapped cross-collar long robe and, for soldiers, a simple lamellar vest; timber halls, woven screens, low wooden tables, earthen paths, canvas tents, wooden warships, timber gates or plank granaries. This is a plausible illustrative vocabulary, not a claim about exact appearance.
2. Replace abstract locations like “command hall,” “council hall,” “road,” “storehouse gates,” and “river map” with the concrete period setting and action above. The current `video.json` has already repaired several of these prompts, including `approval`, `succession-hook`, `sunce-dies`, `why-surrender`, `water-sickness`, `request-thirty`, `city-grain`, `ning-ahead`, `rescue-request`, `ning-freed`, `right-rib`, and `walks-camp`; **judge the next outputs, not these stale initial JPEGs**. `same-age`, `debate`, `zhou-argues`, and `given-command` still deserve precise period/action wording when redrawn.
3. One foreground historical person and one action per shot. A horse, distant boats or faint troop silhouettes are okay as context. Leave maps and petitions unlettered. Blank paper itself is plausible in this era; the blocker is the modern folder, printed full-color map, classroom, office furniture or surfaced road.
4. During human QA, explicitly reject peaked caps/visors with badges, shoulder boards, neckties, modern military uniforms, printed cartography, classroom/auditorium seating, roller shutters, glazed office fronts, road lane markings, and the 208 imperial crown. For wound scenes, check the **actual action** against the narration. The nine-category auto-judge did not catch these errors even at score 10.
5. Keep `look: {"preset":"cute-mystery"}` as the series style; the local curio skill specifies no added `style` or `negative` field. Express period and composition as scene content in `data.prompt`. Do not assert that the cartoon is archaeological evidence. Continue to mark the Zhuge Liang letter/death sequence as the later novel layer only.
