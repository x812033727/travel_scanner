## Story and Shorts notes

The recurring anonymous card gives the list a visible object, and its separation into two stacks at `[x160503]` clarifies that the January 2009 announcement is a second cohort. It is less effective when a run of card sentences only restates that it is illustrative: `[x160302b]` says it is no Seattle address, `[x160317]` says a real address cannot be assigned a reason, and `[x160317c]` says the card does not prove a real store. Keep one such limit, then use the card to mark a new decision or actual closure. The first January-to-July transition is understandable; the five chapter joins listed under Q5 are locally clear. The risk is accumulated time before the final 588 payoff, especially after the story begins following same-store sales.

The late return to the same-store figures repeats methodological caution already introduced at `[x160419] 同店只算開滿十三個月的直營店。` and `[x160420] 才開不久的新店，不在這張同店表。` The explicit callback `[x160603] 還記得去年那張同店帳？關門的店不在裡面。` adds a different exclusion, and `[x160605]` adds the 53rd-week qualification; these may be needed for the comparison, but they defer the selected-store payoff. The exact three-line outro has the right jobs: `[x160641]` supplies the 588 result, `[x160642] 你會先查眼前獲利，還是未來回報？` asks a related comment question, and `[x160643]` offers a reason to subscribe. Its first line needs the explicit callback described in Q8.

**Short 1** stands alone: the `x16-b01-s01` and `x16-b01-s03` beats establish the announcement and the roughly 70% selected-store figure, and the `x16-b03-s08`–`s10` beats answer the selection question. Its `x16-b03-s05` narration, `先看分母：七成是這六百家「入選店」裡的比例。`, is followed by `x16-b03-s02`, `它不是說全美門市七成很新，也不是所有新店七成都失敗。` Both are accurate, but two of eight beats repeat the denominator warning after the setup already says `這些入選店裡`. Combine those warnings into one beat and give the recovered beat to the selection/reversal payoff.

**Short 2** also stands alone: `x16-b05-s13` gives FY2009 `−6%`, `x16-b06-s04` gives FY2010 `+7%`, and `x16-b06-s08` ends with `同時有多種改變，不能把正七％全算給關店。` Its 13-month and 53rd-week qualifications support that specific short-form comparison. This is a stronger home for the full FY2010 sales thread than the long video's final nine scenes.
