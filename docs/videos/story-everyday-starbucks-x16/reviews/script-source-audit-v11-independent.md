## Claim check

**HOLD — January target's denominator (`x160213b`–`x160213c`; S13).** The script says, “美國新店的開店目標，也被往下修。從約一千六百，改為約一千一百七十五。” S13 says Schultz had slowed U.S. “store growth ... to approximately 1,175 stores ... down from a revised target of 1,600 stores.” The same release then explicitly calls the components **“Net new store openings ... approximately 650 company-operated locations and 525 licensed in the United States”**: 650 + 525 = 1,175. Its roughly 100 U.S. underperforming closures are part of the contemporaneous *net* global store target. The script's `新店的開店目標` can be heard as gross openings, and the frozen source matrix repeats that imprecise label. Say **FY2008 U.S. net-new store target** when presenting 1,600→1,175; keep S2's separate FY2009 forecast of *fewer than 200 new U.S. company-operated stores* distinct. S13's circa-100 U.S. closures are a **plan**, not an actual count.

**Other January/July setup — supported (`x160101`–`x160332`; S1/S2/S13/S14).** S1 dates Schultz's immediate CEO return and the board's plan to slow U.S. growth, close some underperformers, improve experience/products/design/training, and execute rather than promise an overnight fix. S2 explicitly says the July approximately 600 U.S. company-operated stores **include the previously targeted 100**; approximately 70% of *selected stores* opened since the start of FY2006, with selection criteria including store-level profitability, prospective acceptable return, site/market factors, and economic trends. The announcement does not list store age as a selection rule or disclose an individual store's weighted score. S2 schedules most closures for the balance of FY2008/FY2009 H1 subject to third-party agreements, anticipates elimination of full- and part-time positions, and **expects** nearby placement of many workers rather than reporting completed placement. S14 acknowledges effects on workers, customers, and communities. The anonymous shop card and apron are illustrations, not a factual named-store reconstruction.

**Estimate versus recognized charge versus shut door — supported (`x160323a`–`x160401`; S2/S14/S3).** S2 estimates lease termination/future obligations at **$120–140m**, asset write-offs around **$200m expected in FY2008 Q3**, severance about **$8m**, and aggregate pretax charges **$328–348m**; it expects nearly all lease costs in FY2008 Q4/FY2009 H1. S14's July 30 Q3 release records **$167.7m pretax restructuring charges comprised of asset impairments** for the July approximately-600 group. S14 also says the then-current opening target **assumes approximately 200 of 600 closures in Q4**; S3 later documents Q4 as when the action began and **205 actually closed** by FY2008 year-end. The script distinguishes the Q3 booked impairment from subsequent doors closing. S6 independently supplies Q4 **$38.6m** restructuring charges related to executing the July closure decision, a raised lease-exit-cost ceiling of **up to $170m** from $120–140m, and then-expected roughly 400 remaining closures by FY2009 year-end. These are different measures and dates, as voiced.

**FY2008 totals and comparable-store population — supported (`x160401`–`x160423i`; S3/S6/S9).** S3's U.S. company-operated table gives FY2007 net +1,065 and ending 6,793, FY2008 net +445 and ending 7,238; its footnote says openings are **net of closures**. Comparable U.S. sales show FY2007 **+4%** and FY2008 **−5%**, with the definition limited to company-operated stores open at least **13 months**. These are nationwide net/eligible-cohort figures, not the July selected group's closure tally. S9 gives FY2009 Q1 **179 additional July-group closures, 384 cumulative**, plus **$53.7m** quarterly restructuring charges and remaining lease costs expected with later closures.

**Separate January 2009 group and quarter-by-quarter July cohort — supported (`x160424`–`x160527`; S12/S9/S10/S11).** S12 attributes the new action to the current difficult operating environment and ongoing review, plans about **300 additional stores globally, about 200 in the U.S.**, and explicitly says they are **in addition** to the July U.S. 600/Australian 61. S10 gives July-cohort Q2 **119 additional, 503 cumulative**, with the January U.S. group separately closing four. S11 gives July-cohort Q3 **60 additional, 563 cumulative**; its sentence about **remaining U.S. closures by FY2009 year-end** follows both U.S. groups, so the script correctly presents it as a then-current overall U.S. expectation rather than a completed store-by-store result. The falling quarterly additions (179→119→60) are a direct arithmetic observation, not a cited causal claim.

**Lease timing and final 588 — supported (`x160601`–`x160641`; S4/S3).** The FY2009 10-K states that when a lease specifies a landlord termination fee, expense is recorded upon written notice; negotiated fees are recorded upon signing; when early exit is refused but subletting is allowed, projected sublease income reduces the estimated excess remaining rent. Its restructuring footnote says some termination agreements were finalized in one period for stores closing in a later period, and those fees are amortized from agreement date to closure. The annual report attributes **higher companywide** lease exit and related costs to **more actual store closures companywide**; the script does not pass this off as the July cohort's isolated dollar amount. S4 explicitly states **383** July-cohort closures in FY2009 and **588** total for that action; the separately announced January U.S. group closed **183** and is excluded. S3 supplies the earlier **205**, and 205 + 383 = 588. That final result appears in `x160641` and the outro card.

**Shorts, cards, and publishing — supported apart from the target label above.** Short 1 confines 70% to the selected July group and says the release **did not list** store age as a criterion. Short 2's FY2009 U.S. comparable sales **−6%** (S7), FY2010 **+7%** with U.S. transactions **+3%** and average value **+4%** (S5/S8), ≥13-month company-operated cohort and 53rd-week exclusion (S5) all match; it asks about attribution and correctly declines to give closures sole credit. S5 mentions labor efficiency as another company action. The seven main stats cards and outro card use the sourced denominators, periods, and estimate/actual qualifiers; the thumbnail asks a question supported by S2's 70% cohort fact. `video.json` contains 103 scenes—95 illustration shots, seven stats cards, one outro—matching `visual-plan.md`; its 149 narrated lines match `drafts/narration.md` by ID and text. The video description's 14 SEC URLs equal the source matrix/video source list, Short source links are subsets, all Short `shot` IDs resolve to a main scene, and `youtube.video_id` is null. The publishing handoff appropriately describes **preproduction**, with no claim that media or a YouTube upload already exists. Visuals remain conceptual; no generated-image or audio result was available for this fact audit.
