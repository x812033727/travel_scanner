## 三個高風險數字與結局

1. **年份：** 主敘事明寫的 `August, 1848` 指妻兒被賣那天 [G L1259–1260](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L1259-L1260)；箱子旅程本身**沒有標明年、月、日**。序言所列讚美詩署 1849-09-01 [G L237](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L237)，不能單憑它把箱子之旅定為 1849 年的某一天；若題名或旁白要斷言「1849 年逃脫」，另附有日期的獨立來源。更不能把 1848 年 8 月寫成入箱日。
2. **27 小時：** 布朗的主敘事未給總路程時長；`TWENTY SEVEN HOURS` 是書末 **Samuel J. May 演說摘錄** [G L2349–2365](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L2349-L2365)。可說「書末 May 說約 27 小時」，不可稱「布朗說自己待了精確 27 小時」。他自述輪船上倒置「似乎」一個半小時、後聽到有人說兩小時，[G L1522–1539](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L1522-L1539)，也別當精確碼表。
3. **箱子尺寸：** 書名頁稱 `3 feet long and 2 wide` [G L40–48](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L40-L48)；布朗敘事稱「three feet by two」，沒有高度 [G L1508–1509](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L1508-L1509)；May 稱親自量得 **3 英尺 1 英寸長、2 英尺寬、2 英尺 6 英寸深** [G L2354–2356](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L2354-L2356)，書末圖說第三維稱 high [G L2383–2388](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L2383-L2388)。若給三維精確數值，歸給 May，別拼成「布朗自己量的」。
4. **妻兒結局：** 主敘事在當下認為從此分離，不等於後來命運已有完整結論。Stearns 腳註說布朗正籌錢購回家人、對方索價 1,200 美元 [G L2452–2455](https://github.com/GITenberg/Narrative-of-Henry-Box-Brown-Who-Escaped-from-Slavery-Enclosed-in-a-Box-3-Feet-Long-and-2-Wid__64992/blob/a92658e80effa4cce03bb19c13e96ffaaae5fa2b/64992-0.txt#L2452-L2455)；本書不能證明最終有無團聚。
