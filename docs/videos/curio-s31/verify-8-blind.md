1. **Where is the opening action?** The poem's view is from Chang'an toward the Huaqing Palace hill; palace gates open as a rider approaches and the consort smiles [ly001–ly005], [ly010–ly015].
2. **What does the rider bring in Du Mu's poem?** Lychees; the poem holds that answer until its end [ly015].
3. **Does `無人知` count all informed people?** No. The poem does not enumerate who knew, so the phrase cannot serve as a citywide count [ly016–ly017].
4. **Does `一騎` prove a single horse for the whole trip?** No. The later biography says `乃置騎傳送`, attesting a mounted transmission arrangement without naming its stations [ly018–ly019], [ly030–ly033].
5. **What does `走數千里` establish?** A long journey in the biography; it does not supply a measured origin-to-destination itinerary or an elapsed number of days [ly034–ly036].
6. **What arrival does the biography actually report?** `味未變已至京師`: the fruit's taste had not changed when it reached the capital. This does not attest delivery into Huaqing Palace or identify Du Mu's exact rider [ly037–ly042].
7. **What happened on the consort's birthday in the music account?** Xuanzong arranged music at Mount Li; a new tune lacked a name until southern lychees arrived and it was called 《荔枝香》. The script describes music, not a documented birthday banquet or an identified shipment shared with the relay account [ly045–ly050], [ly058].
8. **What do Guangzhou and Rongzhou contribute?** The geography lists Guangzhou tribute `荔支` and Rongzhou `荔枝煎`; neither entry tags the fresh fruit delivered to the consort [ly052–ly057].
9. **Why does Bai Juyi appear?** His 《荔枝圖序》 describes Ba Gorge lychees and their appearance, taste, and decay after picking; it is a later text about fruit, not an eyewitness log of Xuanzong's delivery [ly059–ly070].
10. **Which property changes first after picking in Bai Juyi's sequence?** Color after one day, then aroma after two, then taste after three; by four or five days he says color, aroma, and taste are gone [ly067–ly070].
11. **Can `味未變` plus Bai Juyi's sequence calculate the courier's speed?** No. One account reports taste on capital arrival, the other starts its clock when a different fruit leaves its branch; picking, handoff, and arrival dates for one batch are missing [ly073–ly081].
12. **Does Bai Juyi's Ba Gorge locate the consort's fruit source?** No. It locates the fruit he describes, without linking that fruit to the court shipment [ly082–ly083].
13. **What can the closing map responsibly display?** A point at the capital, with `騎傳` attributed to the biography; no source-linked starting point, transfer stations, or route line can be plotted [ly085–ly096].
