# 公司引進AI，資深柴犬要被取代了 #汪汪社畜 EP1

每一鏡依序：畫面（英文是給繪圖模型的描述）、畫面裡發生的事、台詞、音效。

## 1. s01-bed
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level: the white robot vacuum sits in the middle of the Shiba Inu's round dog bed, its blue LED ring glowing; the Shiba Inu stands right beside the bed, ID card on its chest, staring at it with narrowed eyes, blue light on its fur
- 動作：the robot vacuum's LED ring pulses blue once; the Shiba Inu's ears flatten slowly while its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，咬牙、皮笑肉不笑）主管說，它是來「協助」我的。

## 2. s06-crumb
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: a single golden cookie crumb in mid-air just above the light wood floor, sunlight catching it
- 動作：the cookie crumb drops onto the floor and bounces once, then rests
- 無台詞，1 秒

## 3. s07-dash
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the Shiba Inu mid-launch on the slippery wood floor, front paws reaching forward, ears flat, eager face
- 動作：the Shiba Inu's paws scrabble and slip on the wood floor so it runs in place, then it brakes hard and freezes when called
- 【主人】（嚴厲、從畫外大喊）麻糬！不可以！

## 4. s08-suck
- 鏡頭：Insert low angle, locked
- 畫面：Low-angle insert at floor level: the white robot vacuum at the right edge of frame, the cookie crumb on the floor just in front of its bumper, the frozen Shiba Inu's front paws blurred in the background
- 動作：the robot vacuum glides slowly and calmly over the cookie crumb and the crumb disappears under it
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：whoosh

## 5. s09-kpi
- 鏡頭：Close-up, locked
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu frozen mid-step with one front paw lifted, eyes huge and shocked, staring down at the empty floor
- 動作：the Shiba Inu stays frozen, one ear slowly droops; its mouth stays closed the whole time
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，崩潰但壓著）五年的績效……被它吸走了。

## 6. s10-sulk
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level: the Shiba Inu facing the robot vacuum a short distance away, chest out, ears forward, offended face
- 動作：the Shiba Inu barks once sharply at the robot vacuum, then turns its back on it, sits down and looks away with its nose in the air; the robot vacuum does not react
- 無台詞，2 秒

## 7. s11-circle
- 鏡頭：Top-down wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Top-down wide shot of the light wood floor: the Shiba Inu lies flat on its belly in the middle like a protester, the robot vacuum at its side
- 動作：the robot vacuum drives calmly in a full circle hugging the outline of the Shiba Inu's body, then drives away
- 無台詞，3 秒

## 8. s12-outline
- 鏡頭：Top-down wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Top-down wide shot of the clean light wood floor: the Shiba Inu standing up and stepping away, leaving behind a perfect dog-shaped outline of shed cream and red fur on the floor exactly where it lay, like a crime scene outline
- 動作：the Shiba Inu steps out of frame to the left, the dog-shaped outline of shed fur stays on the clean floor
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，受傷又尷尬）……它連跟我吵都不想吵。

## 9. s13-praise
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the owner crouching in jeans and grey slippers beside the robot vacuum, holding a phone pointed at it to film, the face out of frame
- 動作：the owner films the robot vacuum with the phone, its LED ring glows proudly blue
- 【主人】（寵溺、驚喜）比麻糬乖，還不會掉毛！
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：laugh

## 10. s14-resume
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu lying on the floor with its nose resting on the glowing screen of a phone lying flat, the screen showing an unreadable blurred job-listing layout, determined deadpan face lit by the screen
- 動作：the Shiba Inu nudges the phone screen with its nose once; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，認真打字的語氣）履歷：地板清潔五年、迎賓、情緒支持。
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，一本正經）期望待遇：一天兩餐。

## 11. s15-push
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked
- 畫面：Insert close-up of the edge of a low shelf with a large open bag of dog jerky treats on it; a Shiba Inu's red and cream front paw resting on the bag, the rest of the dog out of frame
- 動作：the paw pushes the bag slowly and deliberately off the shelf edge
- 無台詞，2 秒

## 12. s17-stuck
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the white robot vacuum driving toward a pile of dog jerky treats spilled on the floor in front of it, the fallen treat bag beside it
- 動作：the robot vacuum rams into the pile, jams on top of it with its front lifted, wheels spinning, its LED ring blinking red
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：beep

## 13. s18-rise
- 鏡頭：Close-up, locked
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu sitting calmly beside the low shelf, watching something off screen
- 動作：the Shiba Inu slowly turns its head toward the camera, eyes sharp and calm; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，冷靜、帥氣、壓低聲音）自己放的火……自己救。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：fanfare

## 14. s19-clean
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level: the Shiba Inu sitting proudly on a spotless wood floor with a visibly round full belly and a jerky crumb on its muzzle, beside it the robot vacuum now free, its LED ring back to blue
- 動作：the Shiba Inu lifts its chin proudly and its body gives one small hiccup; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，得意）地板，清乾淨了。

## 15. s20-blame
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Wide shot at floor level of the sunny living room: the owner's legs in jeans and grey slippers standing over the Shiba Inu, the empty treat bag on the floor between them, the robot vacuum glowing blue at the right
- 動作：the owner picks up the empty treat bag; the Shiba Inu flattens its ears and looks away
- 【主人】（先生氣、再轉成寵溺）麻糬！你又偷吃！還是掃地機最乖～

## 16. s20b-credit
