# 公司引進AI，資深柴犬要被取代了 #汪汪社畜 EP1

每一鏡依序：畫面（英文是給繪圖模型的描述）、畫面裡發生的事、台詞、音效。

## 1. s01-box
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level in a cozy living room: the Shiba Inu sits stiffly on the left of centre, ID card on its chest, staring with narrowed eyes at the corner of a large closed brown cardboard box out of focus in the right foreground
- 動作：the Shiba Inu leans its head slightly toward the box and its ears twitch once while its eyes narrow
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，嚴肅、警戒）公司今天來了新同事。

## 2. s02-boot
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: the cardboard box flaps fall open and a round white robot vacuum sits inside, its LED ring still dark
- 動作：the LED ring around the robot vacuum lights up blue in one sweep, as if it just woke up
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：ding

## 3. s03-nofood
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu's face, eyes wide, head tilted slightly to one side, soft blue light reflected on its fur
- 動作：the Shiba Inu slowly tilts its head further and blinks once in disbelief
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，震驚又不屑）聽說……它不用吃飯。

## 4. s04-room
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Wide shot of the sunny living room: the white robot vacuum glides out across the light wood floor in the middle, the Shiba Inu sits on a round dog bed at the left watching it
- 動作：the robot vacuum drives smoothly across the floor from right to centre, the Shiba Inu's head turns to follow it
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，資深員工的驕傲）我負責清地上的食物，五年了。

## 5. s06-crumb
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: a single golden cookie crumb in mid-air just above the light wood floor, sunlight catching it
- 動作：the cookie crumb drops onto the floor and bounces once, then rests
- 無台詞，1 秒

## 6. s07-dash
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked
- 畫面：Medium close-up of the Shiba Inu crouched low on the dog bed, ears back, eyes locked forward, ready to spring
- 動作：the Shiba Inu springs off the dog bed and dashes forward out of frame to the right, ears flat, tail streaming
- 無台詞，2 秒

## 7. s08-suck
- 鏡頭：Insert low angle, locked
- 畫面：Low-angle insert at floor level: the white robot vacuum at the right edge of frame, the cookie crumb on the floor just in front of its bumper
- 動作：the robot vacuum glides over the cookie crumb and the crumb disappears under it, its LED ring blinks blue once
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：whoosh

## 8. s09-kpi
- 鏡頭：Close-up, locked
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu frozen mid-step with one front paw lifted, mouth slightly open, eyes huge and shocked, staring down at the empty floor
- 動作：the Shiba Inu stays frozen, only its lower lip trembles and one ear slowly droops; its mouth stays closed the whole time, this is its inner voice
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，崩潰但壓著）它……把我的績效吸走了。

## 9. s10-strike
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Wide shot of the living room floor: the Shiba Inu lies flat on its belly across the middle of the floor like a roadblock, front paws stretched forward, determined face, the robot vacuum far on the right
- 動作：the Shiba Inu flattens itself a little more onto the floor and huffs, the robot vacuum starts driving toward it
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，堅定）罷工。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：laugh

## 10. s11-ignore
- 鏡頭：Medium shot low angle, locked
- 畫面：Low-angle medium shot at floor level: the robot vacuum approaching from the right, a short gap of floor between it and the Shiba Inu's side lying on the left
- 動作：the robot vacuum stops a few centimetres before the Shiba Inu, rotates in place and drives calmly away to the right
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：boing

## 11. s12-nofight
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu still lying flat, chin on the floor, eyes sliding sideways to watch something leave, deadpan face
- 動作：the Shiba Inu's eyes follow to the right, then it lets out a long sigh and its cheeks puff
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，受傷又尷尬）……它連跟我吵都不想吵。

## 12. s13-praise
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the owner's legs in jeans and grey slippers standing right beside the white robot vacuum, sunny living room floor behind
- 動作：the owner steps in and stops next to the robot vacuum, its LED ring glows proudly blue
- 【主人】（寵溺、驚喜）哇～好乖喔～
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：laugh

## 13. s14-shock
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu sitting up, ears perked hopefully, tail mid-wag
- 動作：the Shiba Inu's ears drop flat and its tail stops wagging as it realises the praise was not for it
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，心碎）……誇的是它？
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：glass

## 14. s15-wall
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, slow push in
- 畫面：Wide shot of the Shiba Inu sitting alone facing the corner of the room, back to camera, cool blue light and rain streaks on the window beside it, the rest of the room dim
- 動作：the Shiba Inu sits still facing the corner, rain streaks sliding down the window, its ears droop slowly
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，低落）我是不是……該轉職了。
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，認真考慮）還是去當網紅狗好了。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：wahwah

## 15. s16-drumstick
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: a whole golden fried chicken drumstick in mid-air just above the wood floor
- 動作：the fried chicken drumstick lands on the floor with a small bounce and rolls to a stop
- 無台詞，1 秒

## 16. s17-stuck
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the white robot vacuum driving toward the fried chicken drumstick lying on the floor in front of it
- 動作：the robot vacuum rams into the drumstick, gets stuck on top of it with its front lifted, wheels spinning, its LED ring blinking red
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：beep

## 17. s18-rise
- 鏡頭：Close-up, locked
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu's back and head in the dim corner, one ear just perked up
- 動作：the Shiba Inu slowly turns its head over its shoulder toward the camera, eyes sharp and calm; its mouth stays closed the whole time, this is its inner voice
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，冷靜、帥氣）公司……還是需要我。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：laugh

## 18. s19-walk
- 鏡頭：Wide shot low angle, locked
- 畫面：Wide low-angle shot: the Shiba Inu walking toward camera across the wood floor in warm golden light, the stuck robot vacuum blinking red with the drumstick in the foreground at the right

- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：fanfare

## 19. s20-doorbell

- 畫面：Medium shot of the apartment front door half open, a new large brown cardboard box standing on the doormat, the owner's legs in jeans and slippers beside it
