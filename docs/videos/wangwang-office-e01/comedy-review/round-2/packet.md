# 公司引進AI，資深柴犬要被取代了 #汪汪社畜 EP1

每一鏡依序：畫面（英文是給繪圖模型的描述）、畫面裡發生的事、台詞、音效。

## 1. s01-bed
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level: the white robot vacuum sits in the middle of the Shiba Inu's round dog bed, its blue LED ring glowing; the Shiba Inu stands right beside the bed, ID card on its chest, staring at it with narrowed eyes, blue light on its fur
- 動作：the robot vacuum's LED ring pulses blue once; the Shiba Inu's ears flatten slowly while its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，咬牙、皮笑肉不笑）主管說，它是來「協助」我的。

## 2. s04-room
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Wide shot of the sunny living room: the white robot vacuum rolls off the dog bed onto the light wood floor, the Shiba Inu standing at the left watching it
- 動作：the robot vacuum drives smoothly from the dog bed toward the centre of the room, the Shiba Inu's head turns to follow it
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，資深員工的驕傲）我負責清地上的食物，五年了。

## 3. s06-crumb
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: a single golden cookie crumb in mid-air just above the light wood floor, sunlight catching it
- 動作：the cookie crumb drops onto the floor and bounces once, then rests
- 無台詞，1 秒

## 4. s07-dash
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the Shiba Inu mid-launch on the slippery wood floor, front paws reaching forward, ears flat, eager face
- 動作：the Shiba Inu's paws scrabble and slip on the wood floor so it runs in place, then it brakes hard and freezes when called
- 【主人】（嚴厲、從畫外大喊）麻糬！不可以！

## 5. s08-suck
- 鏡頭：Insert low angle, locked
- 畫面：Low-angle insert at floor level: the white robot vacuum at the right edge of frame, the cookie crumb on the floor just in front of its bumper, the frozen Shiba Inu's front paws blurred in the background
- 動作：the robot vacuum glides slowly and calmly over the cookie crumb and the crumb disappears under it
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：whoosh

## 6. s09-kpi
- 鏡頭：Close-up, locked
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu frozen mid-step with one front paw lifted, eyes huge and shocked, staring down at the empty floor
- 動作：the Shiba Inu stays frozen, one ear slowly droops; its mouth stays closed the whole time
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，崩潰但壓著）它……把我的績效吸走了。

## 7. s10-scream
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level: the Shiba Inu in a play bow facing the robot vacuum, hackles raised, tail up, the robot vacuum a short distance in front of it
- 動作：the Shiba Inu lets out a loud dramatic Shiba scream with its head thrown back, then hops backward; the robot vacuum does not react at all
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：scream

## 8. s11-circle
- 鏡頭：Top-down wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Top-down wide shot of the wood floor covered in a thin layer of grey dust: the Shiba Inu lies flat on its belly in the middle like a protester, the robot vacuum at its side
- 動作：the robot vacuum drives calmly in a full circle hugging the outline of the Shiba Inu's body, cleaning a ring of floor around it
- 無台詞，3 秒

## 9. s12-outline
- 鏡頭：Top-down wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Top-down wide shot of the now clean wood floor: the Shiba Inu standing up and stepping away, leaving behind a dog-shaped patch of grey dust on the floor exactly where it lay, like a crime scene outline
- 動作：the Shiba Inu steps out of frame to the left, the dusty dog-shaped outline stays on the clean floor
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，受傷又尷尬）……它連跟我吵都不想吵。

## 10. s13-praise
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the owner crouching in jeans and grey slippers beside the robot vacuum, holding a phone pointed at it to film, the face out of frame
- 動作：the owner films the robot vacuum with the phone, its LED ring glows proudly blue
- 【主人】（寵溺、驚喜）哇～比麻糬還乖耶，我要放限動！
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：laugh

## 11. s14-resume
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu lying on the floor with its nose resting on the glowing screen of a phone lying flat, the screen showing an unreadable blurred job-listing layout, determined deadpan face lit by the screen
- 動作：the Shiba Inu nudges the phone screen with its nose once; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，認真打字的語氣）履歷：地板清潔五年、迎賓、情緒支持。
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，一本正經）期望待遇：一天兩餐。
