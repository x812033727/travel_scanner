# 柴犬上班第五年，公司來了新同事 #汪汪社畜 EP1

作品類型：擬人化無厘頭寫實短劇：真實外觀的柴犬像人一樣上班（打領帶、戴眼鏡、坐辦公椅、寄信），不用照真狗的行為；只要荒謬好笑都可以

每一鏡依序：畫面（英文是給繪圖模型的描述）、畫面裡發生的事、台詞、音效。

## 1. s01-award
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: in the foreground the white robot vacuum wearing a big red award rosette, glowing blue, confetti falling around it; just behind it the Shiba Inu sits upright on a small office chair in tie and glasses, confetti stuck to its face, deadpan
- 動作：confetti falls over the robot vacuum and its LED ring pulses proudly; the Shiba Inu does not move, a piece of confetti slides down its glasses
- 【主人】（像頒獎典禮主持人一樣熱情）本月績效第一名——掃地機！

## 2. s02-week
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked
- 畫面：Medium close-up of the Shiba Inu sitting upright on a small office chair, tie and glasses on, confetti stuck on its head, deadpan face, a lone party horn hanging from its mouth
- 動作：the party horn in the Shiba Inu's mouth droops slowly; its eyes do not move
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，面無表情、咬牙）它上禮拜才來。

## 3. s03-crumb
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: a single golden cookie crumb in mid-air just above the light wood floor, sunlight catching it
- 動作：the cookie crumb drops onto the floor and bounces once, then rests
- 無台詞，1 秒

## 4. s04-dive
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked
- 畫面：Medium shot: the Shiba Inu in tie and glasses launching off its small office chair in a heroic slow-motion dive, front legs stretched forward, tie flapping
- 動作：the Shiba Inu dives forward in slow motion out of frame to the right, ears and tie flying back
- 無台詞，2 秒

## 5. s05-suck
- 鏡頭：Insert low angle, locked
- 畫面：Low-angle insert at floor level: the white robot vacuum at the right edge of frame, the cookie crumb on the floor just in front of its bumper
- 動作：the robot vacuum zips over the crumb a split second early and the crumb disappears; a moment later the Shiba Inu lands belly-flat on the floor beside it
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：whoosh

## 6. s06-kpi
- 鏡頭：Close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Close-up at floor level of the Shiba Inu lying belly-flat with its chin on the floor, glasses knocked crooked, eyes huge, staring at the empty spot on the floor
- 動作：the Shiba Inu stays flat on the floor, its crooked glasses slowly slide down its nose; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，崩潰但壓著）五年的績效……被它吸走了。

## 7. s07-mail
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked
- 畫面：Medium close-up of the Shiba Inu sitting upright at the tiny desk in tie and glasses, both front paws resting on a laptop keyboard, the screen glow on its serious face
- 動作：the Shiba Inu taps the keyboard with one paw twice, very serious; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，正式、憤怒）我要寄信給人資。

## 8. s08-hr
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the white robot vacuum parked next to the desk leg, a tiny folded paper name card propped against it, its LED ring blinking, an unopened cardboard box in the background with a simple black robot-dog silhouette drawn on its side
- 動作：the robot vacuum's LED ring blinks twice like it just received a message
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，愣住、絕望）……人資也是它。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：ding

## 9. s09-circle
- 鏡頭：Top-down wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Top-down wide shot of the light wood floor: the Shiba Inu in tie and glasses lies flat on its belly in the middle like a protester, the robot vacuum at its side
- 動作：the robot vacuum drives calmly in a full circle hugging the outline of the Shiba Inu's body, then drives away
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：laugh

## 10. s10-outline
- 鏡頭：Top-down wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Top-down wide shot of the clean light wood floor: the Shiba Inu standing up and stepping away, leaving behind a perfect dog-shaped outline of shed cream and red fur on the floor exactly where it lay, like a crime scene outline
- 動作：the Shiba Inu steps out of frame to the left, the dog-shaped outline of shed fur stays on the clean floor
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，冷、像在看命案現場）……它已經幫我畫好白線了。

## 11. s11-resume
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu in tie and glasses lying on the floor with its nose on the glowing screen of a phone lying flat, the screen showing an unreadable blurred job-listing layout
- 動作：the Shiba Inu nudges the phone screen with its nose once; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，認真）履歷：地板清潔五年、迎賓、情緒支持。
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，一本正經）期望待遇：一天兩餐。

## 12. s12-push
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked
- 畫面：Medium close-up of a low shelf with a large open bag of dog jerky treats on its edge, the Shiba Inu in tie and glasses standing with its back to the shelf, glancing sideways at the camera
- 動作：the Shiba Inu's glasses flash with light, then it bumps the shelf with its hip without looking and the treat bag tips over the edge and falls out of frame
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，陰險、壓低聲音）新人，給你一個難的案子。

## 13. s13-stuck
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium shot at floor level: the white robot vacuum driving toward a pile of dog jerky treats spilled on the floor, the fallen treat bag beside it
- 動作：the robot vacuum rams into the pile, jams on top of it with its front lifted, wheels spinning, its LED ring blinking red
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：beep

## 14. s15-clean
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Medium close-up at floor level: the Shiba Inu in tie and glasses sitting proudly on a spotless wood floor with a visibly round full belly and a jerky crumb on its muzzle, the robot vacuum free beside it, LED ring back to blue
- 動作：the Shiba Inu lifts its chin proudly and its body gives one small hiccup; its mouth stays closed
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，得意，中間被打嗝打斷）新人搞不定的案子……學長來收。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：burp

## 15. s16-blame
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Wide shot at floor level of the living room: the owner's legs in jeans and grey slippers standing over the Shiba Inu, the empty treat bag on the floor between them, the robot vacuum glowing blue at the right, an unopened cardboard box in the background with a simple black robot-dog silhouette drawn on its side
- 動作：the owner picks up the empty treat bag and turns toward the robot vacuum; the Shiba Inu flattens its ears; the robot vacuum's LED ring blinks happily
- 【主人】（先罵狗、再轉成寵溺）麻糬你又偷吃！還好有掃地機～

## 16. s17-credit
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba Inu in tie and crooked glasses with a round full belly and ears flat, completely deadpan, eyes half-lidded
- 動作：the Shiba Inu stares without moving
- 【麻糬】（內心獨白，死心）……功勞是它的，鍋是我的。
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：glass

## 17. s18-fired
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: the Shiba Inu's white ID card with a small photo of its face lying on the wood floor with its navy lanyard, the white robot vacuum approaching from the right, its LED ring red
- 動作：the robot vacuum glides over the ID card and the card with its lanyard is sucked in and disappears; the LED ring flashes red once
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：whoosh

## 18. s19-office
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the sleek white robot dog standing on the Shiba Inu's round dog bed, a torn-open cardboard box with a robot-dog silhouette behind it, wearing a tiny navy necktie and its own ID card, its two blue oval eyes glowing, looking straight down toward camera
- 動作：the robot dog tilts its head down slightly and its blue eyes brighten
- 【機器狗】（平板、冷靜、像系統廣播）麻糬，你的履歷我看過了。
- 【機器狗】（平板，停頓後宣判）一天兩餐……太高了。
