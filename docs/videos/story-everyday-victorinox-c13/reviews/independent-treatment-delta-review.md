## Direct source readback and delta

- **S1/S9, chronology:** [Victorinox's own timeline](https://www.victorinox.com/en/All-about-Victorinox/History/cms/history/) says 1884 workshop, 1897 officer's/sports-knife patent, 1945 U.S. soldiers buying knives through Post Exchange stores as souvenirs, 1989 North American Swiss Army watch entry, and **1999 licensed Travel Gear market entry**. It separately dates Wenger 2005, acquisition of the former licensee's travel-gear business 2014, and apparel discontinuation 2017. The revised origin chapter places later travelers outside the 1945 scene. S9 reuses S1 and S2, not an additional witness.
- **S2–S4, legal entities and denominators:** The [2001-07-30 SABI 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731947/000073194701500009/sabeightk.htm) announces a pre-9/11 combination of existing international watch operations of Swiss Victorinox AG and U.S. Swiss Army Brands, Inc. (SABI). The [SABI 2001 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731947/000073194702000003/tenq.htm) gives SABI's 2001 knife/card/tool product-sales fall of 23%, identifies recession/corporate-channel and other factors, and does not quantify 9/11's share. The [SABI 2002 Q2 10-Q](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/731947/000073194702000016/tenq2.htm) gives **SABI Q2 year-on-year** knife/tool −34%, watches +7%, professional cutlery +16%, plus a special-promotion effect in H1. These cannot be substituted for Swiss AG global revenue or profit, nor show that watches wholly filled the knife gap. The Treatment keeps those limits.
- **S5 versus S10, M05:** [Swissinfo's English agency report of 2002-01-29](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/archive-banking-fintech/sales-blunted-at-victorinox/2511740) assigns a near-third knife-sales decline to **2001 Q4** and reports airport sale/carry-on restrictions. In [Swissinfo's Spanish direct interview of 2002-01-25](https://www.swissinfo.ch/spa/archive-finanzas/caen-ventas-de-navajas-suizas/2505136), export manager Paul Camenzin limits approximately 30% to **October** and gives a 7.5% annual decline, especially in pocketknives and the U.S. The [Portuguese edition of the same interview](https://www.swissinfo.ch/por/archive-financas/caem-as-vendas-de-canivetes-su%C3%AD%C3%A7os/2506316) explicitly describes the annual 7.5% as the pocketknife segment. Spanish and Portuguese are one interview, with slightly different wording for the annual denominator; neither resolves the Q4/October conflict. Revised M05, the one-line answer, cold open, engine chapter and timed hooks now surface or avoid that conflict instead of declaring an uncontested Q4 figure.
- **S10 causal attribution:** Camenzin also attributed the decline more to changed consumer habits after 9/11, the tourism decline and recession than to new security measures. The revised S10 row accurately presents this as **his contemporary view**, not an independently quantified split of causes. The Treatment keeps airport restrictions, demand changes, recession and SABI's own corporate-channel changes distinct. The Spanish/Portuguese annual wording difference should be retained as an attribution caveat if a later script adds the 7.5% figure; the current Treatment does not use it as a lead statistic.
- **S5, M09 product statuses:** The English report describes a clock/alarm/timer knife being rushed out; a rounded children's knife in development; bladeless tools being offered; and a checked-luggage reminder brochure being issued. Revised Treatment and M09 now distinguish all four statuses and claim no proven sales recovery. The report's reference to knives packaged so they cannot be opened by hand is omitted, not contradicted.
- **S6–S8, hindsight and rules:** [Elsener's later first-person account](https://www.swissmadebusiness.com/brands/victorinox) supports temporarily lending employees without fixing a disputed headcount; [his 2024 Blick interview](https://www.blick.ch/wirtschaft/victorinox-boss-carl-elsener-im-interview/e7cty4f) calls pre-existing watches, professional knives and kitchen knives helpful as his judgment. [TSA's historical comparison](https://www.tsa.gov/sites/default/files/9766_info_aviation_security_then_now_928.pdf) and [current Swiss Army Knife entry](https://www.tsa.gov/travel/security-screening/whatcanibring/all?combine=knife&field_item_category_value=All&page=0) support a U.S. historical/current distinction, not a reconstruction of every 2001 airport rule or global travel advice. The Treatment does not collapse these into one measured AG result or use today's rules to date the 2001 response.
