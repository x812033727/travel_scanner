# Till the Verdict Do Us Part — adaptation glossary

The one list every writer of the English version follows. The Chinese plan (`../setting.json`,
`../outline.json`, `../season-01.json`) is the story; this file says what each Chinese name, place,
object and procedure becomes. Where the Chinese text and this file disagree, this file wins. Where this
file is silent, adapt the Chinese faithfully and naturally for an American audience — do not invent
new plot.

Decided by the owner: **9:16 vertical** (2026-11-10; was 16:9 on 2026-11-09), **season one only (60 episodes)**, English written natively
(not translated for dubbing), photoreal live-action look with a diverse Western cast.

## Tone of the English

- Dialogue is spoken American English: short, concrete, a line under about ten words. No translated
  idioms ("my mother-in-law's tofu-knife mouth"); find the English equivalent or cut it.
- Keep the restraint: grief is a hallway, a half-knitted hat and a flickering light. Never write the
  word "miscarriage", "abortion", "blood", "suicide", "kill", "dead baby", "sexy", "nude" in a
  `youtube_title`; say "that night", "the hallway", "what he did".
- Money is US dollars. Dates and seasons follow the Pacific Northwest (rain, ferries, grey water).

## Procedure: write it this way (not the Chinese way)

1. **No same-day divorce and remarriage (episodes 19–20).** Washington has a 90-day waiting period.
   Episode 19 ends with Nora signing the three clauses and filing for divorce. **Episode 20 opens
   "Ninety days later"** with a short montage (the motel, Peg's ledger, physical therapy, Nora sketching
   at the motel table) and then the courthouse: the judge enters the divorce decree in one courtroom;
   across the hall at the County Clerk, Elias is waiting for the marriage licence. Peg is the witness both
   times; Bev from the motel is the second witness. "I'll sign the divorce. The house — I'll see you in court."
2. **Dr. Brandt's ban** is "no hot furnace for **six months**", so it is still in force when Kiln Row opens
   (chapter 4) and lifts early in what would be season two.
3. **Reporting the crime.** Nora reports to the police; Deputy Prosecutor Marcus Reyes reviews it and
   **declines to file** ("Half a pill and a pickup log don't prove he switched them. Bring me evidence,
   not a story."). Later the forgery charge is **filed** — the case tracker bead moves from *Report* to *Charges*.
4. **Pharmacy records.** Nora requests **her own** pickup records from the hospital pharmacy herself;
   each pickup carries the signature of the person who collected it: Grant Whitlock.
5. **Property.** Washington is a community-property state: the forged "separate property agreement"
   gives Grant the Glass House, the accounts and the Forever design rights. The court **freezes the
   transfers** once the examiner's report comes back.
6. **The document examiner** is appointed by the court in the civil case; Leonard Fisk moves to replace
   him (thread s01-t36) and loses.
7. **Advertiser safety is unchanged**: no blood, no medical procedure, no violence toward people, no bed
   scenes; glass is only smashed when it is a fake, in gloves and goggles, into a steel drum.
