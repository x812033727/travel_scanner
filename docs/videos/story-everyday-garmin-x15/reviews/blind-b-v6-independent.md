The wrist and phone chapters broaden what GPS sells; the ending reveals Fitness first and Fitness plus Outdoor at about 61% of 2025 total revenue (`x150501`–`x150535`, `x150601`–`x150635`). The title's “手機來了怎麼辦？” is answered as a later revenue mix and set of products, with a necessary fourteen-year evidence limit (`x150541`–`x150542`); it does not claim the phone alone caused the 2008–10 drop (`x150408`–`x150411`) or that two watches caused the later mix (`x150510`–`x150511`). The specific weakness is that the first chapter handoff jumps backward from a future-facing question. A one-line bridge could make the 2006 rewind feel like the answer's starting point.

**Substantive length.** The 128 spoken lines contain 2,401 Han characters and 2,851 total text characters. Excluding the last viewer question and subscribe line leaves roughly 2,350 Han characters. At a plausible 270–290 Han characters per minute, that is about **8.1–8.7 minutes of substantive speech**, before scene gaps; at a fast 300/minute it is just under eight minutes. This is a reasoned script estimate, not a measured TTS duration. It likely meets the ≥8-minute target at normal delivery, with little margin at a fast read.
