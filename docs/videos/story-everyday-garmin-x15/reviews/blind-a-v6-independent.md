The principal fix is the Chapter 1→2 handoff: make the first line answer or act on the opening “later income” question before going back to 2006, or end the opening on the immediate question that 2006 answers. The second is to shorten `x150522–528` or tie those mobile moves to a measured income consequence. Those changes would require a new blind read; this report does not edit the frozen scripts.

**Length and close.** The body after the nine-scene opening and before the outro is 105 scenes, 116 spoken lines, and 2,544 written characters. At roughly 250 Chinese characters per minute it is about 10.2 minutes before scene pauses, so the ≥8-minute body target is plausible; actual TTS timing has not been measured. The opening is 216 characters, around 52 seconds at the same rate. `x15-outro` contains exactly three spoken lines: a quantified answer, an answerable personal comment question, and a reasoned subscribe request.

**Shorts.** Both nine-beat scripts stand alone: Short 1 moves from the 2008 “可能” warning to the 2011 “已使成長停滯” wording and keeps the no-single-cause caveat; Short 2 begins at the 2011 watches, states the old business still led, marks the incompatible segment boundaries, and answers with the 2025 Fitness + Outdoor 60.9%. Their spoken text is 169 and 203 written characters. At 250 characters per minute plus roughly 0.3 seconds between nine beats, they estimate to about **43 and 51 seconds**, within 25–55 seconds. Short 2 has little slack if the voice is slow; actual TTS duration is needed before final runtime approval. Both have a question/turn/proof/payoff independent of a main-film link.
