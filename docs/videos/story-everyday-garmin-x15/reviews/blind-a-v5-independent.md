### Eight-minute substantive body

The five body chapters contain about **2,406 displayed/say characters before the three-line ending**; the opening has about 208. At the repository's rough 250-character-per-minute planning rate, the body is about **9.6 minutes**. It contains real facts and causal limits: licensing pressure, possible market maturity and price cuts, declining car/mobile revenue, the change from “可能” to “已”, products beyond the car, and a later revenue mix. **Pass on script length and substantive material**, provisionally; only recorded speech can establish final duration. Reducing the figure pileups must preserve eight minutes of new information, not fill time with repeated narration.

### Independent Shorts verdict (spoken text only)

Both Shorts have nine narration lines. At the same planning rate, **Short 1: 169 characters, about 41 seconds; Short 2: 180 characters, about 43 seconds**. Both are within the requested **25–55 seconds on the script estimate**; final audio timing remains unverified.

- **Short 1 — acceptable as a standalone cut.** It introduces 2008 “可能”, gives the 2011 “已經” reversal, and avoids claiming the phone alone explains the dollar decline. The closing caveat is accurate but lands after an already-stated conclusion, so it weakens the final beat.
- **Short 2 — revise the standalone story.** Its first question, whether car/mobile still matters in 2011, is answered by “仍占公司過半”; then it jumps fourteen years to the 2025 fitness/outdoor share. The final two lines are consecutive cautions about incomparable segments and not crediting one watch. The facts are useful, but the Short ends on qualifications rather than a concrete payoff to the opening question.

### Most useful spoken revision

Choose one trackable object or decision that can be carried from the car opening to the 2025 mix. Put each essential figure at the moment it changes that object, cut the duplicate 73% and 58% reports, and let chapter 2 begin with an event instead of “開場那道收入疑問”. Keep the three distant payoffs and the honest limit on attributing the revenue fall to phones alone. Replace `x150601`'s unsupported “開場那份二〇二六年二月公告” callback with a truthful handoff from `x150543`.
