The late source distinction appears at `dn079–080`, and `dn087` finally confirms Darwin sailed. The opening is safe on the result, even though its list of issues is weak as a hook sequence.

It says the nose was a late account, but does not plainly distinguish a reported hesitation from an actual exclusion. The third outro sentence (`dn999c`) is a general aphorism; it also does not give the requested spoken subscription invitation.
- **Payoffs:** The JSON lists 15 loops, but at least three target scenes are not their own answer (`dn029→dn073`, `dn065→dn073`, `dn053→dn065`). The story later resolves two of them (`dn074`, `dn074e/dn087`), so the repair is mainly to make the payoff declaration and spoken callbacks match where the new fact actually arrives. `dn069→dn085a` resolves the outcome (“not left ashore”) while leaving the exact route decision undocumented; that limitation is preferable to inventing an answer.

These are estimates, not audio measurements.

The opener's 229 characters are roughly 53 seconds of speech at the rubric pace and about 62 seconds with one second per scene, near or slightly over its 60-second cap.
