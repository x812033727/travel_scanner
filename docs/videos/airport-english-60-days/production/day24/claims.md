## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where has the Seoul flight moved?
  - 正解：C nineteen
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Excuse me. The screen here no longer shows my flight to Seoul.
  - A02: Your flight has moved from gate A six to gate C nineteen.
- Q2: Has the first boarding time changed?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Did boarding also change, or is the time still the same?
  - A04: The boarding time is unchanged. Please head to the new gate now.
- Q3: What is the new Osaka gate?
  - 正解：D twelve
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Flight three-eight-one to Osaka. I was waiting at gate D two.
  - B04: The new gate is D twelve. Please confirm it on the departure screen.
