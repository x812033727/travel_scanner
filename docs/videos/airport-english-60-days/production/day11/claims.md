## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where is the regular entrance?
  - 正解：On the right
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Is this entrance for all departing passengers, or only a special lane?
  - A04: The regular entrance is on the right. The other lane has separate eligibility rules.
- Q2: What is the second traveler's gate?
  - 正解：D eighteen
  - 重播：B02, B03
  - B02: This area serves a different set of gates. Which gate are you using?
  - B03: My boarding pass says gate D eighteen.
- Q3: Where is the elevator?
  - 正解：Behind the information desk
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Is there an elevator nearby for someone with a stroller?
  - B06: Yes. It is just behind the information desk, next to the escalator.
