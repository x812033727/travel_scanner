## 測驗證據

- Q1: What should happen when the sign comes on?
  - 正解：Return to the seat and fasten the belt
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: The seat-belt sign just came on. Should I return to my seat?
  - A02: Yes. Please sit down and fasten your seat belt.
- Q2: When is keeping the belt fastened recommended?
  - 正解：While seated
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: The sign is off now. Is it all right to keep my belt fastened anyway?
  - B02: Yes. We recommend keeping it fastened while you are seated.
- Q3: What should the traveler avoid after dropping a phone inside the seat?
  - 正解：Moving the seat
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: What should I do if I drop my phone inside the seat mechanism?
  - B06: Do not move the seat. Call a crew member for assistance.
