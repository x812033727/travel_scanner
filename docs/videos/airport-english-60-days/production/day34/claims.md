## 測驗證據

- Q1: What are the two regular choices?
  - 正解：Chicken with rice or pasta
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: What are the meal choices on this flight?
  - A02: Today we have chicken with rice or pasta with tomato sauce.
- Q2: What does the first traveler want confirmed?
  - 正解：Whether the pasta is vegetarian
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Is the pasta vegetarian, or does the sauce contain meat?
  - A04: Let me check the meal information before I confirm that for you.
- Q3: What is the second traveler's seat?
  - 正解：Seventeen D
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: I'm in seat seventeen D, and I requested a vegetarian meal.
  - B04: Let me check the meal list for your seat. Please keep your boarding pass available.
