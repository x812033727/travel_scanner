## 測驗證據

- Q1: What water does the first traveler choose?
  - 正解：Still water without ice
  - 重播：A01, A02, A03
  - A01: Could I have some water, please? No ice, if possible.
  - A02: Of course. Would you prefer still water or sparkling water?
  - A03: Still water, please. Could you also tell me when tea will be served?
- Q2: When will hot drinks be offered?
  - 正解：After the meal service
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Still water, please. Could you also tell me when tea will be served?
  - A04: We will offer hot drinks after the meal service.
- Q3: What does the second traveler want in the coffee?
  - 正解：A little milk
  - 重播：B02, B03
  - B02: Certainly. Would you like milk or sugar with it?
  - B03: Just a little milk, please. Is the cup safe on this tray table?
