  - 正解：The airline service desk
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: My flight has been cancelled. Where can I discuss a replacement booking?
  - A02: Please go to the airline service desk or use its official support channel.
- Q2: When does the replacement flight depart?
  - 正解：Seven forty-five
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: You offered a flight tomorrow morning. Can you confirm the details before I accept?
  - B02: Yes. It departs at seven forty-five and arrives at ten ten.

  - 正解：No, they need checking
  - 重播：B03, B04

  - B04: We need to confirm those separately. I'll explain what is included.
