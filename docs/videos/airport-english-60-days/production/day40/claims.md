## 測驗證據

- Q1: What should happen to the overhead bin during turbulence?
  - 正解：Leave it closed
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: I was about to open the overhead bin. Should I wait?
  - A04: Yes. Please leave the bin closed until it is safe and the crew permits it.
- Q2: What may happen to meal service?
  - 正解：It may pause
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Will the meal service continue during the turbulence?
  - A06: It may be paused. The crew will resume when conditions allow.
- Q3: When should the traveler change device settings after landing?
  - 正解：When permitted by the crew
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Can I switch off airplane mode as soon as the wheels touch down?
  - B06: Wait for the crew's announcement and follow the instructions for this flight.
