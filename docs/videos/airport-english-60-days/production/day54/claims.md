## 測驗證據

- Q1: What is broken on the first suitcase?
  - 正解：A wheel
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: I collected my suitcase, and one wheel is broken. Where should I report it?
  - A02: Please bring the bag to the airline baggage desk before leaving the area.
- Q2: What should the traveler photograph?
  - 正解：The damage
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Should I take photographs of the damage now?
  - A04: Yes, keep clear photos and your baggage documents for the report.
- Q3: Is replacement guaranteed in the second conversation?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Can you promise that the airline will replace the suitcase?
  - B06: The airline must assess the claim. Ask about its process and applicable terms.
