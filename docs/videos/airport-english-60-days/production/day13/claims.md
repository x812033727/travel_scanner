## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where does the laptop go in this lane?
  - 正解：A separate tray
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Should I take my laptop out of its sleeve?
  - A04: For this lane, yes. Put the laptop in a separate tray.
- Q2: When should the traveler walk through?
  - 正解：When signaled
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I've placed my bag on the belt. Should I walk through now?
  - B02: Please wait until I signal you. One person at a time.
- Q3: What was left in the pocket?
  - 正解：Keys
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: My keys are still in my pocket. Shall I put them in a tray?
  - B06: Yes, place them in this small tray, then wait for the next instruction.
