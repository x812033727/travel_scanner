## 測驗證據

- Q1: What seat type does the traveler request?
  - 正解：Aisle
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Here you are. Could I have an aisle seat if one is available?
  - A04: There is one in row sixteen. Your gate is B twenty and boarding starts at nine ten.
- Q2: What is the new gate?
  - 正解：B thirty-two
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: The screen now says B thirty-two. Has our flight changed gates?
  - B02: Yes. Please go to B thirty-two. Your boarding time is still nine ten.
- Q3: Which group is currently boarding?
  - 正解：Group Two
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: My boarding pass shows Group Three. Is it my turn to board?
  - B04: We are calling Group Two now. Please wait until Group Three is announced.
