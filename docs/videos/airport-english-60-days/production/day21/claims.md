## 測驗證據

- Q1: Who is boarding first in the first conversation?
  - 正解：Passengers needing assistance
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: The announcement was hard to hear. Has boarding started for our flight?
  - A02: Yes. The gate team has started boarding passengers who need assistance.
- Q2: Where is the group number printed?
  - 正解：Near the seat number
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Where can I find my group number on the boarding pass?
  - A06: It is printed near the seat number. Let me help you find it.
- Q3: What does shortly mean in the second conversation?
  - 正解：Soon
  - 重播：B01, B02, B03
  - B01: Attention, passengers on flight seven-two-four to Singapore. Boarding will begin shortly.
  - B02: I think that's our flight. Did they say boarding is happening right now?
  - B03: The announcement said shortly, so it has not started yet.
