## 測驗證據

- Q1: When was the first traveler's previous visit?
  - 正解：About three years ago
  - 重播：A02, A03
  - A02: Certainly. I asked whether you have visited this country before.
  - A03: Yes. I visited once about three years ago for a holiday.
- Q2: How long was that previous visit?
  - 正解：Two weeks
  - 重播：A04, A05
  - A04: And how long did you stay on that previous visit?
  - A05: Two weeks. I can give you the approximate dates if that would help.
- Q3: Which address does the second question require?
  - 正解：The accommodation for this visit
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: I'm not sure whether you are asking about my current address or my hotel address.
  - B04: For this question, I need the address where you will stay during this visit.
