## 測驗證據

- Q1: Who should clarify uncertain declaration requirements?
  - 正解：A customs officer
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: I'm unsure whether I need to declare something in my luggage. Where should I ask?
  - A02: Speak to a customs officer before choosing an exit or submitting your declaration.
- Q2: What items does the first traveler mention?
  - 正解：Packaged food and gifts
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: I have packaged food and some gifts. Should I explain exactly what they are?
  - A04: Yes. Describe the items accurately and follow the current local requirements.
- Q3: Who asked the second traveler to carry the gift?
  - 正解：A family member
  - 重播：B02, B03
  - B02: Are any items in your bags being transported on another person's behalf?
  - B03: Yes. A family member asked me to bring a sealed gift.
