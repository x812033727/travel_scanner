## 測驗證據

- Q1: Which group is boarding now?
  - 正解：Group Two
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: My pass says Group Four, but the announcement mentioned Group Two.
  - A02: Please wait until Group Four is called. We are boarding Group Two now.
- Q2: Where should waiting passengers stand?
  - 正解：Behind the marked area
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Can I stand near the scanner so I don't miss my turn?
  - A04: Please wait behind the marked area and leave room for the current group.
- Q3: Is boarding still open in the second conversation?
  - 正解：Yes
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I arrived after my boarding group was called. Have I missed boarding?
  - B02: Boarding is still open. Please join this line and have your pass ready.
