## 測驗證據

- Q1: When should the traveler remain seated?
  - 正解：While the seat-belt sign is on
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Is there a restroom toward the back of the aircraft?
  - A02: Yes, but please remain seated while the seat-belt sign is on.
- Q2: Where is the occupancy indicator?
  - 正解：By the restroom door
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: How can I tell whether the restroom is occupied?
  - A04: Check the indicator by the door. Please avoid blocking the aisle while waiting.
- Q3: Should the traveler guess missing form details?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: I don't know the postal code. Should I guess one?
  - B06: No. Check your booking or ask the relevant officer how to complete the missing detail.
