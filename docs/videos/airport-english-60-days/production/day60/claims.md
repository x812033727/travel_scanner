## 測驗證據

- Q1: How long is the planned visit?
  - 正解：Eight days
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: What is the purpose and length of your visit?
  - A02: I'm here for a holiday, and I will stay for eight days.
- Q2: When does the return flight leave?
  - 正解：Next Saturday
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Do you have a return ticket available to show?
  - A06: Yes. My flight home leaves next Saturday. Here is the confirmation.
- Q3: What does the missing suitcase look like?
  - 正解：Green with a white strap
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: The suitcase is green, with a white strap and a small blue tag.
  - B04: We'll record those details. Please give us an address where you can be contacted.
