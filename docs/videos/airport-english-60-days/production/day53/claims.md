## 測驗證據

- Q1: What color is the first suitcase?
  - 正解：Dark blue
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Could you describe the suitcase that has not arrived?
  - A02: It is a medium-sized dark blue suitcase with four wheels and a silver handle.
- Q2: What is attached to the first handle?
  - 正解：A yellow ribbon
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Does it have any stickers, ribbons, or other distinctive features?
  - A04: There is a yellow ribbon on the handle and a small scratch near one wheel.
- Q3: What goes around the second suitcase?
  - 正解：A red strap
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Can you also tell us the brand or any identifying features?
  - B06: The brand is on the front, and a red strap goes around the middle.
