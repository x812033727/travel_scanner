## 測驗證據

- Q1: Which zone should the traveler go to?
  - 正解：Zone D
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: Excuse me. Is this the check-in area for Maple Airlines?
  - B02: No. Maple Airlines is in Zone D. D as in Delta.
- Q2: Listen for the correct counter numbers.
  - 正解：30–34
  - 重播：B03, B04, B05, B06
  - B03: Which counters should I use?
  - B04: Counters thirty to thirty-four.
  - B05: Thirteen?
  - B06: No, thirty. Three zero.
- Q3: What should you do after passing the pharmacy?
  - 正解：Turn right
  - 重播：B07, B08
  - B07: How do I get to the check-in area?
  - B08: Go straight past the pharmacy, then turn right. The counters are next to the stairs.
