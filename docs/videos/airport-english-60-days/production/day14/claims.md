## 測驗證據

- Q1: What is inside the reusable bottle?
  - 正解：Water
  - 重播：A02, A03
  - A02: Please check the posted limit. Is the bottle empty or does it contain liquid?
  - A03: It's a reusable water bottle, and it still has some water inside.
- Q2: Where are refill stations mentioned?
  - 正解：After security
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Can I refill the empty bottle after I pass through security?
  - A06: There are refill stations in the departure area. Follow the drinking-water signs.
- Q3: Who should inspect the medicine?
  - 正解：The screening officer
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I need to carry this liquid medicine. Should I tell someone before screening?
  - B02: Yes. Please show it to the screening officer and explain what it is.
