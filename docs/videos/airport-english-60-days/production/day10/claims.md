## 測驗證據

- Q1: What is the first traveler's seat?
  - 正解：Fourteen A
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Could you help me read my boarding pass? Which number is my seat?
  - A02: Your seat is fourteen A. The gate is shown separately as C twelve.
- Q2: What does seven forty refer to?
  - 正解：Boarding time
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: And is seven forty the departure time or the boarding time?
  - A04: That is the boarding time. The flight is scheduled to depart at eight twenty.
- Q3: What should a traveler use to find the flight on a screen?
  - 正解：The flight number
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Where can I find the gate once it is announced?
  - B04: Check the airport departure screens using your flight number.
