## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where is the safety card?
  - 正解：In the seat pocket
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Where can I find the safety card for my seat?
  - A04: It is in the seat pocket in front of you. Check the nearest exits.
- Q2: Could the nearest exit be behind the traveler?
  - 正解：Yes
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Is the closest exit always in front of my row?
  - A06: Not necessarily. It may be behind you, so look in both directions.
- Q3: How does the demonstrated buckle open?
  - 正解：Lift the metal buckle
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: Could you explain how this seat belt opens? I haven't used this type before.
  - B02: Lift the metal buckle to release it. To tighten it, fasten it and pull the loose end of the strap.
