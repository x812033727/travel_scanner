## 測驗證據

- Q1: Why did the traveler miss the connection?
  - 正解：The first flight arrived late
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: I missed my connection because the first flight arrived late. Can you help?
  - A02: Please show your itinerary and boarding passes so I can review the booking.
- Q2: When might another flight be available?
  - 正解：This evening
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: What is the earliest flight you can offer me today?
  - A06: There may be a flight this evening. Let me confirm whether seats are available.
- Q3: What does the traveler request in writing?
  - 正解：The new itinerary and instructions
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Can I get the new itinerary and any instructions in writing?
  - B06: Certainly. Keep these documents and check the departure screen for updates.
