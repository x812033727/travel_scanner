## 測驗證據

- Q1: When is the first return flight?
  - 正解：Next Friday
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Do you have a return or onward ticket?
  - A02: Yes. I have a return flight next Friday. Here is the confirmation.
- Q2: How long is the second traveler staying before continuing?
  - 正解：Five days
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: Your itinerary shows an onward flight to another country. Is that correct?
  - B02: Yes. I plan to continue to New Zealand after five days here.
- Q3: What is the onward destination city?
  - 正解：Auckland
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Please show the date and destination on the onward booking.
  - B06: Here they are. The flight leaves on the twenty-fourth for Auckland.
