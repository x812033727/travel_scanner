## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where are the restrooms?
  - 正解：Before the cafe, on the left
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Excuse me. Is there a restroom near this gate?
  - A02: Yes. Walk toward the cafe, then turn left before you reach it.
- Q2: Where are charging sockets mentioned?
  - 正解：Under some window seats
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: And where can I charge my phone while I wait?
  - A06: Some seats near the windows have charging sockets underneath them.
- Q3: Who can direct the traveler to lost property?
  - 正解：The information desk
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Do you know where I can ask about a lost passport cover?
  - B06: The information desk can direct you to the airport's lost-property service.
