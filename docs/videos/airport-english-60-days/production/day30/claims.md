## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where is seat eighteen F?
  - 正解：On the right
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Hello. My seat is eighteen F. Which side of the aircraft is that?
  - A02: It is on your right. Please continue down the aisle to row eighteen.
- Q2: Where may a small backpack go if it fits?
  - 正解：Under the seat in front
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Can this small backpack go under the seat in front of me?
  - A06: Yes, if the crew permits it and it fits fully under the seat without blocking the aisle.
- Q3: What caused the seating confusion?
  - 正解：A row mix-up
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: The other passenger has twenty-three A. Is that the next row back?
  - B04: Yes. It looks like a simple row mix-up. I'll help you both get settled.
