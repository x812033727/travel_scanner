## 測驗證據

- Q1: What allergy does the first traveler report?
  - 正解：Peanuts
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: I have a severe peanut allergy. Could you help me check this meal?
  - A02: Thank you for telling us. Let me look at the available ingredient information.
- Q2: Can the crew guarantee no cross-contact?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: I also need to know whether you can guarantee there is no cross-contact.
  - A04: We cannot guarantee that. Please decide based on your own medical advice and the information provided.
- Q3: What does the second snack label list?
  - 正解：Milk
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I cannot eat dairy products. Does this snack contain milk or butter?
  - B02: The label lists milk. I can check whether we have another packaged snack.
