## 測驗證據

- Q1: Why may the suitcase be checked?
  - 正解：Limited overhead space
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: An agent said my cabin suitcase may need to be checked at the gate.
  - A02: The overhead space is limited. Let me check your bag and issue a tag.
- Q2: What should the traveler ask about before surrendering the bag?
  - 正解：Batteries and restricted items
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Should I remove any items before handing over the suitcase?
  - A04: Keep travel documents and valuables with you, and ask about batteries and restricted items.
- Q3: What should the traveler keep until collection?
  - 正解：The baggage tag
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Could I have a receipt for the suitcase you are taking?
  - B06: Certainly. Keep this tag until you have collected your bag at the destination.
