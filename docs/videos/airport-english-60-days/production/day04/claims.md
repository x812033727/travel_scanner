## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where is the first booking reference?
  - 正解：In an email
  - 重播：A02, A03
  - A02: Good morning. May I see your passport and your booking reference, please?
  - A03: Here's my passport. The booking reference is in the email on my phone.
- Q2: How many passengers are on the first reservation?
  - 正解：Two
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Of course. There are two passengers on this reservation, including my sister.
  - A06: I can see both names. Please have your sister's passport ready as well.
- Q3: Where is the second traveler connecting?
  - 正解：Vancouver
  - 重播：B04, B05
  - B04: I've found a booking. Could you confirm your destination for me?
  - B05: I'm going to Toronto, with a connection in Vancouver.
