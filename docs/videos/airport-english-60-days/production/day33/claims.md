## 測驗證據

- Q1: What is wrong with the first seat?
  - 正解：It will not stay upright
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Excuse me. My seat won't stay upright. Could you check it?
  - A02: Of course. Please step into the aisle briefly while I inspect the seat.
- Q2: What does the crew try for the second problem?
  - 正解：A screen reset
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: The screen at my seat isn't working, but the neighboring screen is on.
  - B02: Let me try resetting your entertainment system. It may take a few minutes.
- Q3: Does the screen reset move the seat?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Will that change my seat position or turn off the light?
  - B04: No. This reset only affects the entertainment screen.
