## 測驗證據

- Q1: How much is the first bag overweight?
  - 正解：Three kilograms
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Is my suitcase within the weight allowance for this booking?
  - A02: It is three kilograms over the allowance shown on your ticket.
- Q2: What does the traveler do before reweighing?
  - 正解：Repack the suitcase
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I have repacked my suitcase. Could you weigh it again, please?
  - B02: Certainly. It is now within the checked-baggage allowance.
- Q3: What else must be checked for the cabin bag?
  - 正解：Its size
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Great. I also want to confirm that my cabin bag is acceptable.
  - B04: Please place it on this scale, and we'll check its size as well.
