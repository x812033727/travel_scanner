## 測驗證據

- Q1: Which row has two seats together?
  - 正解：Row thirty
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: We don't mind sitting near the back if that helps.
  - A04: There are two seats in row thirty, one window and one middle.
- Q2: What new documents does the first pair receive?
  - 正解：Boarding passes
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: That would work. Could you move both of us to that row?
  - A06: Yes. I'll print new boarding passes. Please use the new seat numbers.
- Q3: How old is the child?
  - 正解：Seven
  - 重播：B02, B03
  - B02: I'll look for adjacent seats. How old is your child?
  - B03: He's seven, and our boarding passes currently show separate rows.
