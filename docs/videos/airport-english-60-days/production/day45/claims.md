## 測驗證據

- Q1: When does the first connection board?
  - 正解：In ten minutes
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: My incoming flight was late, and my next flight boards in ten minutes.
  - A02: Please show me your boarding pass so I can check the gate and current status.
- Q2: May the traveler use a staff-only corridor?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: Should I run through the staff-only corridor to save time?
  - A06: No. Stay on the passenger route and follow airport staff instructions.
- Q3: Where should the traveler go if the gate closes?
  - 正解：The airline transfer desk
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: If the gate closes before I arrive, where should I go?
  - B04: Go to the airline transfer desk so they can review your booking.
