## 測驗證據

- Q1: What comes after putting the bag on the belt?
  - 正解：Put the jacket in a tray
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: I'm sorry, there were several instructions. Could you repeat them more slowly?
  - A02: First, put your bag on the belt. Then place your jacket in a tray.
- Q2: Where might the missing coat be?
  - 正解：The second belt
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I've collected my bag, but I can't find the tray with my coat.
  - B02: Please check the second belt. Some trays are directed there for another check.
- Q3: Where should the traveler pack?
  - 正解：The table on the left
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Once it's returned, can I pack at this table?
  - B06: Yes. Use the table on the left so the exit lane stays clear.
