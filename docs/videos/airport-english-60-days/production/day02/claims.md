## 測驗證據

- Q1: Which terminal does the first traveler need?
  - 正解：Terminal Two
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Excuse me. My boarding pass says Terminal Two. Am I in the right building?
  - A02: This is Terminal One. Take the free shuttle outside Door Four.
- Q2: How often does the shuttle leave?
  - 正解：Every ten minutes
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: How often does the shuttle leave, and where should I get off?
  - A04: It leaves every ten minutes. Get off at the next terminal.
- Q3: Where is the larger elevator?
  - 正解：On the left
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Can I take these elevators with my luggage cart?
  - B04: Yes. Use the larger elevator on the left, next to the stairs.
