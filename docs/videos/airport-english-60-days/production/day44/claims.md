## 測驗證據

- Q1: Which lane should the first traveler use?
  - 正解：Transfers
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: Where should I join the line for connecting passengers?
  - A04: Use the lane marked Transfers and have your onward boarding pass ready.
- Q2: What should accompany the sealed duty-free bag?
  - 正解：The receipt
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I bought a sealed duty-free item at the first airport. Can I take it through?
  - B02: Please show the sealed bag and receipt to the screening officer.
- Q3: Should the traveler open the sealed bag without instructions?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Should I open the bag so they can see the bottle?
  - B04: Do not open it unless instructed. The officer will explain the applicable rules.
