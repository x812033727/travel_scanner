## 測驗證據

- Q1: What is the current departure estimate?
  - 正解：Eleven thirty
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: The screen says delayed. Do you have a new departure time yet?
  - A02: The current estimate is eleven thirty, but it may change.
- Q2: Where is the service desk?
  - 正解：Beside gate eight
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: This delay may affect my connection. Can someone look at my booking?
  - B02: Yes. Please show your itinerary to the service desk beside gate eight.
- Q3: Is the connection guaranteed to wait?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Can you guarantee that the connecting flight will wait for me?
  - B04: I can't guarantee that. The service team can explain the available options.
