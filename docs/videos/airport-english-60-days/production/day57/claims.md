## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where are the train ticket machines?
  - 正解：Beside the station entrance
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Where can I buy a ticket for the airport train to the city?
  - A02: Follow the train signs downstairs. The ticket machines are beside the station entrance.
- Q2: Where is the official taxi queue?
  - 正解：Outside Door Three
  - 重播：B01, B02
  - B01: I would prefer a taxi. Where is the official taxi queue?
  - B02: It is outside Door Three. Follow the signs rather than unsolicited offers.
- Q3: Who can help find a suitable taxi?
  - 正解：The queue attendant
  - 重播：B05, B06
  - B05: Is there room for two large suitcases in a standard taxi?
  - B06: Ask the queue attendant to help you find a suitable vehicle.
