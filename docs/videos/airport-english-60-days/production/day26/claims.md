  - 重播：A03, A04

- Q2: What is about to close?
  - 正解：The boarding gate
  - 重播：A03, A04
  - A03: My travel companion is in the restroom. Can you wait a moment?
  - A04: Please contact your companion immediately. The gate is about to close.
- Q3: Does departure time mean the latest gate arrival time?
  - 正解：No
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Does the departure time mean the last moment I can arrive at the gate?
  - B04: No. Follow the boarding and gate-closing information for your flight.
