## 測驗證據

- Q1: Where is the first connection?
  - 正解：Dubai
  - 重播：A01, A02
  - A01: Before we check in, could you confirm that this flight goes to London?
  - A02: Yes. Flight eight-four-two goes to London, with a stop in Dubai.
- Q2: How long is the scheduled connection?
  - 正解：Three hours
  - 重播：A05, A06
  - A05: How much time do I have between the two flights?
  - A06: Your itinerary shows three hours. Please check the departure screen after landing.
- Q3: What is the updated departure time?
  - 正解：Nine twenty
  - 重播：B03, B04
  - B03: Yes. My email says eight fifteen, but the screen says nine twenty.
  - B04: The flight has been delayed. Nine twenty is the current estimated departure.
