## Treatment holds and corrections

1. **HOLD T01 — unanswered comparative “why bank?” question.** `treatment.md` lines 7, 11, 16, 27–28 promise to explain why the group chose its own licensed bank despite other convenience-store ATM partnerships. S1/S3 attribute the founding broadly to customer demand, and S6 documents the resulting own-account plus partner-ATM model. S6 p. 2 even depicts a joint ATM operating-company concept, but gives no comparative decision record. Chapter 3's evidence answers *what this bank did*, not *why a partnership could not do it*. Reframe the audience/opening/second title/chapter 2 question around what the new bank built beyond installing a machine, or obtain a contemporaneous primary decision source before keeping the comparative promise. Do not suggest a bank license was legally required merely to host an ATM.
2. **HOLD T02 — rollout funding inference.** The causal-arrow sentence at line 8 says the initial 66 machines expanded “靠地點、合作銀行與交易收費.” S7 supports geographic and partner expansion, and S3/S4 explain later ATM fee income; none establishes that transaction fees financed or caused the 2001–2007 rollout. Separate the chronology: the network expanded with sites and partners; later reports explain the fee model and its costs. Do not turn S6 p. 8's intended store-attraction synergy into a measured claim that any named ATM caused customer growth.

**Editorial clarity after these repairs:** Chapter 5 explicitly jumps from FY2009/10 accounts back to 2005 and 2007. Put the 2005/2007 events before the 2010 cost example, or label the rewind so numbers cannot sound contemporaneous. In the hook, specify that the customer-versus-bank fee distinction is the *partner-card* example. The 1987 bill-payment context and later ATM request are company-linked chronology, not independently established causation.

**Visual/thumbnail PASS with wording note:** The plan calls for a generic, unbranded ATM and store, no real card, screen, receipt, logo, or reconstructed 2001 store. “SEVEN BANK” is post-produced text under the series brand-title rule, not an image-model prompt. The thumbnail question “開店還是開銀行？” implies a false either/or; “超商為何開銀行？” better matches a retail group that continued operating stores while establishing a separate bank.
