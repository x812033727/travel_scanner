# 叫老公幫忙曬衣服的下場 #汪家夫妻

作品類型：寫實 AI 擬人化無厘頭短劇，約 15 秒、零台詞：真實外觀的狗像人一樣穿衣服過日子（柯基老公、柴犬老婆），只靠畫面、音效和剪接好笑；不用照真狗的行為

每一鏡依序：畫面（英文是給繪圖模型的描述）、畫面裡發生的事、台詞、音效。

## 1. s01-basket
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked
- 畫面：Medium shot of a cozy living room: the Corgi husband in striped pyjamas lies on the sofa scrolling a phone; the Shiba wife in a floral apron and hair curlers stands beside the sofa with a plastic laundry basket full of wet clothes
- 動作：the Shiba wife sets the laundry basket down next to the sofa and points firmly toward the balcony; the Corgi nods without looking up from the phone
- 無台詞，2 秒

## 2. s02-dump
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Wide shot of a small sunny balcony with an empty metal clothes drying rack; the Corgi husband in striped pyjamas stands holding the full laundry basket above the rack
- 動作：the Corgi tips the whole basket upside down so the wet clothes flop onto the rack in one messy heap, then drops the basket and waddles away out of frame
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：splat

## 3. s03-sofa
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked
- 畫面：Medium close-up of the Corgi husband lying back on the sofa with a phone above its face, a satisfied smug face
- 動作：the Corgi stretches its short legs out and wiggles into the cushions contentedly
- 無台詞，2 秒

## 4. s04-see
- 鏡頭：Medium shot, locked
- 畫面：Medium shot from behind: the Shiba wife in a floral apron with four pink hair curlers stands in the balcony doorway, looking at a messy heap of wet clothes dumped on the drying rack
- 動作：the four pink hair curlers pop off the Shiba's head one after another and bounce on the floor
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：pop

## 5. s05-curlers
- 鏡頭：Insert close-up, locked, floor level
- 畫面：Insert close-up at floor level: four pink plastic hair curlers bouncing on the light wood floor of the balcony doorway
- 動作：the pink hair curlers bounce once and roll to a stop
- 無台詞，1 秒

## 6. s06-turn
- 鏡頭：Close-up, slow push in
- 畫面：Close-up of the Shiba wife's face over its shoulder, hair now loose and frizzy without curlers, a calm sweet expression with narrowed eyes, the balcony behind
- 動作：the Shiba slowly turns its head toward the camera and its sweet smile tightens; its eyes do not blink
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：tension

## 7. s07-hung
- 鏡頭：Wide shot, locked
- 畫面：Wide shot of the sunny balcony: every piece of laundry now hung neatly and evenly on the clothesline, and in the middle of the line the Corgi husband in striped pyjamas hangs by four wooden clothespins clipped to the shoulders and back of its pyjamas, short legs dangling in the air, a sock on its head, a deadpan face
- 動作：the Corgi sways gently in the breeze like the laundry around it, its short legs paddle slowly in the air once
- 無台詞，3 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：wind

## 8. s08-tea
- 鏡頭：Medium close-up, locked
- 畫面：Medium close-up of the Shiba wife sitting on a small balcony stool holding a teacup, relaxed, with the Corgi's dangling short legs and striped pyjama hem visible at the top of the frame
- 動作：the Shiba turns to the camera and gives a sweet satisfied smile; the Corgi's legs above sway in the breeze
- 無台詞，2 秒
- 音效（這一鏡開始時）：birds
