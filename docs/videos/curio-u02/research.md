# U02 研究卷宗：為什麼 CIA 否認 51 區存在 58 年，2013 年突然承認？

讀法：每條事實給「主張 / 原文或數字 / 來源 / 信心」。信心分三級：**單一來源**、**兩個以上來源**、**來源不一致**。原文保留原語言，撰稿引用時請照抄原文再翻譯。凡標「⚠」者，撰稿只能當「傳聞」或「某人聲稱」處理。

## 0. 一句話結論（給撰稿）

「58 年否認」是新聞標題式的說法：1955 年選址到 2013 年公開，中間政府其實多次以「Groom Lake 附近的空軍作業地點」稱呼它（1995 年起每年總統令、1998 年空軍回應衛星照片、2000 年五角大廈發言）。2013 年真正新的是：CIA 自己的正史**第一次以「Area 51」這個名字、附地圖**公開，且是為了回覆 2005 年的一件資訊自由法申請，不是「突然良心發現」。

## 1. 傳說段：大家聽過的版本

### 1.1 羅斯威爾 1947：「飛碟」新聞稿隔天改口氣球
- 主張：1947 年 7 月 8 日羅斯威爾陸軍機場新聞稿說撿到「flying disc」，次日第八航空軍司令改口是雷達追蹤氣象氣球。
- 原文（GAO 報告）：RAAF 公共資訊室 "reported the crash and recovery of a 'flying disc'"；次日 "announced that RAAF personnel had recovered a crashed radar-tracking (weather) balloon, not a 'flying disc.'"　另：1994 年空軍報告認為 "the most likely source of the wreckage was from a balloon-launched classified government project"（即 Project Mogul）；GAO 只找到 "only two government records originating in 1947"，且 "RAAF outgoing messages (from Oct. 1946 through Dec. 1949) were destroyed."
- 來源：GAO, *Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico*, GAO/NSIAD-95-187, 1995-07-28. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GAOREPORTS-NSIAD-95-187/html/GAOREPORTS-NSIAD-95-187.htm
- 信心：單一來源（但為一手官方文件）。
- 查核註：羅斯威爾在新墨西哥州，與內華達 51 區相距約 1,000 公里；「殘骸運到 51 區」沒有任何官方文件支持，撰稿只能說「傳說裡」。CIA 史全文沒有 alien、creature 等字（NPR 2013 年檢索，見 3.4）。

### 1.2 Bob Lazar 1989 年的「聲稱」⚠
- 主張：1989 年 5 月 Lazar 以化名「Dennis」、隱去面孔在 KLAS-TV 接受 George Knapp 訪問，11 月 KLAS 系列「UFOs: The Best Evidence」公開他的本名；他聲稱在 Groom Lake 以南的「S-4」參與逆向工程九架飛碟，動力來自「元素 115」。
- 原文（Skeptic 雜誌）：他告訴 Knapp 自己 "worked at a secret facility called S4 near Area 51"，協助逆向工程一架 "not made by human hands" 的飛行器；作者結論：Lazar 的核心主張 "remains unproven"。
- 來源：Émile Kinley-Gauthier, "The Strange Case of Bob Lazar", *Skeptic*, 2026-04-23. https://www.skeptic.com/article/the-strange-case-of-bob-lazar/ ；KNPR（內華達公共電台）2015-02-12 報導：Lazar "told investigative reporter George Knapp there were nine saucers located in a hangar"，並在 25 週年訪問說 "wished he had never done the interview"。https://knpr.org/knpr/2015-02/area-51-whistleblower-speaks-ufo-conference
- 日期細節：KLAS 本站回顧文（8newsnow.com「I-Team: 25 years later」）寫首播為 1989 年 5 月；系列「UFOs: The Best Evidence」1989-11-06 起播。本次抓取被 8newsnow 的機器人驗證擋下，日期取自搜尋摘要，**未親見原頁**。
- 信心：主張本身為兩個以上來源轉述；S-4、九架飛碟、元素 115 **全是他的聲稱，無任何文件佐證**。

### 1.3 Lazar 學經歷查核結果⚠
- 主張：他自稱有 MIT 物理碩士與 Caltech 工程碩士，兩校均無就學紀錄；Los Alamos 國家實驗室沒有他的人事紀錄，只在 1982 年內部電話簿出現一筆標註承包商（Kirk-Mayer）的「Robert Lazar」；1982 年 *Los Alamos Monitor* 一篇噴射車報導稱他為「physicist」；1990 年在內華達承認一項 pandering 重罪。
- 原文（Skeptic）：懷疑論者包括 Stanton Friedman "reported finding no record of him at either institution"；"Lazar pleaded guilty in 1990 to a felony pandering charge in Nevada"。
- 來源：同 1.2 Skeptic 文章（學歷、Los Alamos Monitor、1990 認罪）；電話簿「K/M」標註與 Pierce Junior College 的細節來自 Friedman 的調查筆記轉載與維基百科索引，**非一手來源**。
- 信心：學歷無紀錄＝兩個以上來源（Knapp 與 Friedman 分別查過，皆經 Skeptic/維基轉述）；電話簿細節＝單一來源層級。撰稿建議只說「兩所學校查無紀錄；實驗室查無人事紀錄」。

### 1.4 「Storm Area 51」2019
- 主張：2019 年 6 月底一則玩笑性 Facebook 活動「Storm Area 51, They Can't Stop All of Us」在 7 月中突破 100 萬人報名，最終號稱逾 200 萬人「going」；9 月 20 日清晨實際到大門口的約 75 人，1 人被捕；前一晚 Rachel 與 Hiko 兩地節慶約 1,500 人。
- 原文（AP）："About 75 people arrived early Friday at a gate at the once-secret Area 51 military base in Nevada." "Lincoln County Sheriff Kerry Lee estimated late Thursday that about 1,500 people had gathered at the festival sites." "A man who was urinating near the gate was arrested and a woman was detained for an undisclosed reason, Lee said." "The military responded with stern warnings that lethal force could be used if people entered the Nevada Test and Training Range."
- 來源：AP, "About 75 people gather at Area 51 gate, 1 person arrested", HIKO, Nev., 2019-09-20（KTVU 與 Fox 5 DC 轉載）。https://www.ktvu.com/news/about-75-people-gather-at-area-51-gate-1-person-arrested ；NPR 2019-07-15 標題 "More Than 1 Million People Agree To 'Storm Area 51,' But The Air Force Says Stay Home" https://www.npr.org/2019/07/15/741938966/ ；NPR 2019-09-20 "'Storm Area 51' Fails To Materialize" https://www.npr.org/2019/09/20/762897934/storm-area-51-fails-to-materialize
- 信心：到場 75 人、1,500 人、1 人被捕＝兩個以上來源（AP 多家轉載）；「200 萬人」只見於 ABC/GMA 與 Digital Trends 等轉述（"more than 2 million"、"about 2.1 million"），**未見一手計數**，撰稿寫「號稱兩百多萬人按了參加」即可。NPR 兩篇本次抓取逾時，數字取自搜尋摘要與標題。
- 不一致：被捕人數 AP 當日寫 1 人，稍晚版本寫「至少 2 人被拘」、整個週末 5 人涉非法入侵；NPR 寫門口「約 40 人」而 AP 寫「約 75 人」（時間點不同）。撰稿用「數十人」最安全。

## 2. 檔案段：紀錄怎麼說

### 2.1 1955 年 4 月 12 日選址
- 主張：1955 年 4 月 12 日 Richard Bissell（CIA 計畫主持人）與空軍上校 Osmund Ritland、Lockheed 的 Kelly Johnson，搭由試飛員 Tony LeVier 駕駛的小型 Beechcraft 飛越內華達，發現 Groom Lake 鹽湖旁一條二戰廢棄跑道。
- 原文（CIA 史 p. 56）："On 12 April 1955 Richard Bissell and Col. Osmund Ritland (the senior Air Force officer on the project staff) flew over Nevada with Kelly Johnson in a small Beechcraft plane piloted by Lockheed's chief test pilot, Tony LeVier. They spotted what appeared to be an airstrip by a salt flat known as Groom Lake, near the northeast corner of the Atomic Energy Commission's (AEC) Nevada Proving Ground." … "From the air the strip appeared to be paved, but on closer inspection it turned out to have originally been fashioned from compacted earth that had turned into ankle-deep dust after more than a decade of disuse. If LeVier had attempted to land on the airstrip, the plane would probably have nosed over when the wheels sank into the loose soil, killing or injuring all of the key figures in the U-2 project."
- 來源：EBB 434, "U2 - Chapter 2.pdf", 書頁 56（PDF 第 18 頁）。https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB434/docs/U2%20-%20Chapter%202.pdf
- 信心：一手文件；CIA 官網 2015 年文章同述（見 2.3）。

### 2.2 「Area 51」名稱來源：地圖編號；Strauss 與艾森豪批准
- 原文（CIA 史 p. 56）："Bissell and his colleagues all agreed that Groom Lake would make an ideal site for testing the U-2 and training its pilots. Upon returning to Washington, Bissell discovered that Groom Lake was not part of the AEC proving ground. After consulting with Dulles, Bissell and Miller asked the Atomic Energy Commission to add the Groom Lake area to its real estate holdings in Nevada. AEC Chairman Adm. Lewis Strauss readily agreed, and President Eisenhower also approved the addition of this strip of wasteland, known by its map designation as Area 51, to the Nevada Test Site. The outlines of Area 51 are shown on current unclassified maps as a small rectangular area adjoining the northeast corner of the much larger Nevada Test Site."（最後一句跨到 p. 57）
- 來源：同上，書頁 56–57。
- 信心：一手文件。查核註：這段 1998 版整段塗黑，2013 版才可見。

### 2.3 「Paradise Ranch」「the Ranch」「Watertown Strip」
- 原文（CIA 史 p. 57）："To make the new facility in the middle of nowhere sound more attractive to his workers, Kelly Johnson called it the Paradise Ranch, which was soon shortened to the Ranch." … "project managers decided that a paved runway was needed so that testing could also take place during the times when rainwater runoff from nearby mountains filled the lake (at such times the base acquired yet another unofficial name, Watertown Strip). By July 1955 the base was ready, and Agency, Air Force, and Lockheed personnel began moving in."
- CIA 官網補充：Watertown 一名另有一說與 CIA 局長 Allen Dulles 出生地（紐約州 Watertown）有關，CIA 文章自己標為未經證實的傳聞；"Dreamland" 是 1960 年代末起的無線電呼號，指該空域。
- 來源：CIA 史 p. 57；CIA, "Area 51 and the Accidental Test Flight", 2015-08-06. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/area-51-and-the-accidental-test-flight/
- 信心：Paradise Ranch / Watertown 兩個以上來源；Dulles 出生地說法⚠。

### 2.4 第一次飛行：1955 年 8 月 1 日意外離地，8 月 4 日首飛，8 月 8 日「官方」首飛
- 原文（CIA 史 pp. 69–70）：1955 年 8 月 1 日高速滑行測試中，"'It was at this point that I became aware of being airborne,' LeVier noted afterward, 'which left me with utter amazement, as I had no intentions whatsoever of flying.'" … "Taxi trials continued for one more day and were followed by the first planned flight on 4 August 1955." … "Kelly Johnson scheduled the 'official' first flight for 8 August 1955. This time outsiders were present, including Richard Bissell, Col. Osmond Ritland, Richard Horner, and Garrison Norton. The U-2 flew to 32,000 feet and performed very well. Kelly Johnson had met his eight-month deadline."
- 來源：EBB 434 Chapter 2, 書頁 69–70；CIA 官網 2015 文章同述 8 月 1 日與 8 月 4 日。
- 信心：兩個以上來源。查核註：「首飛」有三個日期，撰稿要說清楚是哪一個。

### 2.5 U-2 的高度 vs. 當年客機
- 主張：U-2 設計巡航高度 70,000 呎以上；1950 年代中期客機飛 10,000–20,000 呎。
- 原文（CIA 史 Chapter 1）：空軍 1953 年需求書要求 "an optimum subsonic cruise speed at altitudes of 70,000 feet or higher"；Kelly Johnson 的 CL-282 設計 "maximum altitude would be just over 70,000 feet with a 2,000-mile range."（EBB 434 "U2 - Chapter 1.pdf" PDF 第 9、11 頁）
- 原文（CIA 史 p. 72）："In the mid-1950s, most commercial airliners flew at altitudes between 10,000 and 20,000 feet and military aircraft like the B-47s and B-57s operated at altitudes below 40,000 feet. Consequently, once U-2s started flying at altitudes above 60,000 feet, air-traffic controllers began receiving increasing numbers of UFO reports."
- 來源：EBB 434 Chapter 1、Chapter 2；史密森尼航太館 U-2C 館藏頁寫作業高度 "in excess of 60,000 feet"（https://www.si.edu/object/lockheed-u-2c:nasm_A19820380000 ，本次只讀到搜尋摘要）。
- 信心：兩個以上來源。查核註：CIA 史在 UFO 段用的是「above 60,000 feet」，設計目標才是 70,000；撰稿寫「六、七萬呎」或分別交代。

### 2.6 「U-2 佔了 UFO 通報一半以上」原文與頁碼
- 原文（CIA 史 pp. 72–73，小節標題 "U-2s, UFOs, AND OPERATION BLUE BOOK"）：
  p. 72："High-altitude testing of the U-2 soon led to an unexpected side effect—a tremendous increase in reports of unidentified flying objects (UFOs)." … "Such reports were most prevalent in the early evening hours from pilots of airliners flying from east to west. When the sun dropped below the horizon of an airliner flying at 20,000 feet, the plane was in darkness. But, if a U-2 was airborne in the vicinity of the airliner at the same time, its horizon from an altitude of 60,000 feet was considerably more distant, and, being so high in the sky, its silver wings would catch and reflect the rays of the sun and appear to the airliner pilot, 40,000 feet below, to be fiery objects. … At this time, no one believed manned flight was possible above 60,000 feet, so no one expected to see an object so high in the sky."
  p. 73："Not only did the airline pilots report their sightings to air-traffic controllers, but they and ground-based observers also wrote letters to the Air Force unit at Wright Air Development Command in Dayton charged with investigating such phenomena. This, in turn, led to the Air Force's Operation BLUE BOOK. Based at Wright-Patterson, the operation collected all reports of UFO sightings. Air Force investigators then attempted to explain such sightings by linking them to natural phenomena. BLUE BOOK investigators regularly called on the Agency's Project Staff in Washington to check reported UFO sightings against U-2 flight logs. This enabled the investigators to eliminate the majority of the UFO reports, although they could not reveal to the letter writers the true cause of the UFO sightings. U-2 and later OXCART flights accounted for more than one-half of all UFO reports during the late 1950s and most of the 1960s."
- 來源：EBB 434 Chapter 2, 書頁 72–73（PDF 第 34–35 頁）；CIA 官網 "Ask Molly: What really went on at Area 51?"（2022-07-01）照抄最後一句：https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-what-really-went-on-at-area-51/
- 信心：一手文件 + CIA 官網複述。
- 查核註（重要）：這個「一半以上」是 CIA 史家的說法，腳註引的是內部 OSA History，**沒有附統計**；Blue Book 總案件數與「U-2 解釋了多少件」沒有公開對帳。另外 CIA 史把 Blue Book 寫成 "Operation"，空軍正式名稱是 Project Blue Book。撰稿請寫「CIA 自己的史書說」，不要寫成「統計顯示」。

### 2.7 Project Blue Book 的規模與結論
- 原文（國家檔案館）："From 1947 to 1969, a total of 12, 618 sightings were reported to Project BLUE BOOK." 其中 701 件仍列 "Unidentified"；空軍結論之三："there has been no evidence indicating that sightings categorized as 'unidentified' are extraterrestrial vehicles." 紀錄存於 "94 rolls of 35mm microfilm (T-1206)"。空軍於 1969-12-17 宣布結束。
- 來源：NARA, "Project BLUE BOOK – Unidentified Flying Objects". https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos ；空軍 fact sheet 同數字。
- 信心：兩個以上來源。

### 2.8 1955 年 11 月 17 日運輸機失事（可用作「保密的代價」細節）
- 原文（CIA 史 p. 72）：Burbank–Area 51 的 MATS 定期班機 1955-10-03 開航，"Less than seven weeks after it started, a MATS aircraft bound for Area 51 crashed on 17 November, killing all 14 persons aboard the plane … This crash represented the greatest single loss of life in the entire U-2 program."
- 信心：一手文件。

### 2.9 OXCART / A-12：1959 年雷達測試、1962 年首飛
- 原文（CIA 史 p. 274）：1959-09-10，EG&G 同意 "to move its radar test facility to the former U-2 testing site at Area 51 of the Atomic Energy Commission's Nevada Proving Grounds."
- 原文（pp. 288–289）："On 25 April 1962, test pilot Louis Schalk took 'article 121' for an unofficial, unannounced flight, which was an old Lockheed tradition. He flew the craft less than two miles at an altitude of about 20 feet…" "on the next day, 26 April, Schalk made the official 40-minute maiden flight." "the OXCART's official first flight took place on 30 April 1962, witnessed by a number of Agency personnel including DDR Scoville. Richard Bissell was also present … the OXCART took off at 170 knots and climbed to 30,000 feet. During the 59-minute flight, the A-12 achieved a top speed of 340 knots." "On 2 May 1962, during the second test flight, the OXCART broke the sound barrier, achieving a speed of Mach 1.1."
- 設計性能（p. 262 一帶）：Mach 3.2、90,000 呎；CIA 博物館頁：A-12 "Mach 3.29 (just over 2,200 miles per hour) at 90,000 feet altitude"，唯一實戰部署 BLACK SHIELD 1967-05 至 1968-05，"President Johnson ordered retirement of the A-12 by 1968." https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/untouchable/
- 來源：EBB 434 "U2 - Chapter 6.pdf" https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB434/docs/U2%20-%20Chapter%206.pdf
- 信心：一手文件。查核註：A-12 首飛也有 4/25、4/26、4/30 三個日期，Commons 圖說各執一詞（見第 6 節）。

### 2.10 之後的機種：HAVE BLUE、F-117、蘇聯 MiG
- 主張：1977 年 4 月隱形驗證機 HAVE BLUE 在 51 區首飛；F-117 的作業基地是 Tonopah Test Range（1981-06-18 首飛，1988-11 公開）；1960 年代末在 51 區測試取得的蘇聯 MiG-21（HAVE DOUGHNUT）、MiG-17（HAVE DRILL / HAVE FERRY）。
- 原文（NSA Archive EBB 443）：HAVE BLUE "resulted in two experimental aircraft, with a first flight in April 1977"；MiG-21 計畫 "was designated HAVE DOUGHNUT"；"Two other late 1960s exploitation efforts at Area 51 both focused on evaluating the MiG-17"。
- 來源：National Security Archive, "The Area 51 File: Secret Aircraft and Soviet MiGs", EBB 443, 2013-10-29（60 餘份解密文件）。https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence/2013-10-29/area-51-file-secret-aircraft-soviet-MiGs ；F-117 首飛日期與 Tonopah：Air & Space Forces Magazine 轉載空軍 fact sheet（"First Flight: June 18, 1981"；"Tonopah Test Range, Nev."）https://www.airandspaceforces.com/weapons-platforms/f-117/ ；af.mil 原頁本次 403。
- 信心：HAVE BLUE 與 MiG＝NSA Archive 文件集（單一彙整來源，但內含一手文件）；F-117 首飛**地點**來源不一致：空軍資料寫 Tonopah，維基寫 Groom Lake。撰稿只說「F-117 以 Tonopah 為基地」，首飛地點不要講。

## 3. 為什麼保密這麼久、2013 年到底公開了什麼

### 3.1 1998 版 vs. 2013 版
- 主張：同一本 1992 年機密史，1998 年先公開 U-2 部分（272 頁，Area 51 全塗黑）；2013 年 6 月 25 日核准、8 月 15 日由 National Security Archive 公開的版本（CIA 閱覽室 406 頁）恢復了 Area 51 名稱、地圖、飛行員姓名、代號、經費、海外合作、亞洲行動等。
- 原文（EBB 434 導言）：Richelson 2005 年提出 FOIA 申請要求較少塗黑的版本；"The CIA completed its review and approved release on 25 June 2013."（導言摘述）；1998 版 CIA 自述 "This book is a partially redacted version of the classified original, which was published in 1992."
- 來源：EBB 434 https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/NSAEBB/NSAEBB434/ ；CIA 1998 版頁面。
- 信心：兩個以上來源。頁數不一致：新聞寫 400 頁或 407 頁，CIA 閱覽室 PDF 為 406 頁。撰稿寫「約 400 頁」。
- 歷史註：英國 U-2 史家 Chris Pocock 在 EBB 434 附的評註說多數「新」內容他的書早已寫過；CIA 史本身稱他的 *Dragon Lady* 是 "by far the most accurate unclassified account of the U-2 program."

### 3.2 2013 年「新」在哪：名字＋地圖
- 主張：2013 年是 CIA 第一次經高層決定，在官方文件中承認「Area 51」一詞及其具體位置（p. 58 整頁地圖，標題 "Area 51"，插圖顯示 Groom Lake 緊鄰 "AEC Nevada Test Site" 東北角，右下角 Las Vegas、Indian Springs）。
- 原文（Reuters, Steve Gorman, 2013-08-17）：400 頁文件 "contains the first deliberate official references to Area 51"；Richelson："It's the first time that there must have been a senior-level decision to acknowledge the term 'Area 51' and its specific location." CIA 發言人 Edward Price："What readers won't find are any references to aliens or other conspiracy theories."
- 來源：Reuters 經 Christian Science Monitor 轉載 https://www.csmonitor.com/USA/Latest-News-Wires/2013/0817/Area-51-is-real-say-CIA-documents ；地圖見 EBB 434 Chapter 2 PDF 第 20 頁（書頁 58）。
- 信心：兩個以上來源。Richelson 另對 AP 說他 2002 年就看過全被塗黑的版本，2005 年再申請——2002 一事只見 AP 轉述（單一來源）。

### 3.3 其實早就「承認」過：1995 年起每年總統令
- 主張：1995 年 9 月 29 日起，柯林頓、小布希每年簽署總統決定，依《資源保育與回收法》42 U.S.C. 6961(a) 豁免「空軍 Groom Lake 附近作業地點」的有害廢棄物資訊揭露義務，以免洩露機密；聯邦公報可查到 1995–2003 共 9 件。
- 原文（PD 95-45）：標題 "Presidential Determination on Classified Information Concerning the Air Force's Operating Location Near Groom Lake, Nevada"；"pursuant to 42 U.S.C. Sec. 6961(a)"；"it is in the paramount interest of the United States to exempt the United States Air Force's operating location near Groom Lake, Nevada"；"shall be effective for the full one-year statutory period"。
- 來源：Federal Register 1995-10-10, 60 FR, doc. 95-25244 https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1995-10-10/html/95-25244.htm ；聯邦公報 API 檢索（2026-10-10）列出 1995、1996、1997、1998、1999、2000、2001、2002、2003 共 9 件，**2003 年之後聯邦公報查無**；柯林頓致國會信（1996-01-31）把該豁免與訴訟 Kasza v. Browner 直接連結 https://clintonwhitehouse6.archives.gov/1996/01/1996-01-31-determination-exempting-usaf-from-conservation-laws.html
- 信心：一手文件。查核註：2004 年以後是否仍逐年簽署，本次未能從一手來源確認（見第 5 節），撰稿寫「至少到 2003 年每年簽」。

### 3.4 1994–1998 工人訴訟 Kasza v. Browner / Frost v. Perry
- 主張：前基地工人與遺孀以 RCRA 公民訴訟控告 EPA 與空軍，指基地露天焚燒有害廢棄物；空軍部長主張國家機密特權，第九巡迴法院 1998-01-08 維持駁回。
- 原文（Environmental Law Reporter 摘要）：兩件 RCRA 公民訴訟針對 "classified military installation near Groom Lake, Nevada"；法院認定空軍援用特權 "was not overbroad"，採 "mosaic theory"，因 "the very subject matter of the action is a state secret" 而維持駁回；對 EPA 的揭露請求因總統豁免而 moot。
- 來源：*Kasza v. Browner*, 133 F.3d 1159 (9th Cir. 1998)，ELR 20449 摘要 http://www.elr.info/litigation/kasza-v-browner ；後續 *Frost v. Rumsfeld / DR v. Whitman*（9th Cir. 2003-04-14）重述前案 https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/us-9th-circuit/1054233.html ；案號 D. Nev. CV-S-94-795-PMP 見 1996 柯林頓信。
- 信心：兩個以上來源（判決原文本次 Justia/CourtListener 皆未能取得，依法學摘要與後續判決）。「露天焚燒」「工人」的具體指控**未從一手判決文確認**，撰稿用「工人聲稱」。

### 3.5 衛星與太空照片：1974、1998、2000
- 主張：1974 年 Skylab 4 太空人違反指示拍到 Groom Lake；2000 年 4 月 17 日 Aerial Images Inc. 在 TerraServer 公開 1998 年 3 月俄國衛星 2 公尺解析度影像；同年 FAS 訂購的 IKONOS 影像公開。
- 原文：CIA 1974-04-19 備忘錄 "There were specific instructions not to do this"，Groom "was the only location which had such an instruction"（Dwayne A. Day, *The Space Review*, 2023-01-23 更新版）https://thespacereview.com/article/4518/1 ；*Las Vegas Sun* 2000-04-18（Mary Manning）："The companies launched a Russian satellite from Kazahkstan in 1998 … Area 51, the Nevada Test Site and the Tonopah Test Range are all visible in the photos shot in March 1998."；CBS 2000-04-17："The panchromatic, or black-and-white, images have a 2-meter resolution." https://www.cbsnews.com/news/satellite-image-of-area-51/
- 信心：1998/2000 影像＝兩個以上來源；Skylab＝單一（但引 CIA 備忘錄）。空軍 2000 年回應 "We acknowledge having an operating site there, and the work is classified"（Las Vegas Sun 轉述，本次只見搜尋摘要）⚠。
- 查核註：題目要的「USGS/Landsat」：USGS 1968 年航照確實存在（Commons 檔案，見第 6 節；Day 文提到 1959 與 1968 兩批 USGS 航照早就躺在公開檔案庫）；Commons 上那張 "Landsat GeoCover 2000" 其實是 NASA World Wind 截圖，且有人質疑是商業影像，**不要用它說「Landsat 拍到」**。

### 3.6 2013 年的接收
- NPR（2013-08-16, The Two-Way）：報告 "not only mentions Nevada's Area 51, it places it on a map"，並被視為 "the CIA's most public acknowledgement ever that Area 51 really is a government facility"；NPR 另查全文 "the words 'space,' 'alien' and 'creature' don't seem to appear."（本次 NPR 頁面抓取逾時，引自搜尋摘要）https://www.npr.org/sections/thetwo-way/2013/08/16/212549163/
- Reuters 與 AP 皆提醒：柯林頓與小布希早已多次用「location near Groom Lake」一詞；AP 版還寫 "Area 51 has also been referenced in government documents in the past"。
- 2013-12-08 歐巴馬在甘迺迪中心榮譽獎致詞提到 Area 51，NPR 稱是首位公開提及的總統（搜尋摘要，⚠未親見）。
- 信心：單一來源層級，只用於「反應」段。

## 4. 什麼還是機密

- CIA 史 2013 版仍有大量塗黑（新聞：407 頁 "still contains many redactions"）；OSA（Office of Special Activities）內部史仍未全部公開（EBB 434 導言）。
- 總統令與法院的理由是「mosaic theory」：連「有沒有有害廢棄物」都算機密（ELR 摘要）。
- 1977 年後的隱形機、MiG 計畫只部分解密；TACIT BLUE 與 Bird of Prey "Both have been partly declassified"（EBB 443）。
- 今天官方對該地的稱呼仍是 Nevada Test and Training Range 的一部分；空軍對 2019 年活動的回應只說它是訓練場、擅闖將被制止，沒有說明用途（AP）。

## 5. 未能以非維基來源確認、或來源不一致的事

1. **2004 年以後是否仍每年簽總統豁免令**：聯邦公報 API 只到 2003 年；可能改以備忘錄形式不刊公報，未能確認。
2. **Storm Area 51「200 萬人」**：只見 ABC/GMA、Digital Trends 等轉述，無 Facebook 一手計數；NPR 7 月 15 日「逾 100 萬」有標題可引。
3. **空軍對 Storm Area 51 的原話**（發言人 Laura McAndrews、"any attempt to illegally access…"）：本次未取得原文，請勿直接引述。
4. **Lazar 的 Los Alamos 電話簿「K/M」標註、Pierce Junior College、Pacifica University**：僅 Friedman 調查筆記轉載與維基索引。
5. **Watertown＝Dulles 出生地**：CIA 文章自己標為傳聞。
6. **F-117 首飛地點**：空軍寫 Tonopah，維基寫 Groom Lake，不一致。
7. **A-12 首飛日期**：CIA 史 4/26（官方 40 分鐘首飛）與 4/30（官方首飛、收輪）並列；Commons 圖說還有 5/4，不一致。
8. **2013 版頁數**：400 / 406 / 407 三種說法。
9. **Richelson 2002 年先看過塗黑版**：僅 AP 轉述。
10. **Kasza 案一手判決文**：Justia、CourtListener、FindLaw 皆取不到全文；「露天焚燒」細節只在二手。
11. **歐巴馬 2013-12-08 提及 Area 51**：僅 NPR 搜尋摘要。
12. **Las Vegas Sun 2000 年空軍「acknowledge having an operating site」原句**：頁面抓到一半被付費牆截斷。
