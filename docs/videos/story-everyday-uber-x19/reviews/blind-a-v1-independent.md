## Action, metric, Shorts, and ending

- **Causal answer.** The supported sequence is legible: a previously smaller Delivery network grows while Mobility demand collapses, some drivers get Delivery work, and Delivery's 2020 Q2 Gross Bookings exceed Mobility's. `[x190618]`–`[x190621]` eventually summarize this, but the summary comes after the 2022 result. Move that synthesis beside x190301–x190305. Keep x190621's limit: the script cannot apportion the crossover among demand, dispatch changes, and new merchants. Do not imply Postmates caused Q2; `[x190401]` and `[x190501]` correctly place signing and closing after it.
- **Money versus counts and profit.** The central comparison is dollar Gross Bookings, not number of orders, recognized revenue, or profit. `[x190416] 外送拿下較多訂單，載客卻還在支撐更多部門獲利。` makes “more orders” sound like a count finding. `[x190623] 訂單額回答「誰跑得多」，部門指標才接著問「誰留得住」。` risks the same inference. Say “外送的訂單總金額較大” and “訂單額回答哪邊的交易金額較大；部門調整後 EBITDA 回答另一題.” The June contrast at x190304/x190317/x190318 is useful; later GAAP net-income figures need far less air time.
- **Opening and handoffs.** x190101–x190104 give an observable action. x190105–x190109 then stack five questions, with July and December outcomes partly disclosed. Keep three distinct future hooks and the final governing question, but let one unanswered moment breathe. The chapter transitions themselves are sound: the last question in each chapter finds an answer in the next chapter's first line.
- **Short 1 (slug `story-everyday-uber-x19-short-1`).** It stands alone on the Gross Bookings versus adjusted EBITDA distinction, but its `proof` beat at shot `x19-b03-s09` says `誰接到較多交易，與誰留下獲利，是兩題。` “較多交易” implies transaction count. Change it to “哪邊交易總額較大”. Its first title, `Uber 外送交易額超車，為何還虧？`, promises a cost explanation the Short does not provide; `外送交易額超車，為何不等於賺錢？` matches the actual payoff. Name adjusted EBITDA in the spoken loss line, as well as on the card, so audio-only viewers know which profit scale is being used.
- **Short 2 (slug `story-everyday-uber-x19-short-2`).** It is standalone on the timing argument: Q2 ends, July 6 signs, Q3 is still pre-close, December 1 closes. Its `proof` beat at shot `x19-b04-s09` says `第三季還沒交割，外送仍有八十五點五億。` while the headline claims it is still ahead. Add the Mobility comparator (五十九點零五億美元) to that beat so the lead is demonstrated within the Short, not borrowed from the long video. The price in the July beat is less useful than this comparator.
- **Exactly three outro lines.** The outro has x190625, x190626, and x190627 only: answer, comment question, subscription invitation. The invitation gives a reason and names no next video. The first answer has no numeric payoff and restates earlier causes/dates. A tighter final answer can give the new synthesis “2020 年第二季，外送訂單額約為載客的 2.3 倍” and name the demand/dispatch combination, while keeping the causal-apportionment limit. The question at x190626 is answerable as a viewer preference.
