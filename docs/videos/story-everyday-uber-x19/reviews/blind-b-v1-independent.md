## Checks that matter most on a first listen

**Opening.** The first four lines are concrete enough for Q1: [x190101] changes a driver's dispatch in April 2020, [x190102] says those drivers are also sent food jobs, [x190103] places one phone between passenger and bag, and [x190104] says city demand diverges. The first line could show the food order arriving on the phone instead of naming a “派單方式,” but this is a polish point rather than a panel failure. June, July, December, and eventual passenger return provide at least three future moments. December presently crosses the reveal boundary: [x190107] says integration **started**, while chapter 5 later treats that as a reveal. Keep the moment; withhold the operational result.

**Handoffs and genuine payoffs.** The major chapter turns work: [x190222] asks if the platform amounts will swap and [x190301] answers; [x190323] asks whether a newly mentioned delivery network caused the crossing and [x190401] puts its July agreement after the June quarter; [x190522] asks when delivery's own adjusted measure turns positive and [x190601] supplies the 2021 Q4 result. Chapter 4 ends [x190422] by asking whether the networks actually joined; chapter 5 first gives the legal close [x190501], then only at [x190502]–[x190504] distinguishes integration in progress from two still separate consumer apps. That is comprehensible, but its first answer would be sharper as “the deal closed; the networks were only beginning to join.” The per-order-cost question at [x190420] has no payoff, and a later department total cannot establish unit cost. The [x190107] integration-versus-loss question should likewise resolve with the later department metric **without attributing the improvement to the acquisition**.

**Chronology and denominators.** The narrated order runs 2019 Q2 baseline → 2020 Q1 → April dispatch → 2020 Q2 crossover → July agreement → 2020 Q3 while the deal is unclosed → December completion/integration → 2021 Q2 migration → 2021 Q4 adjusted delivery positive → 2022 Q4 rides bookings larger again. That ordering supports the point that December completion did not cause the June crossover. The gross-bookings comparisons themselves use matched quarters and matched measures. However, the climactic sequence puts [x190303]「載客部門確認的營收，也降到七點九億美元。」 **between** mobility bookings at [x190302] and delivery bookings at [x190304]. A listener can temporarily compare unlike amounts. Put the two booking figures next to each other, then explain recognized revenue once. [x190312]–[x190313] also say consumers and trips fell “由…降到…” without audibly restating that the baseline is the prior-year quarter; add “比去年同季” to one sentence. Segment adjusted EBITDA and whole-company GAAP net profit are distinguished in words, but repeating that distinction at every later quarter weakens the forward motion.

**Repetition and ending.** The late sequence [x190617]–[x190624] circles through the demand shock, dispatch change, July/December deal timing, and booking-versus-profit distinction after the 2022 result. Those facts have already been established; [x190622] is the clearest unmarked duplicate. [x190615]–[x190616] add yet another company-net-profit caveat after [x190517]–[x190519] and [x190607]–[x190608]. End instead on the 2022 car and bag image, one sentence limiting causal attribution, and the answer.

**The three outro lines.** [x190625]「外送先超過載客，是乘客急縮、既有外送擴張與派單轉向相遇；那比的是訂單額，Postmates 還沒交割。」 answers the premise but repeats the preceding explanation and gives no new scale. A shorter callback could add the new approximate ratio (2020 Q2 delivery bookings were about 2.3 times mobility bookings), while saying the script cannot apportion the contribution of each move. [x190626]「如果你看同一季的訂單額和部門損益，你會先盯哪個數字？」 is answerable but sounds like an accounting quiz; a viewer question about which changed first in their own riding/ordering habits would connect more naturally to the car-and-bag story. [x190627] gives a specific reason to subscribe and avoids a next-video tease.

**Short 1, standalone.** Its hook gives the year, quarter, subject, and anomaly; its booking figures (delivery 69.61億, mobility 30.46億) are on the same platform-order basis; it then distinguishes company revenue from department adjusted EBITDA and lands “超車不等於賺.” It stands alone. Its existing「全公司那一季確認營收」wording usefully identifies the wider denominator.

**Short 2, standalone.** The June-before-July-before-December timeline directly defeats the proposed acquisition explanation, and the separate-app/integrating-network ending is useful. Its Q3 beat says [headline]「交割前仍領先」 and narrates「第三季還沒交割，外送仍有八十五點五億。」 It does not give the Q3 mobility comparator, so the standalone viewer cannot check “still ahead” from the shown 85.5億 alone. Add mobility's 59.05億 in the card or narration; keep both explicitly labeled as Q3 platform bookings. The acquisition price is optional to this Short's causal payoff.
