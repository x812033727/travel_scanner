## Required corrections before PASS

1. **North Side flashback is lit as day.** `s56127` says “同夜稍早” after Foster's Monday-dawn sequence, and Loesch dates the street ignition shortly **after 1 a.m. Monday** [L378–404](https://github.com/GITenberg/Personal-Experiences-During-the-Chicago-Fire-1871_66433/blob/08931a6f2629fd4b8be37b1ec548857d48d17eb9/66433-0.txt#L378-L404). Yet nine shot prompts in `s56127–s56143` explicitly request `pale overcast light` or `grey daylight`: **`s56127`, `s56130`, `s56132`, `s56135`, `s56136`, `s56138`, `s56139`, `s56141`, `s56142`**. The five intervening prompts use ambiguous smoke-filtered light. Re-anchor the entire 14-shot flashback to night or predawn; the later Monday daytime sequence can then brighten. `s56135` also places the scorched refugee beside the lake, whereas Loesch describes seeing her **later after** she took that route [L334–342](https://github.com/GITenberg/Personal-Experiences-During-the-Chicago-Fire-1871_66433/blob/08931a6f2629fd4b8be37b1ec548857d48d17eb9/66433-0.txt#L334-L342).
2. **Waterworks visuals overstate who saw what and what existed.** `s56147` puts Loesch looking at the smoking Chicago Avenue pumping station. His 1925 text reports its destruction [L547–557](https://github.com/GITenberg/Personal-Experiences-During-the-Chicago-Fire-1871_66433/blob/08931a6f2629fd4b8be37b1ec548857d48d17eb9/66433-0.txt#L547-L557), but does not say he personally observed the station or its destruction. `s56146` depicts an empty **hand pump** as the image for municipal waterworks loss, even though Loesch later describes usable **well water** [L589–594](https://github.com/GITenberg/Personal-Experiences-During-the-Chicago-Fire-1871_66433/blob/08931a6f2629fd4b8be37b1ec548857d48d17eb9/66433-0.txt#L589-L594). `s56148–s56151` show a dry hose, dry hydrant, or parked/unused engines as facts on screen; Loesch says engines **could have been obtained** and uses their hypothetical effectiveness as retrospective judgment. Keep the station report and hypothetical equipment visibly attributed without inventing Loesch at the station or actual parked engines and dry hand pumps.
3. **The carriage payment shot reverses the letter's sequence.** The shared `s56112–s56113` image prompt shows Foster handing a coin to the driver while narration first says he agreed to five dollars and then loaded his boxes. Foster explicitly refused advance payment and **paid only after the packages were aboard** [F420–428](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Letter-from-the-Fire-Being-an-account-of-the-Great-Chicago-Fire_66472/blob/60c777551301c30942acf749d4bb37ca32752353/66472-0.txt#L420-L428). Show loading or the loaded wagon when payment appears.
4. **The family-copy image collapses the source layers.** `s56017` has the traveler setting down a “plain copied page” while the narrator describes the lost original and family copy. The copy is known through the **1923 editor**, after Foster's 1871 letter [F161–171](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Letter-from-the-Fire-Being-an-account-of-the-Great-Chicago-Fire_66472/blob/60c777551301c30942acf749d4bb37ca32752353/66472-0.txt#L161-L171). Showing Foster with that copy suggests it existed in his hands then, and “copied page” may invite generated lettering. Use a clearly later/editorial or blank conceptual document, with no readable facsimile.
5. **Two narration claims need narrower wording.** `s56077` says Foster “並沒看見火屑飛越全程.” [F360–370](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Letter-from-the-Fire-Being-an-account-of-the-Great-Chicago-Fire_66472/blob/60c777551301c30942acf749d4bb37ca32752353/66472-0.txt#L360-L370) establishes what he **wrote** (two fires and his inference), not everything he did or did not see. Say the letter does not record him following one ember across. `s56160` says the plowed furrows “只護住眼前這片地,” implying demonstrated protection; Loesch says they were planned as **partial protection**, then rain came before that defense was tested [L578–609](https://github.com/GITenberg/Personal-Experiences-During-the-Chicago-Fire-1871_66433/blob/08931a6f2629fd4b8be37b1ec548857d48d17eb9/66433-0.txt#L578-L609). Say they were intended to protect that field.
6. **Claims table has a wrong source range.** `s56155` (“有時間先搬走或埋起家具”) cites only P11 / **L578–620**, which has no furniture burial. The claim is supported at [L537–545](https://github.com/GITenberg/Personal-Experiences-During-the-Chicago-Fire-1871_66433/blob/08931a6f2629fd4b8be37b1ec548857d48d17eb9/66433-0.txt#L537-L545). `s56154` also needs L537–541 for the daytime move. Correct the row references without changing the historical claim.
7. **Thumbnail count conflicts with the source.** `video.json` thumbnail subline says “一夜兩次換岸.” Foster labels a **first, second, and third** river crossing [F440–459](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Letter-from-the-Fire-Being-an-account-of-the-Great-Chicago-Fire_66472/blob/60c777551301c30942acf749d4bb37ca32752353/66472-0.txt#L440-L459): West → South, South → North, North → West. Use an unnumbered subline or the accurate count.
8. **A few shared shots lag the narration's new location or action.** The `s56017` shot remains a later copied document while `s56018` narrates Foster viewing the October 7 wooden-house fire; the `s56107` cupola shot remains on screen when `s56108` narrates carrying luggage downstairs; the `s56128` tunnel remains on screen when `s56129` narrates boarders packing back at the house. `s56081` also remains at the gasworks while `s56082` says Foster knocked on doors. Align those prompts or scene boundaries so a viewer can follow the already-supported chronology. The `s56017` source-layer fix and `s56112` payment fix above take priority.

Smaller precision edit for the same author pass: `s56109` calls Ackroyd a **friend who entrusted** Foster with a trunk; the letter establishes that the trunk belonged to Ackroyd and that Foster took it into his hotel [F220–226](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Letter-from-the-Fire-Being-an-account-of-the-Great-Chicago-Fire_66472/blob/60c777551301c30942acf749d4bb37ca32752353/66472-0.txt#L220-L226), [F414–419](https://github.com/GITenberg/A-Letter-from-the-Fire-Being-an-account-of-the-Great-Chicago-Fire_66472/blob/60c777551301c30942acf749d4bb37ca32752353/66472-0.txt#L414-L419), but does not establish that relationship or an explicit entrustment. “艾克羅伊德的大箱子” is sufficient.
