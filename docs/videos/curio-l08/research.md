## 一、兩個人是誰

1. **兩人是風景畫家，1991 年分別 67 歲與 62 歲。**【兩源】
   S1：「two land-scape painters, David Chorley, 62, and Douglas Bower, 67」。S2：「described by the newspaper as "jovial con men in their 60's"」。S6 給 Bower 生日 1924-06-25，推算 1991 年 9 月為 67 歲，吻合。Chorley 的年齡只有 S1 一源。
2. **兩人來自英格蘭南部漢普郡，Britannica 寫南安普敦。**【兩源】
   S5：「Doug Bower and Dave Chorley, of Southampton, England」。S17：「two men from Hampshire」。S6：Bower 1968 年回漢普郡後「opened a gallery and picture-framing studio」。
3. **每週五晚上固定見面喝一杯，兩人都是皇家空軍退伍。**【單源 S6；週五酒吧另有 S16 旁證】
   S6：「his friend and fellow RAF veteran Dave Chorley」「They used to meet for a beer every Friday evening」。
   酒吧名稱「Percy Hobbs」只在通俗網站出現，【未證實】，**不要念出店名**；S1 只說「a pub near Cheesefoot Head」。

## 二、什麼時候開始、為什麼

4. **起始年份：1976 或 1978，來源分歧。**【分歧】
   - 1976：S3「first created a representation of a "flying saucer nest" in a wheat field in Wiltshire, England, in 1976」；S6「In 1976 … snuck into a wheat field on the Longwood Estate and laid down a simple circle, 30ft (9m) across」；S19「One Friday night in June 1976」。
   - 1978：S1「Chorley and Bower say they conceived their hoax in 1978」；S17（轉述 Today）「back in 1978」；S16 站上另一頁轉錄的 Chorley 口述「I guess it was 1978」。
   - 建議旁白：「他們自己說，是 1970 年代後半；有的報導寫 1976，有的寫 1978。」
5. **第一個圈的地點也不一致。**【分歧】S3 寫 Wiltshire；同一位作者在 S6 寫 Longwood Estate（近 Winchester，屬 Hampshire），S19 亦寫 Hampshire。建議說「英格蘭南部的麥田」。
6. **靈感來自澳洲的「飛碟巢」。**【兩源】
   S5：「inspired by a 1966 account of a UFO sighting near Tully, Queensland」。S4：Bower 向 Chorley 講了「an Australian farmer who had reported a UFO rising into the sky and leaving behind a circular "saucer nest"」。S3 引 Bower 的話：「Let's go over there and make it look like a flying saucer has landed.」
   S1 的說法不同：「recalling crop circles created with tractors by Australian farmers several years earlier」。【分歧，小】
7. **Bower 住過澳洲，但住的是維多利亞，不是昆士蘭；他是在報上讀到的。**【兩源】
   S6：約 1958 年以「£10 assisted passage to Victoria」移居，1968 年回英國；「the mysterious 'saucer nests' he'd read about in a newspaper」。S16：「I saw an article in the Australian newspaper one day, about some circles in some reed and grass in Tully in Queensland」。居留年數 S16 口述為「eight and a half years」，與 S6 推算的約十年不同。【分歧，小】
8. **Tully 事件本身：1966 年 1 月，香蕉農 George Pedley 說看到碟形物從潟湖升起，蘆葦留下約 30 呎的圓。**【單源 S18】
   S18：「On a clear morning in January 1966, banana farmer George Pedley … saw what he described as a flying saucer」「tall reeds were now woven into a 30-foot diameter circle」。那是潟湖蘆葦，不是麥田。日期「1 月 19 日」與官方調查結論只在愛好者網站見到，【未證實】。
9. **頭幾年沒人注意，直到報紙報了才成事。**【分歧：年份】
   S1：「this and other forays during the next three years went unnoticed. But one of their circles was spotted in 1981」。S6：「in 1980, in a field near Westbury, someone noticed and their circle made the newspapers … "The Return of the Thing", ran the headline」。

## 三、工具與做法

10. **最早用的是一根鐵棒。**【單源，圈內人自述】S19：「Using a heavy iron bar - normally employed to secure the rear doors of Doug's picture-framing gallery - they swirled a simple circle, about 30 feet across」。
11. **後來是木板、繩子，加上棒球帽上的鐵絲瞄準器。**【兩源】
    S1：「a 4-ft. long wooden plank, a ball of string and a baseball cap with wire threaded through the visor as a sighting device」；做法：Bower 在圓心拉住繩子一端，Chorley 把木板「held horizontally at knee level」繞圈推倒麥子。
    S2 引 Today：「two wooden boards, a piece of string and a bizarre sighting device attached to a baseball cap」。
    S4：「a "sight" that consisted of a circular wire attached to his cap」；工具通稱「"stompers" (wooden planks attached to two hand-held ropes)」。
    「stalk stomper」這個確切說法未在上列來源找到（S4 只用 stompers）。【未證實】木板是一塊還是兩塊，S1 與 S2 不同。【分歧，小】
12. **他們宣稱的數量：說法不一。**【分歧】
    S5「more than 200」；S4「confessed to making 250 formations」；S1「as many as 25 to 30 new circles each growing season」持續 13 年（相乘約 325–390）；S17「hundreds」。建議說「兩百多個」。

## 四、1991 年 9 月的自白

13. **1991-09-09，小報 Today 刊出，標題〈Men Who Conned the World〉。**【兩源】
    S2：「According to Today, which broke the story with the headline "Men Who Conned the World,"」。S17 給日期 9 月 9 日。Today 原版面未取得，內容皆為他報轉述；「頭版」一說只見於次級來源。
14. **設局：兩人在記者眼前做了一個圈，請專家來鑑定。**【兩源】
    S1：Today 記者 Graham Brough 在肯特郡 Sevenoaks 把 Pat Delgado 介紹給兩人，「They had created the Sevenoaks circle while Brough looked on」。Delgado 先說：「No human could have done this.」Bower 說：「I'm afraid we've been having you on.」Delgado：「We have all been conned」。
    S2：專家把肯特郡麥田的圖案判為「the genuine article, of the sort no human could have made」。**S2 把他的名字誤寫成「David Delgado」**，以 S1 的 Pat Delgado 為準。
15. **Delgado 隨後改口，主張現象仍在。**【兩源】
    S1：「The two gents may have hoaxed some of the circles … but the phenomenon is still there」。S2：「Have these guys been out there with their board?」（指加拿大也有圈）。
    Delgado 的身分：S1 稱「retired engineer」，與 Colin Andrews 合著 *Circular Evidence*，「sold more than 50,000 copies」。他是否在世、何時過世：【未證實】，節目不要提。
16. **當天稍晚，兩人在記者面前再示範一次。**【分歧：地點】
    S2：「demonstrated their craft before reporters in a field in Hampshire」；Colin Andrews 的反應：「There is nothing here to impress us at all except two very fit 60-year-olds」。通俗網站寫示範地在 Warminster（Wiltshire），無可靠來源。**被騙的鑑定圈在肯特、公開示範在漢普郡，是兩件事，不要混為一談。**
17. **為什麼說出來。**【分歧】
    S1：「It was only after circle enthusiasts began seeking government funding that the two jovial con men decided to admit」。S6：公開「was not his choice but Dave's」，Bower 後悔的是「telling people about it」。S3：「he wishes he had kept quiet」。
18. **太太起疑的里程數。**【單源 S16，次級】
    S16 轉錄 Bower 口述：太太說「this car needs servicing quite a lot … with the enormous amount of miles you're doing」，他拿出剪報簿，再帶她去看一次才信；「it was seven years before my wife really found out」。太太名 Ilene 由 S6 證實（1948 年結婚，2017 年 11 月過世）。
    「懷疑外遇」「請太太自己設計一個圈」只見於通俗網站，【未證實】，建議只講里程。這是對太太坦白，早於 1991 年對報社公開。

## 五、氣象學假說與搞笑諾貝爾

19. **Terence Meaden 主張圈是旋風／「電漿渦旋」造成。**【兩源】
    S1：「whirling columns of air pick up electrically charged matter, flatten the crops below」。S4：他提出「an electromagneto-hydrodynamic "plasma vortex"」。
20. **兩人為了反駁氣象說而把圖形做得更複雜；直線圖形出現後，Meaden 承認那些是人做的，但仍主張單純圓圈可能是大氣現象。**【單源 S4】
    S4：「the pair increased the number of circles in their formations, hoping to demonstrate that they were not weather-related」；「even Meaden conceded that these straight-line designs … were man-made, although he stressed that simple circles could still be a consequence of atmospheric phenomena」。S4 也記 Stephen Hawking 1991 年說：「crop circles are either hoaxes or formed by vortex movement of air」。Meaden 現況來源未載。
21. **1992 年搞笑諾貝爾物理學獎。**【單源，主辦方名單】
    S7：「PHYSICS: David Chorley and Doug Bower, lions of low-energy physics, for their circular contributions to field theory based on the geometrical destruction of English crops.」

## 六、圈為什麼沒有停

22. **自白之後反而出現第二波創作者。**【兩源】S4：「the pictographs they created inspired a second wave of crop artists」「hundreds of sophisticated pictographs now appearing annually around the globe」。S3：「each year teams of anonymous circle-makers」。
23. **1991 年自白前就已有別人在做。**【兩源】S6：「By summer 1991, others had joined in … way beyond Doug and Dave's capabilities」。S13：Rod Dickinson 說他的第一個圈做於 1991 年夏天。S1 也寫兩人的作品「apparently inspired copycats」。
24. **藝術團體接商業委託。**【兩源】
    S13：Circlemakers（Rod Dickinson、John Lundberg）客戶包括「Weetabix, O2, Big Brother, Mitsubishi, and Thompson Holidays」；Daily Mail 委託的一個圈「paid the farmer £6,000 for the equivalent of around £100 worth of crops」。S3 圖說：麥田圈「are used extensively in advertising」。
25. **圖形越來越複雜。**【單源 S4】「featuring up to 2000 individual shapes」；1998 年 BBC 拍攝時，該團隊做出「a 100-circle roulette pattern」，速度約「one every minute」。
26. **1996 年巨石陣旁的 Julia Set：45 分鐘之說 vs. 製作者圈內的說法。**【分歧，雙方皆為單方陳述】
    - 傳說：S14「a doctor in a private plane flew over the field at about 5:30 pm that day and again about 45 minutes later. On his first pass … the formation was not there」。日期 1996-07-07。
    - 反駁：S14 Dickinson：「It was made the previous night, by three people, in about two and three-quarters hours, starting around 2:45 am … When that doctor flew over, he just didn't see it the first time.」他沒有說自己是製作者；訪問未錄音。
    - 圓圈數（常見說法 151）未在可靠來源確認。【未證實】S4 另記：同月 Windmill Hill 的 Triple Julia 有 194 個圓、全長 115 公尺；較早的單一 Julia 圖形「took a team of 11 surveyors five hours just to measure out」。
27. **Bower 的口頭禪。**【單源 S19，圈內人轉述】「It's only flattened corn.」

## 七、科學主張與反駁

28. **W. C. Levengood 主張圈內麥稈的節（pulvini）被拉長，推測是電磁輻射加熱。**【兩源：S4、S15】
    S4：「found similar results on 95% of 250 crop formations in seven countries, proposed that the elongated pulvini were a result of superheating from electromagnetic radiation」；論文刊於 *Physiologia Plantarum*（1994, 92:356；1999, 105:615），Haselhoff 的評論在同刊 2001 年。
29. **反駁：義大利 CICAP 三位作者 2005 年發表論文，指其統計與取樣有問題。**【單源 S15，為作者方新聞稿】
    S15：指出「the misleading application of statistical procedures, the arbitrary discarding of unwanted results」，結論是證據只顯示「a mere difference in the stem elongation between the flattened plants lying inside the circles and those standing outside them, as one should expect when whatever kind of mechanical force flattens the plants」。原期刊編輯的退稿信稱該題目「more suited to the popular press than to a scientific forum」。CICAP 是懷疑論團體，請寫成「批評者指出」。
30. **物理學者 Richard Taylor 的評語。**【單源 S4】「neither Levengood nor Haselhoff's work has ever been reconfirmed or disproved by subsequent studies」；他以奧坎剃刀支持人為說，並推測有人可能用微波爐磁控管。**後者是推測，不可當成事實。**

## 八、觀光、農民、法律

31. **觀光。**【單源 S13】Avebury 遊客中心職員：「Around 85 to 90 per cent of our custom during the summer months is connected to crop circles」（2004）。同文轉述某農民四週收入約 £30,000，原文為「is said to have」，屬傳聞，不要用。
32. **捐款箱。**【兩源】S11（2010，Old Sarum 附近）：「an honesty box asking visitors for a £5 donation to enter the circle」。S10（2017，Swindon 附近）：「put a bucket in the field, asking for donations to help offset some of the crop loss」；該圈「about 60m wide and covers about two acres of quality milling wheat」。
33. **損失數字。**【單源 S12，報社自行估算】「Farmers lost £30,000 in income between 2018 and 2022 as a result of 92 crop circles」「More than 40 football pitches' worth of arable land」；個案 George Hosford（North Dorset，2021-07-18，直徑 70 公尺）「lost about £1,000 in income」。
34. **法律：屬刑事毀損（criminal damage）。**【兩源】S10 Wiltshire 警方鄉村犯罪警員 Marc Jackson：「reiterated that creating a crop circle is criminal damage and an offence」。S12：2021–2022 年 Wiltshire 15 個圈，警方「received only three reports of criminal damage」（資訊公開資料）。
35. **2000 年的定罪。**【兩源】
    S8（2000-11-04）：Matthew Williams，29 歲，Bishops Cannings 人，「believed to be the first person in Britain to be accused」；因匿名照片被捕。全國農民聯盟（NFU）郡主席 Tim Carson：「It's no different if someone comes into your garden and causes damage.」
    S9（2000-11-07，轉述 The Times）：「fined 100 and ordered to pay 40 costs by Devizes magistrates after pleading guilty to causing criminal damage」（原頁英鎊符號遺失，應為 £100 與 £40）；圖形為 West Overton 的「seven-point star」，「after experts said it could not be done」。
    小分歧：S8 寫「pasture near Marlborough」，S9 寫「cornfield at West Overton」（West Overton 在 Marlborough 旁，地點相容，作物描述不同）。「至今唯一被定罪者」之說未在主要媒體確認。【未證實】Williams 現況來源未載。

## 九、兩人的結局

36. **Bower：2018-07-21 過世，94 歲。**【兩源】S6：「born Horton Heath, Hampshire, 25 June 1924; died West End, Hampshire, 21 July 2018, aged 94」（同篇前段另寫「a month into his 94th year」，與 aged 94 字面不合，以生卒日為準）。S19 同。
37. **Chorley：約 1996 年過世。**【未證實】查不到主要報紙訃聞；多個次級來源寫 1996，一個通俗網站寫 1997。建議旁白只說「Chorley 在 1990 年代過世」。
38. **Bower 晚年後悔公開。**【兩源】S3、S6（見第 17 條）。
