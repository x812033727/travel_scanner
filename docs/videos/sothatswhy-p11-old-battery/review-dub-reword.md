# P11 old battery: review of the flagged dub lines

Lengths are counted in characters (NFC). Every rewrite is the same length as the current text or shorter. The safety line was not flagged in any locale and is unchanged.

## ko (2 REWORD, 3 KEEP)
- p007 KEEP: 온종일 갔대요! (heard 같대요?). 갔/같 sound the same ([갇]), so this is a homophone slip.
- p017 KEEP: 뜻밖의 이유요? 뭔가 자라요? (heard 쪽 뻗게…잘하여). The recognizer garbled 뜻밖의 [뜯빠께], and 자라요/잘하여 sound alike. The line echoes p015's 뜻밖의 이유, so the wording stays.
- p061 REWORD: 물때가 전기 담는 작은 것들을 가둬. → 물때가 전기 담는 알갱이를 가둬. The recognizer heard almost nothing right (Jev 0.02). The vague 작은 것들 becomes the concrete 알갱이, which is easier to hear and shorter.
- p161 REWORD: 그럼 엄마 옛 폰은요, → 엄마 옛날 폰은요,. A bare 옛 is short and unnatural and was heard as 내 폰. A child would say 옛날 폰. The 그럼 is dropped to stay within length.
- p169 KEEP: 폰은 대체 왜 그랬어요? (heard 푸는). 폰은 [포는] was misheard (Jev 0.25, mild).

## es (0 REWORD, 3 KEEP)
- p002 KEEP: Fotografié ardillas en el parque (heard Fotografiar). Only the stressed ending differs. The meaning is clear, and any natural alternative would be longer.
- p030 KEEP: cargar es volver a llenar la botella. (heard Recargar). An extra syllable at the clip edge.
- p070 KEEP: …Hoy media, (heard o y media). Hoy and "o y" are homophones.

## pt (2 REWORD, 1 KEEP)
- p024 KEEP: Cabe energia? (heard Caiu energia?). The recognizer chose the more common phrase. Cabe is an everyday word, and the line comes right after "Minha garrafa?", which gives it context.
- p129 REWORD: mas pouca, num bico estreito, não dá. → pouca água e bico estreito: não dá. The elliptical "pouca" was heard as "pô, coar". Saying "pouca água" outright is clearer, and the contrast with p128 "Muita água sai forte;" still holds.
- p172 REWORD: bico velho estreito, e o frio, → bico velho e estreito, o frio,. The stacked "velho estreito" was garbled ("Bem que vai ser"). Adding "e" makes it a natural pair of adjectives.

## id (5 REWORD, 2 KEEP)
- p034 REWORD: Persen tak bilang besar botol. → Besar botol? Persen tak tahu. The recognizer merged "Persen tak" into "Persentase", which drops the negation and flips the meaning. The new wording keeps "tak" away from "persen".
- p038 REWORD: tak kuat seharian! → cepat habis!. "tak kuat" was heard as "Aku Aqua" (Jev 0.03), which loses the negation. "cepat habis" (it runs out fast) carries the same idea without a negation that can disappear.
- p068 REWORD: kerak tumbuh sekali? → kerak ikut tumbuh?. "tumbuh sekali" can mean "grows a lot", which is ambiguous. With p067 "Tiap cas," the new text reads "each charge, the scale grows too?".
- p084 KEEP: Tak kucas, (heard Takut, cemas). A near-homophone, and the meaning is plain with p085.
- p121 REWORD: Tapi tak bohong, air masih ada, → Tapi ia jujur, air masih ada,. The negation was dropped ("Tapi bohong"), which would mean the reverse. "ia jujur" (it is honest) is a positive form that cannot flip.
- p122 REWORD: cuma mulut sempit, tak deras. → mulut sempit, susah keluar.. It was heard as "tapi deras", the opposite meaning. "susah keluar" (hard to get out) needs no negation. "mulut sempit" stays, the term used elsewhere.
- p159 KEEP: Panas percepat kerak, ingat? (heard gerak). Kerak/gerak is a near-homophone, and kerak is the term the whole episode uses.

## vi (2 REWORD, 2 KEEP)
- p057 KEEP: Pin già đi, (heard Pizza đi). The short clip ran the words together, and the wording is natural.
- p068 KEEP: là mọc thêm cặn ạ? (heard cạnh). A tone slip, and cặn is the episode's term.
- p143 REWORD: Nên nhiều máy học thói quen cháu: → Nên nhiều máy học cách cháu sạc:. It was heard as "thói quen xấu" (bad habits), and "thói quen cháu" without "của" is awkward. "học cách cháu sạc" (learns how you charge) is clear.
- p163 REWORD: Nhớ cặn không mất chứ? → Nhớ là cặn không tan?. The old phrasing is awkward, and cặn was heard as cạn ("dried up"), which is easy to confuse in a story about water. The new line reads "remember the scale doesn't dissolve?" and leads into p164.

## th (2 REWORD, 2 KEEP)
- p054 KEEP: ในแบตมีตะกรันด้วย? (heard ในแบดมินตันด้วย). The recognizer ran the syllables together. The words are the series' standard ones.
- p084 KEEP: ถ้าไม่ชาร์จ (heard ถ้าไม่ใช่). A final-consonant slip on a common word, and p085 completes the meaning.
- p143 REWORD: หลายเครื่องเรียนนิสัยหนู → หลายเครื่องจำนิสัยหนู. "เครื่องเรียน" was heard as "เครื่องเรือน" (furniture), and "เรียนนิสัย" is unnatural. "จำนิสัยหนู" (remembers your habit) is natural and shorter, and it matches the later "จะจำ…" idea.
- p172 REWORD: ปากกระติกเก่าแคบ อากาศก็หนาว → กระติกเก่าปากแคบ อากาศก็หนาว. "ปากกระติก" was heard as "ปกติ". The new word order (old bottle, narrow mouth) uses the episode's "ปากแคบ", is the same length, and avoids the merge.
