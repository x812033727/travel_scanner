#### 1. a01-s001

無台詞動作（1 秒）：Shen's study in the alliance mansion at night: a pale slender hand resting on the long dark wooden writing desk with brushes, an inkstone and one candle, a writing brush tipping sideways out of its loosening fingers over a sheet covered in columns of illegible brush marks, the very wide white silk sleeve embroidered with silver cranes spilling over the desk edge, warm candlelight from the right, cold blue moonlight from the left
