## 劇情

Rosa 往前一站，湯勺舉著，勺裡是一勺冷掉的巧達湯：「Lyle Becker. Your mother still owes me for chowder.」湯勺一甩，冷湯潑上他的鏡頭、落在他的鞋上。「My lens!」另外兩支相機一支支放下，記者散了。
門內的 Sparks 湊到 Nora 耳邊：「That's Rosa. Corner shack.」Rosa 走到門縫前，看著 Nora，看了一拍太久，什麼都沒說，只把湯勺換到左手：「Come. Chowder's hot.」右手搭著 Nora 的左手肘，把她帶過四十步，帶進街角的小館。
碗「咚」一聲放在她面前：「Eat.」蒸氣裡，她蒼白的臉回了點血色。Rosa 在爐前回頭看她，攪湯的手停住，看得太久；Nora 低頭吃，沒發現。
Sparks 端著碗站在門口唏哩呼嚕：「Extra crackers?」Rosa 不回頭：「Get 'em yourself.」Nora 嘴角輕輕一提。
吃到一半，她惦著爐子，起身：「Thank you. For out there.」Rosa 背對著她攪鍋：「You've gotten thin.」Nora 停在門口：「Have we met?」Rosa 不轉身：「Finish your bowl first.」

- **地點與場景圖**：Rosa's `rosas`（待畫，**陰的早上版**）：`a tiny corner chowder shack, vertical view: a wooden counter with four stools, two burners and a big dented steel stockpot behind it, a bare bulb hanging over the counter, a plain weathered wooden board on the wall, a shelf of bowls on the back wall, a string of small bells on the door, a window onto the street corner, warm yellow light, no readable writing`。門開著，蒸氣往街上飄；大湯鍋 `a big dented steel stockpot about as wide as her shoulders, steam rising` 在後牆偏西那段的爐上；`a small plastic basket of oyster crackers` 在 3、4 號凳之間的吧台上。牆上的木板是空白的。
