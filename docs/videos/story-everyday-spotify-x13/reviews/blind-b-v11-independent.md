## Shorts watched as standalone scripts

**Short 1 — Yes, self-contained.** The hook at shot `x13-b04-s01`, “二〇二三年第二季，Spotify 沖銷三千萬歐元的部分播客內容資產”, immediately fixes the date and the *partial* scope. The closure at `x13-b04-s05` gives a reason to ask at `x13-b04-s06`, “這代表 Spotify 不做播客了嗎？” It then gives two distinct answers: July 2023 availability at `x13-b04-s08` (“平台有逾十萬檔可用影片播客”) and the more decisive 2025 new action at `x13-b06-s01`/`x13-b06-s03` (“合格影片播客創作者，可依觀看表現拿報酬”). The final line at `x13-b06-s19`, “關閉部分節目是重整；新方案仍在做播客，並非全面退出”, resolves the exact question. The spoken “另一批影片供給” and description avoid claiming the closed shows became those videos. It would be tighter if the 2025 program followed the closure sooner; the 2023 inventory count is context, not proof of renewed investment.

**Short 2 — Yes, self-contained, with a small narration fix recommended.** Its hook at shot `x13-b06-s11` shows `−€21m`, `2025 Q2 同比｜Ad-Supported`, and `非全平台播客總支出` to establish the measure. “這是真省，還是轉記會員帳？” at `x13-b06-s07` is answered by “為這種新觀看體驗花的相關播客成本，開始記在會員帳” at `x13-b06-s12`, then “其餘仍留廣告帳；報表沒拆出轉記和真正節省各多少” at `x13-b06-s14`. The payoff at `x13-b06-s15` says “不能當成整個播客業務省下同額.” The opening *spoken* line says “少兩千一百萬” without “歐元”; adding that unit would make the audio alone clear. The Short otherwise states the denominator and unknown split without needing the long video.
