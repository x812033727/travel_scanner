## Visual plans read after the blind judgment

`visual-plan.md` makes the intended folder and separate evidence cards explicit; this prevents a viewer from mistaking a 2023 write-off, later video supply and 2024 company profit for one program’s life cycle. Its opening row nevertheless proposes showing the write-off, >100,000 available video podcasts and annual profit as separate number cards. That reinforces the script’s early disclosure at `x130106`–`x130108`, while the later payoff shots at `x130403`, `x130416` and `x130522` have less to reveal. The final split between company profit and 2025 cost reclassification is visually appropriate, and the unstamped return folder supports `x130624`.

`shorts-portrait-plan.md` specifies a 1920×1080 source image’s central x=656–1264 window, native 1080×1920 number cards, distinct headline and subtitle areas, and a 360px phone check. Those are sensible production instructions, not proof of finished 9:16 shots. The 608px central window is only about 32% of the source width: Short 1’s two separate cards at `x13-b01-s03`, studio team at `x13-b02-s05`, and two-card answer at `x13-b02-s06` may lose one half of the comparison when cropped. Short 2’s `x13-b06-s08`→`x13-b06-s09` cost transfer needs both ledgers intelligible across successive shots; the native `x13-b06-s11` number card avoids cropped type. Review generated frames at actual phone size and measure finished TTS duration before marking either Short production-ready. No generated frames, animation, audio, or export were available in the two plans, which expressly describe themselves as preproduction.
