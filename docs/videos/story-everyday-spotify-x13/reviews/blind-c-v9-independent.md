## What a new listener hears

The opening meets the formal scene and teaser checks, but the teaser stack is hard to retain by ear. `[x130101]` and `[x130103]` name the two acquisitions, and `[x130104]` supplies a tangible €308m stake. Then `[x130106]` jumps to free listening, `[x130107]` to an unspecified restructuring record, and `[x130108]` to member video costs. The single question tying all three to the purchases does not land until `[x130109]`. The anonymous researcher and blank cards supply continuity in the illustrations, but “查帳者／卡／帳” becomes an abstraction when heard without those pictures. A novice gets the most usable description of the two purchases from Short 1; the long film does not make the two different capabilities into an equally vivid opening premise.

Chapter 2's actual monetization answer is clear: `[x130205]` requires a sold ad impression, `[x130207]` locates revenue in the Ad-Supported line with music ads, and `[x130208]` distinguishes Premium fees. The path to it is slow: `[x130201]` starts with play, `[x130202]` backs up to creation, and `[x130203]`–`[x130204]` reassemble anonymous cards already pictured in the opening. `[x130214]` finally clarifies that purchase consideration included share-based compensation, but it interrupts the ongoing revenue/cost sequence. `[x130209]` states the missing per-deal revenue line early; later returns to that absence should add evidence rather than repeat it.

Chapters 3 and 4 are the most satisfying evidence turns. `[x130312]` gives the +€11m to −€9m gross-margin reversal, `[x130315]`–`[x130320]` separates +€22m revenue, +€42m total costs, +€12m podcast-content costs, and +€20m royalties. Chapter 4's €30m content-asset write-off `[x130403]` and inability to assign it to Anchor or Gimlet `[x130409]`–`[x130412]` are a real new development, with a defensible limit on what it proves.
