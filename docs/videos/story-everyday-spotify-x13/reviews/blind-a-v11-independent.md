## Standalone Shorts

**Short 1 — 7/10, stands alone.** Its own first two spoken beats establish the 2023 partial €30m content write-down and program closures; `這代表 Spotify 不做播客了嗎？` is a clear question. The July >100,000 available video podcasts and the 2025 eligible-creator pay program answer full exit without claiming the closed shows returned. The visual shots place closed audio slots and open video tiles separately. The Premium no-dynamic-ad line is a second product feature that slows a short whose question has already been answered; cutting that beat would make the last line land sooner. Retain the July timing and the warning that available videos are a different set of content.

**Short 2 — 7/10, stands alone with one spoken fix.** It starts with an Ad-Supported cost drop, asks `這是真省，還是轉記會員帳？`, shows why the new Premium video experience changes the cost classification, and ends with the supported answer that the split is unknown. Its first narration says `二〇二五年第二季，Spotify 廣告欄播客成本同比少兩千一百萬。` without saying **歐元**; the on-screen `−€21m` supplies the unit only to viewers looking at the card. Say `兩千一百萬歐元` in that first line and, if space allows, in the payoff. The middle creator-pay and no-ad beats explain the new experience but could be one beat to keep the cost question moving.
