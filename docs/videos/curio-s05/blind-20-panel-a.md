## Thirteen audience comprehension questions

1. **Who receives the *Chushi Biao*?** Liu Chan, Liu Bei's son.
2. **What does Zhuge Liang say happened at his cottage?** Liu Bei visited him three times.
3. **Who introduces Zhuge Liang to Liu Bei in Chen Shou's account?** Xu Shu.
4. **What does Liu Bei first ask Xu Shu to do?** Bring Zhuge Liang to him.
5. **What does Xu Shu tell Liu Bei to do instead?** Go to Zhuge Liang personally rather than summon him.
6. **What does Liu Bei ask Zhuge Liang during their private talk?** What plan to follow after the Han's decline (“計將安出”).
7. **Who is unhappy about Liu Bei's growing closeness to Zhuge Liang?** Guan Yu and Zhang Fei.
8. **Which quoted work reverses the first visitor?** The *Wei Lüe*, quoted in Pei Songzhi's annotation, says Zhuge Liang went to Liu Bei first.
9. **Where is Liu Bei stationed in the *Wei Lüe* account?** Fancheng.
10. **What is Liu Bei doing when Zhuge Liang starts the pointed conversation?** Weaving an ornament from a newly delivered yak tail.
11. **What advice leads Liu Bei to treat Zhuge Liang as an honored guest?** Zhuge Liang suggests asking Liu Biao to let unregistered wandering households report themselves, increasing available manpower; the account says Liu Bei adopts the idea and his forces strengthen.
12. **Why can the script not establish an order that fits both meetings?** The *Wei Lüe* calls them strangers before its meeting, while Chen's account has Xu Shu introduce Zhuge Liang before the cottage visit, and neither passage gives dates fixing their order.
13. **What evidence does Pei use to decide the direction, and can the script name the reverse story's original teller?** He cites Zhuge Liang's *Chushi Biao*, especially “三顧臣於草廬之中,” to conclude Liu Bei went to Zhuge Liang; the quoted *Wei Lüe* passage does not name its original teller.
