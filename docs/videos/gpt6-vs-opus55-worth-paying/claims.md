## 我懷疑但沒動的事

- Resolved in round 1: the OpenAI table's $12.50 column is headed "Cache writes" (page `<thead>` and tooltip). The writer's additive reading (input price plus write price on the same tokens) contradicted the tooltip, so the Astra, Sol and Opus 5.5 job-2 rows were redone.
- OpenAI says prompt caching is "enabled by default" and, for GPT-5.6 and later, implicit caching puts a breakpoint at the end of the latest eligible message (minimum 1,024 tokens) and bills writes at 1.25x. A one-off prompt such as job 1's daily PDF or job 3's chats could therefore be billed at the write rate ($12.75 instead of $10.50 for job 1 on Astra; up to $525 instead of $450 for job 3) unless the developer uses explicit-only mode with no breakpoints, or gains cache reads on a shared prefix. Jobs 1 and 3 stay at the plain input price as list-price arithmetic; the video does not say this.
- Whether Grok 4.7's agent conversation in job 2 would cross the 200k long-context threshold; the under-200k rates are used.
- Gemini's per-hour cache storage price is listed but not counted (c5): implicit caching avoids it, explicit caches do not.
- xAI's docs are titled "SpaceXAI Docs" today; the pricing page itself still says "the public xAI API", so "xAI's Grok 4.7" stands.
- Fast-mode prices (OpenAI 2x, Anthropic $8 / $40) are deliberately left out of the arithmetic.
