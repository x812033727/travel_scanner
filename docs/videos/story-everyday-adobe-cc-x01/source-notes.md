## 可直接支撐的敘事事實

下列「公司說」只證明 Adobe 當時的主張或預期，不能改寫成獨立證實的消費者偏好。引號是精確片段；每列的連結指向同一份原始申報，章節標明定位。

1. **訂閱先於 Creative Cloud。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media Strategy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`In the second quarter of fiscal 2011, we released CS5.5. At that time we also introduced a monthly subscription offering.` 因而不能把 2012 Creative Cloud 說成 Adobe 首次推出月費。
2. **Adobe 當時把低首付作為訂閱賣點。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media Strategy](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`This lower upfront fee, as opposed to the higher upfront perpetual license fee`。這是公司解釋的定價差異，未證明所有使用者更喜歡月費。
3. **Creative Cloud 先於上市推出被宣布。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`we announced Creative Cloud in October 2011`。
4. **正式推出在 2012 年 5 月，與 CS6 同期。** [S12，Item 7／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`In May 2012, we launched Adobe Creative Suite 6`；同一段稱 Creative Cloud `was also released in May 2012`。
5. **CS6 一開始同時有買斷與訂閱取得方式。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`Customers obtained CS6 capabilities through both our historical perpetual licensing model as well as through our new subscription-based model.`
6. **「雲端」沒有使桌面程式消失。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：Creative Cloud `lets our customers download and install the latest version of any of our Adobe Creative Suite desktop products`。
7. **會員另有線上功能。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`Creative Cloud members also get online services to sync, store, and share files`。可畫「桌面安裝」與「檔案同步」兩條服務，而非只畫瀏覽器。
8. **2012 財年 Adobe 仍主要靠永久授權。** [S12，Item 7／Financial Performance Summary for Fiscal 2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`We continue to derive the majority of our revenue from perpetual licenses.`
9. **公司整體訂閱收入占比逐年上升。** [S12，Item 7／Financial Performance Summary for Fiscal 2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`has increased to 15% in fiscal 2012 from approximately 11% and 10% in fiscal 2011 and fiscal 2010`。這是 Adobe 全公司，不是 Creative Cloud 單品。
10. **2012 公司營收沒有在推出年立即下滑。** [S12，Item 7／Financial Performance Summary for Fiscal 2012](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`Our total revenue of $4.4 billion increased $187.4 million and $603.7 million, or 4% and 11%`，分別比 2011、2010 財年。不能把 2013 年的下滑提前到 2012 年。
11. **訂閱收入不只 Creative Cloud。** [S12，Item 7／Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：收入項目包括 `our digital marketing services and Creative Cloud`。圖卡不可把全公司訂閱數字貼成 Photoshop 或 Creative Cloud 收入。
12. **訂閱分期認列，與舊式授權認列時點不同。** [S12，Item 7／Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`We recognize subscription revenue ratably over the term of agreements`；同申報的 Digital Media Business Summary 比較 `subscription offerings ratably whereas revenue associated with our perpetual licenses is generally recognized at the time of initially licensing the products.`
13. **Adobe 在 2012 年就警示轉換會壓低當期報表數字。** [S12，Item 1／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`the success of Creative Cloud subscription adoption adversely affected reported revenue`，其後緊接第 12 點的認列時點解釋。這是管理層對報表效果的描述，不表示現金都消失。
14. **推出未滿一年時，續訂資料尚不足。** [S12，Item 7／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634313000008/adbe10kfy12.htm)：`we anticipate that meaningful data regarding subscription renewal rates will first become available later in fiscal year 2013.` 不可宣稱 2012 年報證明長期續訂率。
15. **2013 年 5 月的關鍵決策是後續新功能只給 Creative Cloud。** [S13，Item 1／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`In May 2013 we announced we would exclusively deliver new creative product innovations and features to Creative Cloud subscribers`；同句說 CS6 `would be the last major update we provide for perpetual licensees.`
16. **買斷 CS6 當時並未立即停售。** [S13，Item 1／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`While we continue to offer CS6 on a perpetual licensing basis moving forward`。不能說 2013 年起「所有買斷軟體隔夜消失」。
17. **同時可買 Photoshop CC 訂閱與 Photoshop CS6 永久授權。** [S13，Item 1／Photoshop](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`Customers can also subscribe to Photoshop CC as an individual subscription product, or acquire a perpetual license of Photoshop CS6.`
18. **Creative Cloud 仍安裝 Photoshop 等桌面程式。** [S13，Item 1／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：服務 `allows members to download and install the latest versions of our creative products such as Adobe Photoshop`。
19. **Adobe 當時列出的會員價值還有更新、同步和社群。** [S13，Item 1／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`more frequent product updates, storage and access to user files stored in the cloud with syncing of files across users' machines, community-based features and services through our acquisition of Behance in December 2012`。這是產品說明，不能倒推使用者喜好。
20. **2013 財年訂閱占比升至 28%，但買斷仍占重要部分。** [S13，Item 7／Financial Performance Summary for Fiscal 2013](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`We continue to derive the majority of our revenue from perpetual licenses`；訂閱占比 `increased to 28% from 15% and 11%`，分別比 2012、2011 財年。
21. **付費 Creative Cloud 訂閱數在 2013 財年末上升。** [S13，Item 7／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`We exited fiscal 2013 with 1.4 million paid Creative Cloud subscriptions, up from 0.3 million at the end of fiscal 2012.` 是期末存量，並非全年新增訂閱。
22. **Creative ARR 上升，與會計營收不能混算。** [S13，Item 7／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`Total Creative ARR exiting fiscal 2013 was $768.0 million, up from $155.0 million exiting fiscal 2012`；同段註明 `ARR should be viewed independently of revenue, deferred revenue and unbilled deferred revenue`。
23. **數位媒體部門 2013 年收入下降 4.76 億美元。** [S13，Item 7／Fiscal 2013 Revenue Compared to Fiscal 2012 Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`Revenue from Digital Media decreased $476.0 million during fiscal year 2013 as compared to fiscal 2012, primarily due to continued strong adoption of Creative Cloud and ETLAs`。不能把這整筆下降只歸於 Creative Cloud。
24. **全公司 2013 財年收入也下降約 8%。** [S13，Item 7／Revenue by Reportable Segment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：表列 `Total revenue $4,055.2 $4,403.7 $4,216.3 (8)% 4 %`，欄序是 2013、2012、2011 財年。約 40.55 億對 44.04 億美元，差約 3.48 億美元；不能宣稱全因訂閱。
25. **全公司淨利 2013 財年下降，年報自己列出兩類原因。** [S13，Item 7／Financial Performance Summary for Fiscal 2013](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634314000004/adbe10kfy13.htm)：`Net income of $290.0 million decreased by $542.8 million, or 65%, during fiscal 2013 from $832.8 million in fiscal 2012.` 接著說 `The decrease is primarily due to the revenue model becoming more ratable as well as the cost and expense increases stated above.` 不可用「客戶大量流失」代替申報中的解釋。
26. **2013–2015 年公司收入結構實際翻轉。** [S15，Item 7／Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634316000224/adbe10kfy15.htm)：2015／2014／2013 財年 `Subscription $3,223.9 $2,076.6 $1,137.9` 百萬美元、`Percentage of total revenue 67% 50% 28%`；同表 `Product 1,125.1 1,627.8 2,470.1` 百萬美元、產品占比 `24% 39% 61%`。產品收入不等於所有買斷收入，分類也不等於單一 Creative Cloud 產品。
27. **2015 財年公司總營收高於 2013、2014。** [S15，Item 7／Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634316000224/adbe10kfy15.htm)：`Total revenue $4,795.5 $4,147.1 $4,055.2` 百萬美元，欄序是 2015／2014／2013 財年。可作中期收束，但不能單獨證明訂閱轉型是唯一原因。
28. **付費 Creative Cloud 訂閱數到 2015 財年末為 616.7 萬。** [S15，Item 7／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634316000224/adbe10kfy15.htm)：`We exited fiscal 2015 with 6.167 million paid Creative Cloud subscriptions, up 78% from 3.458 million at the end of fiscal 2014.`
29. **2015 年數位媒體收入回升，Adobe 歸因包含 Creative Cloud 採用。** [S15，Item 7／Fiscal 2015 Revenue Compared to Fiscal 2014 Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634316000224/adbe10kfy15.htm)：`Revenue from Digital Media increased $492.0 million during fiscal 2015 as compared to fiscal 2014, primarily driven by increases in revenue associated with our creative offerings due to continued strong adoption of Creative Cloud.`
30. **2015 年仍有少量舊創意產品永久授權收入。** [S15，Item 1／Digital Media Business Summary](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634316000224/adbe10kfy15.htm)：`revenue from perpetual licensing of these products was immaterial for fiscal 2015.` `these products` 指舊版 Creative 產品，不能擴寫成 Adobe 全公司不再有永久授權。
31. **2025 財年 Adobe 全公司 96% 收入屬訂閱，但不是 Creative Cloud 單品占比。** [S25，Item 7／Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)：表列 `Subscription$22,904 $20,521 $18,284` 百萬美元與 `Percentage of total revenue96 %%95 %%94 %%`（2025／2024／2023 財年），並列 `Product325 386 460`、`Total revenue$23,769 $21,505 $19,409`。2025 公司總營收 237.69 億美元，其中訂閱 229.04 億美元；HTML 空白正規化後百分比欄保留原 XBRL 的雙 `%`。
32. **2025 訂閱收入跨多個部門。** [S25，Item 7／Subscription Revenue by Reportable Segment](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)：2025 財年表列 `Digital Media$17,389 $15,547 $13,838`、`Digital Experience5,409 4,864 4,331`（欄序 2025／2024／2023；單位百萬美元），另有 Publishing and Advertising。即便數位媒體也含 Acrobat 等產品，不能把 96% 說成 Photoshop 收入。
33. **2025 的產品收入分類不只買斷。** [S25，Item 7／Product](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000003/adbe-20251128.htm)：`Our product revenue is comprised primarily of fees related to licenses for on-premise software purchased on a perpetual basis, for a fixed period of time, or based on usage`。不可把 Product 欄全部改稱「盒裝軟體」。
34. **2026 最近季度只能按中期資料說。** [S26Q3，Item 2／Revenue](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634326000156/adbe-20260828.htm)：`Subscription$6,582 $5,791 $19,196 $16,915`、`Total revenue6,760 5,988 19,776 17,575`，欄序為截至 2026／2025 年 8 月底的三個月與九個月；同表九個月訂閱占比 `97 %`。不可把 2026 九個月當全年，也不可說是 Creative Cloud 單品收入。
35. **CS6 永久授權產品的一般供應在 2014 財年第二季停止。** [S15，Item 7／Fiscal 2014 Revenue Compared to Fiscal 2013 Revenue／Digital Media](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/796343/000079634316000224/adbe10kfy15.htm)：`we discontinued the general availability of our perpetually licensed CS6 products in the second quarter of fiscal 2014.` 這是 **CS6 產品的「一般供應」**，不能寫成 2013 年 5 月立刻停售，或 Adobe 所有永久授權產品自此停賣；同一份年報在 Item 7／Fiscal 2015 Revenue Compared to Fiscal 2014 Revenue／Digital Media 仍提及 `our Document Cloud perpetual license offerings.`

## 可寫成六章的因果主線

1. **濃縮開場／鉤子：訂閱人數升，財報卻降。** 以 2013 財年末 Creative Cloud 付費訂閱數從 30 萬升至 140 萬（第 21 點）、全公司營收跌約 8%（第 24 點）及淨利跌 65%（第 25 點）開場。最後停在「人變多了，帳上為什麼一度變少？」不能說公司快破產。
2. **起點：買斷主導的創意軟體。** 2011 年先試月費（第 1–2 點），2012 年公司收入仍主要來自永久授權（第 8–10 點）。章尾問：「如果已經賣得動，為何要換收錢的方法？」
3. **點子：Creative Cloud 並非把 Photoshop 變成網頁。** 2012 年 CS6 與 Creative Cloud 一起推出、兩種授權並存（第 3–7 點）；下載安裝與同步服務分開描繪。章尾問：「同一個工具、不同付法，哪裡先改變？」
4. **生意引擎：同樣成交，認列時點不同。** 用 2012 年報對分期認列與永久授權初始認列的描述（第 11–14 點），說清全公司訂閱收入同時有行銷業務；ARR 不是營收（第 22 點）。可用無商標的帳本、月曆與年度卡示意，圖上數字須後製字卡，示例金額需明標為假設，不作史實。
5. **轉折：Adobe 主動收窄買斷的未來。** 2013 年 5 月公告未來新功能只給訂閱、但 CS6 買斷仍售（第 15–19 點）；CS6 永久授權的一般供應到 2014 財年第二季才停止（第 35 點）。接著看實際 2013 數據：訂閱占比與人數升、數位媒體和全公司營收下滑（第 20–25 點）；淨利下降另有成本上升，不把所有變化歸因一個產品。章尾問：「這是在失去生意，還是在改變計數方式？」
6. **現在：分期收入變成主流，範圍仍須講清。** 2013–2015 全公司訂閱收入占比 28%→50%→67%，2015 年付費 Creative Cloud 訂閱 616.7 萬（第 26–29 點）；2025 財年全公司訂閱占比 96%（第 31–33 點），2026 九個月約 97% 只作中期註腳（第 34 點）。結尾回答開場：人數增與當期財報跌可同時發生，因收入改為逐期認列且成本也增加。留言問題可問「你看重一次買斷，還是持續取得更新？」；訂閱邀請接在這個選擇上，不預告下一支影片。

## 寫稿前仍需補的查核

- 如要講創作者反彈、失去檔案、實際個人價格、產品使用授權細節、轉換率、流失率或客戶滿意度，必須另找當時可歸屬的官方文件及獨立資料；五份申報不能支撐這些說法，缺資料就刪去。也不能用股價或市值倒推轉型成功。
- 2012 年報「低首付吸引使用者」是 Adobe 的管理層判斷，旁白需加「Adobe 當時認為」。2013 年年報對收入、淨利下滑的歸因亦應以「Adobe 在年報解釋」表述；不可將 2013 財報下跌全歸於 Creative Cloud。
- 所有 2013–2025 百分比都屬 **Adobe 全公司** 的收入分類，包含 Digital Marketing／Experience、Document Cloud 等。付費 Creative Cloud 訂閱數是期末存量；ARR 是年化績效指標；Product 不全等於永久授權；2026 九個月不等於全年。
- 圖像依品牌故事與奇聞檔案局定案：MiniMax `image-01`、`look: {"preset":"cute-mystery"}`、匿名人物及無商標無可讀字物件。這份查核只證明敘事可做，尚未替 75–120 張圖、台灣口音旁白、配樂、字幕與 5 秒／21 秒品牌動畫取得正式素材或過關。
