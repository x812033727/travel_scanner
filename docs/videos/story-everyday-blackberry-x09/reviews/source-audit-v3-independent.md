# X09 BlackBerry — independent primary-source audit, v3

**Decision: FAIL for release pending two wording fixes.** The amounts, contract mechanics, and central distinction between BlackBerry's own Handheld Devices revenue and its partners' branded-phone activity pass. One long-film sentence makes the FY2017–FY2018 comparison sound like a clean before/after experiment, and Short 2 leaves out a necessary qualification when answering what made up the US$64 million category. This is a source verdict, not a script change.
