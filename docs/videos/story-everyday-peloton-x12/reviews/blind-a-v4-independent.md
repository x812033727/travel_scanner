**Blind ballot: 8/12 required yes; four required no.** The six chapters keep the planned Ohio factory in view and carry a real reversal from proposed capacity to a search for a buyer. The delivery weakens where the opening turns into five consecutive topic questions, the accounting qualifications repeat, and the ending raises a recovery question after admitting that no recovery figure is known. This ballot does **not** pass all required questions. The production gate combines two independent readers; this is one reader's verdict only.

The most useful story repair is to keep the opening's concrete 2021 announcement and 2022 contradiction, then replace the five-question run with three distinct later moments that stop before their results. In the fifth chapter, the sale-status answer at `x120522` would land more cleanly beside its question at `x120517`; the two repeated money caveats can be compressed. The ending should state the known FY2023 limit once and avoid asking how much will be recovered unless the script can provide a later answer. The subscription-cost aside at `x120416` can be shortened or removed without losing the hardware-to-Ohio connection.
