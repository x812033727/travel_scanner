cut (`564812bd…`, intro after chapter 1 at body frame 682, 21 s outro) with en, ja, ko and vi; es and

## Timeline, rebuilt from the delivered captions

`dub` needs the zh-TW timeline, which only Codex's work directory held. The delivered zh-TW captions
of `564812bd` are cut from that timeline, so `receipts/claude-finish-20261026/rebuild-timeline.mjs`
reads them back: each of the 92 lines takes its cues in order (all 97 cue texts match the script),
its start from its first cue, its length from the gap to the next line's start on the frame grid,
and `buildTimeline` lays out the rest. The result was accepted only because all three checks hold:

| Check | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Every line starts on its cue | 92 of 92, 0 off |
| Total frames | 15,383, the approved narration's length |
| `speech_hash` | `c6d70ecaef91a15d`, the value Codex recorded |

The per-line audio lengths it infers are rounded up to the frame, so zh-TW cue ends come out up to
about 30 ms late; the zh-TW captions delivered stay Codex's own. The four new languages' captions
follow their own dub timelines, which this does not affect. Titles and descriptions composed by the
pipeline from this timeline are byte-identical to the five Codex delivered (zh-TW, en, ja, ko, vi).

## Dubs, checks and translations

Made with `dub` against the rebuilt timeline (no music or effects in this video), checked with
`check-audio` and the Whisper second opinion (`faster-whisper`, `medium`), one language at a time.
Every line both transcribers missed in the same place was reworded or re-taken; each rewording was
reviewed by an independent reviewer for meaning, unchanged numbers, TTS-safety and length. 40 lines
changed in `i18n/{es,pt,id,th}.json` (es 9, pt 13, id 10, th 8), in three kinds:

- too long for their window even at 1.15x, mostly around numbers (decimals read slowly);
- words the TTS mangles: Thai จึง, ค่า before a pause or a number, อย่างไร said as ยังไง;
- lines heard with the opposite meaning (pt "Não se infere", id "Tidak bisa" heard as "Kita bisa",
  id "buka" heard as "bukan", es "no da igual").

Final check: th, pt and id 0 flagged; es 1 (`sbkg`), where both transcripts have the script's words
and only miss the question intonation, kept as is. Every dub is wrapped with the same bookend audio
at body frame 682 (branding hash `39d34ab0…`, the final's own), 538.805 s, -14.1 LUFS.
