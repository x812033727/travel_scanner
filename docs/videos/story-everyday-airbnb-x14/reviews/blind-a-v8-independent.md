## Story and conditional example

The opening does **not** invent a documented March 20 cancellation: `x140101` says 「想像」, `x140102` calls the order illustrative, and `x140220` labels the March 10 booking/March 30 check-in hypothetical. The script keeps the March–April **combined** cancellation comparison distinct from an assertion about either month alone (`x140103`, `x140217`–`x140218`). Its initial unpaid room price is separated from platform service-fee revenue (`x140203`–`x140210`).

The guest and host branches do not swap beneficiaries. The guest needs the pre-March-14 booking and a pandemic impediment that passes policy review (`x140301`–`x140303`); the cash-refund route is conditional and credits remain possible. Host support is separately gated by the original booking date, March 14–May 31 check-in window, and exception-policy eligibility (`x140306`–`x140310`). The hypothetical host's **40-unit original cancellation entitlement**, not the 100-unit room rate, supplies the 25% base and produces a hypothetical 10 units (`x140311`–`x140315`). `x140317`–`x140319` distinguish the company's annual US$205.1m accounting amount from the capped US$250m commitment and from proof that this model host was actually paid. `x140320`–`x140321`, `x140425`, and `x140503` deny any inference that the same host or guest later completed a booking.

The 2022 outro gives the central answer exactly once: 393.7m **net booked nights and experiences after cancellations and alterations**, `x140617`. It follows the 2020 193.2m and 2021 300.6m totals (`x140423`, `x140502`), so the number is an actual aggregate recovery measure rather than an available-listing count or GAAP profit. The `outro` has exactly three narration lines, `x140617`–`x140619`, in the prescribed answer/question/subscribe order. The comment question at `x140618` is answerable but asks a guest to choose between their refund and a host's empty calendar; a viewer-centered version would sound more natural.

The long script has 120 narration lines and about 2,617 nonpunctuation characters spread across six chapters. With normal Mandarin delivery and the scene pauses, it plausibly supplies over eight substantive minutes. This is a text estimate; no audio runtime was supplied. Questions are frequent, but repeated measurement cautions around `x140217`–`x140219`, `x140318`–`x140319`, `x140418`–`x140426`, and `x140508`–`x140515` may make the back half feel more like accounting notes than a continuous decision story. `x140426` is the clearest isolated finance tangent; `x140516` and `x140613`–`x140615` are relevant only because the final line rejects profit as the proof of completed bookings.
