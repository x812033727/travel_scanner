## Spoken-story verdict, recorded before viewing the visual plan

**Revise the long-form script before narration.** The cold open works, the two Shorts make sense on their own, and the long-form body has enough text for about nine minutes at a normal Mandarin storytelling pace. Four of the twelve required narrative gates are not yet convincing: a full-year accounting passage makes the timeline double back, one money question receives only a partial answer, the opening's imagined guest/host transaction does not remain the acting subject, and the net-loss accounting subplot pulls away from the promised booking mechanism. There is also a definite false callback to an opening “year-end table.”
