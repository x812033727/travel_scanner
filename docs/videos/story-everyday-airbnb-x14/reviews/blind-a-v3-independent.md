The six chapters mostly have event titles and a chronological shape. Chapter 3's question title is less event-shaped, but its refund rule and host support are concrete actions. The main repairs are to keep the opening's listing comparison unresolved, start chapter 2 on a consequence or new event rather than the same cancellation image, remove the repeated cross-border limitation, and turn the chapter-6 comparisons into one developing booking situation or leave only the figures needed for the payoff.

The paper length is substantive: 2,632 Han characters in all spoken lines, including 201 in the opening and 83 in the last three lines. At the brand-story planning rate of roughly 250 spoken Chinese characters per minute, that suggests about 10.5 minutes total and 9.4 minutes of body excluding opening and close. The opening is about 48 seconds and the three-sentence close about 20 seconds. Actual TTS and edit timing have not been measured.

Both Shorts stand alone on the storyboard. Short 1 asks whether the US$4.6bn loss was a cancellation bill, distinguishes reduced bookings from IPO and warrant items, and answers at its `payoff` card. Short 2 asks whether long stays alone caused first annual profit, gives 2021 and 2022 comparisons, and ends with the limited causal conclusion. Each has eight narrated cards and a beginning, turn, and answer. For 9:16, the longest headline is Short 1's scene 6 (`x14-b02-s11`,「全年非現金變動 ~8.7億美元」); it needs a deliberate two-line wrap. Short 2's scene 4 (`x14-b05-s13`,「逾九成活躍刊登可接長住」) also needs comfortable side margins. The headline lengths appear workable on a vertical card, and the referenced visual prompts keep the calendar or ledger as the focal object. Rendered safe-area and font-size legibility still require a frame check; no rendered frames were part of this blind vote.
