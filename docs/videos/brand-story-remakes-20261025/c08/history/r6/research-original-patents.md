# C08 patent/design research

Actual original granted-patent PDFs were fetched and read. The useful match is **JP5727888B2, for the later sealed 200mL tabletop-bottle lineage**. No original 1961 Ekuan patent/design filing was located; do not fill that gap with a later valve patent.

## Strongest evidence chain

1. [Kikkoman 2011 launch](https://www.kikkoman.com/jp/corporate/news/11034.html) identifies a 200mL inner-bag squeeze bottle and double-check-valve cap, with patent applied for but no number. The original release is dated 2011-07-12 and launches 2011-08-10.
2. [JPAA design pamphlet](https://www.jpaa.or.jp/cms/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/design-pamphlet.pdf), PDF/printed pp2–3, explicitly associates the named 200mL tabletop bottle with patent **5727888** and designs **1443164 / 1459666**. Page 3 connects the design discussion to the August 2011 double-wall squeeze-bottle launch. The 1961 glass bottle is a form/design predecessor in this account.
3. [JP5727888B2 original PDF](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/76/60/a8/45cd90f41229d0/JP5727888B2.pdf), 19 pages, directly establishes the granted claims. **Registration 2015-04-10; B2 publication 2015-06-03; application 2011-07-11; priority 2011-02-28.** The face page is rendered locally and visually checked. Google incorrectly collapses the registration/publication event to June 3 in its timeline.

Claim 1 is a specified combination of shrinking flexible inner container, outer container with air intake, air valve, cap/plug, axially sliding elastic liquid valve and a displacement restrictor. Page 1 says “内容物が収容されるとともに内容物の減少に伴いしぼみ変形する可撓性に富む内容器”. Description [0004]–[0010], PDF pp3–4, addresses residual liquid leakage and how valve return draws contents away from the outlet. This supports a later sealed-container mechanism, not the red cap of the 1961 glass dispenser. Later grant and educational product mapping do not prove every production version met every later claim.

The [US9238527B2 original grant](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/fd/7c/4b/e0c1daef1dae4f/US9238527.pdf), granted 2016-01-19, cites JP2011-153262 among its priorities and provides original English claim language at PDF p34, columns 25–26. Its claims differ from the Japanese grant and should not be called identical.

The already-requested [official JPO-created instructional deck hosted by INPIT](https://www.inpit.go.jp/content/100882381.pdf) also completed. PDF p6 (printed p5) shows the 200mL sealed bottle with patent 5727888 and the two design numbers. PDF p7 (printed p6) reproduces the patent J-PlatPat record, including the separate registration/publication dates. PDF p8 (printed p7) reproduces the two design records: D1443164 filed 2011-06-08 / registered 2012-05-11 / published 2012-06-11; D1459666 filed 2012-07-18 / registered 2012-12-14 / published 2013-01-21. These pages were visually checked. This is further official corroboration for the later sealed bottle, not an original 1961 filing.

## Exclusions that matter

- **JP5053463B2** is a different detailed hinged rubber check-valve claim, with claim 2 explicitly reciting a film container. No direct match to the 2011 200mL bottle was established. Its 15–25° angle is between valve-lid side and valve-body interior: it is **not the 1961 dispensing-spout angle**. The related **US20130119092A1** is an application publication, not a granted US patent.
- Original design gazettes for D1443164/D1459666 were not acquired. The JPAA pamphlet corroborates the numbers and partial-design categories; it is not itself an original filing.
- **2018 3D trademark 6031041** belongs to a different kind of IP evidence. JPO workshop PDF p16 gives March 30 2018 registration and acquired-distinctiveness evidence. It establishes no functional patent or original 1961 filing. The current official IP protection page separately mentions a **2022** entire-bottle 3D trademark for the later sealed bottle.
- No current patent-register/annuity check or marketed-product claim chart was performed. Historical grant/publication dates and claims are safe; present enforceability and every-current-SKU use are not established.
