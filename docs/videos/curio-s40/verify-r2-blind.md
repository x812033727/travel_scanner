## Thirteen blind comprehension answers

1. **Where does the opening happen, and what is inspected?** Outside Cleopatra's Alexandrian tomb, guards inspect a villager's fig basket and see fruit beneath leaves (cl001-002, L9-13).
2. **What remains unseen in the teaser?** Whether the basket concealed a snake, what the return messenger will find behind the door, and whether the later triumph image represents the death mechanism (cl003-005, L17-25).
3. **How is Antony brought to Cleopatra?** He arrives wounded below the tomb; she and two attendants use ropes from above to pull him in, where he dies beside her in Plutarch's account (cl006-008, L31-39).
4. **How does Proculeius get into the shut tomb?** While another envoy speaks at the door, he and two others climb a ladder to a high window; he disarms Cleopatra of a dagger and searches for poison (cl014-017, L63-75).
5. **Why are guards assigned, and what happens with fasting?** Octavian orders gentle custody that prevents suicide. Plutarch reports she tries to starve and Octavian uses concern for her children to make her eat (cl018-021, L81-93).
6. **What is the omitted treasure story?** A steward reveals missing items on Cleopatra's inventory; she says she hoped to reserve small gifts for Octavian's family to plead for her, and Octavian concludes she wants to live (cl025-029, L109-125).
7. **Who brings the transfer warning?** Dolabella secretly tells her that Octavian will return by Syria and that she and her children will be sent away within three days (cl030-032, L129-139).
8. **What does she do before the basket returns?** With permission she visits Antony's tomb, expresses fear of being separated from him, places a garland and kisses the tomb, then bathes and eats (cl034-039, L147-167).
9. **What makes the fig basket pass the guard?** The carrier reveals large figs under leaves; guards praise them but take none, and admit the basket (cl040-043, L171-183).
10. **What does Octavian's messenger find?** After Cleopatra sends a sealed burial request and dismisses everyone but Iras and Charmion, the envoy finds Cleopatra dead in royal dress, Iras dying, and Charmion fixing her crown before dying; Octavian permits the burial with Antony (cl044-053, L189-225).
11. **What competing Plutarch mechanisms are described?** A rumored asp beneath figs biting her extended arm, an asp in a vessel touched with a golden spindle, or poison hidden in a hollow hairpin; Plutarch says the truth is unknown, with no observed snake or clear poison signs (cl055-064, L235-271).
12. **How does Dio's version differ?** Octavian moves Cleopatra from tomb to palace; she says she will sail, sends a sealed letter via a guard, then dies in royal dress. Dio cannot identify whether shallow arm marks reflect a snake concealed in a water jar/bouquet or a poisoned hairpin (cl067-076, L285-321).
13. **What does the ending establish?** Suetonius says snake bite was *believed* and Psylli were brought to try to extract poison; Plutarch describes a snake bearing image in the triumph. That image establishes a public version, while none of the three authors certifies the manner of death (cl079-084, L335-355).
