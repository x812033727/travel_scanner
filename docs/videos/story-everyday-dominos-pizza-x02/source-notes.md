## 可用主張與原文定位

1. **同店銷售是可比店口徑。** [S09，Item 6／Selected Financial Data 註 3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`including only sales from stores that also had sales in the comparable period of the prior year`，並排除部分 `seasonal locations such as stadiums and concert arenas`。不能把同店銷售增率改寫成公司總營收增率、全球銷售增率或「每一家店都增長」。
2. **美國本土同店銷售先跌後轉正。** [S09，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`Domestic same store sales decreased 1.7% in 2007, decreased 4.9% in 2008 and increased 0.5% in 2009.` 這是美國本土自營與加盟合計的可比店銷售，不能單看本表推定全球或公司營收。
3. **2007–08 的弱勢不只是一句「披薩難吃」。** [S09，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：公司列 `underperformance of our product and promotional offerings`、`continued challenges in our domestic business`、`a weak consumer environment`。原件未把 -4.9% 全歸因於配方。
4. **2009 的小幅轉正也涉及多款新品。** [S09，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`success of several initiatives`，列 BreadBowl Pasta、American Legends pizzas、Chocolate Lava Crunch Cakes，以及第四季的 Inspired New Pizza。不能把 FY2009 +0.5% 全部寫成核心披薩改配方帶來。
5. **變動觸及招牌產品的核心配方。** [S09，Item 1／Products](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`In the fourth quarter of 2009, we introduced our Inspired New Pizza, which was a change to our core pizza recipe.` 可說 2009 年第四季推出改良核心披薩配方；本原件未提供可供逐項複述的醬料／餅皮／起司比例。
6. **新配方與「Oh Yes We Did」同期宣傳。** [S09，Item 1／Advertising and Marketing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`In 2009, in connection with the launch of our Inspired New Pizza, we launched the campaign, “Oh Yes We Did.”` 申報只證實宣傳活動存在；未證實電視廣告裡出現哪些顧客批評鏡頭或原話。
7. **公司稱新品與宣傳獲得關注。** [S09，Item 1／Advertising and Marketing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`The launch of this product and this campaign has received significant attention in the news media, social networking internet sites and other media outlets.` 這是公司對曝光的敘述，不能當成每位消費者態度已轉變。
8. **專設網站兼做改良說明與回饋入口。** [S09，Item 1／Advertising and Marketing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：`www.pizzaturnaround.com` 用來 `highlight the improvements to our core pizza recipe and receive consumer feedback on the Inspired New Pizza`。不推定網站回饋直接決定後續所有配方變更。
9. **全國廣告分攤的配置同時調整。** [S09，Item 1／Advertising and Marketing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510045334/d10k.htm)：原件先說 2008 年 `each of our domestic stores contributed 4% of their retail sales` 用於全國廣告，且 `in addition to contributions for market-level advertising`，地方市場另有分攤；2009 年是 `our domestic stores within active co-operatives elected to allocate an additional 1% of their advertising contributions` 給全國宣傳，公司接著說結果是 `each of our domestic stores contributing 5% ... for national advertising in 2009`。須保留「活躍合作廣告組織的門市作額外 1% 配置」與「公司稱各美國本土門市全國廣告分攤達 5%」這兩層敘述；不能說所有門市各自增加了 1% 的**總廣告支出**。本點是輔助背景，非片中必要財務卡。
10. **2010 第一季美國本土同店銷售 +14.3%。** [S10Q1，Item 2／Results of Operations 表](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510105403/d10q.htm)：`Domestic stores 14.3 % 1.0 %`，欄序為截至 2010-03-28 與 2009-03-22 的第一財季；比較季 +1.0%。不能稱 2010 全年 +14.3%。
11. **增幅同時出現在美國本土自營與加盟可比店。** [S10Q1，Item 2／Results of Operations 表](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510105403/d10q.htm)：`Domestic Company-owned stores 14.7 % (0.1 )%`、`Domestic franchise stores 14.2 % 1.1 %`（兩欄同上）。可說兩類都上升，不可把 14.3% 當成只有自營門市。
12. **公司稱這是 IPO 以來最大的美國單季同店增幅。** [S10Q1，Item 2／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510105403/d10q.htm)：`largest quarterly domestic same store sales growth since our IPO`。須標明是 Domino’s 當時的表述，並限定為 2010 Q1。
13. **公司將新品推出與當季客流、銷售增長連起來。** [S10Q1，Item 2／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510105403/d10q.htm)：`This new product launch generated significant media attention and resulted in significant increases in traffic and sales in both our domestic Company-owned and franchise stores.` 這是管理層歸因，非對照試驗；不可說一支廣告單獨創造 +14.3%。
14. **公司不預期 +14.3% 的速度維持，仍預期下一季增長。** [S10Q1，Item 2／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312510105403/d10q.htm)：`we do not believe that the first quarter domestic same store sales growth rate will continue at this level`；緊接著公司稱當季動能 `will result in continued growth in the second quarter of 2010`。兩句都是 2010 年第一季時點的管理層展望，並非已觀測的 Q2 數字；不能改述為公司預告門市銷售下滑。
15. **2010 全年美國本土同店銷售 +9.9%。** [S10，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312511050979/d10k.htm)：`decreased 4.9% in 2008, increased 0.5% in 2009 and increased 9.9% in 2010`。這是全年可比店銷售，不能和 2010 Q1 +14.3% 當同一期間相加。
16. **公司對 2010 增長的歸因是組合。** [S10，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312511050979/d10k.htm)：`advertising campaign for our improved pizza recipe, continued focus on operational excellence and efforts to strengthen our franchisee base`；年報別處又寫 `new pizza recipe combined with an innovative and effective advertising campaign`。要說配方、宣傳、營運、加盟一起作用；不確定各因素獨立貢獻額度。
17. **2010 年的美國本土客流增長遍及四季。** [S10，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312511050979/d10k.htm)：`traffic increases in both our domestic Company-owned and franchise stores in all four quarters of 2010`。是公司描述的客流，不等於每家店每一季都有增長。
18. **2010 淨美國本土門市數也轉正。** [S10，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312511050979/d10k.htm)：`positive net domestic store growth for the first time since 2007`。此店數指標和同店銷售是兩件事，不應合成同一個百分比。
19. **2011 全年同店 +3.5%，站在 +9.9% 高基期上。** [S11，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312512083193/d256751d10k.htm)：`domestic same store sales rose 3.5% which was rolling over a very strong 9.9% same store sales growth in 2010`。不能說 2011 的 +3.5% 是相對 2008 基數。
20. **公司把 2011 改善連到多項投入。** [S11，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312512083193/d256751d10k.htm)：公司稱 `higher customer satisfaction, retention and purchase frequency`，並列 `investments to improve upon our menu, marketing, technology, operations and franchise base`。顧客滿意／留存是公司說法，沒有在此提供獨立調查；不能獨立宣稱因一支廣告使人回流。
21. **2011 另推其他產品。** [S11，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312512083193/d256751d10k.htm)：`including the launch of two improved chicken products, boneless chicken and wings, and the Domino’s Artisan pizzas`。2011 的 +3.5% 不能全歸於 2009 年那次核心披薩改良。
22. **公司認為 2010 年有首次下單與再次下單。** [S10，Item 7／Business Performance](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1286681/000119312511050979/d10k.htm)：`We believe our new pizza recipe resonated with existing and new customers and drove both trial and repeat orders throughout 2010`。這是管理層對新配方的歸因；原件沒有在此拆算首次／回購訂單的門市別數量或獨立實驗效果，不能改述成每位顧客都再次購買。

## 敘事邊界與六章主線

- **數字卡務必寫「美國本土同店銷售」。** FY2008 −4.9%、FY2009 +0.5%、2010 Q1 +14.3%、FY2010 +9.9%、FY2011 +3.5% 有不同期間；它們不是公司總營收、全球門市銷售或總門市成長。`same store` 定義見 #1；美國自營與加盟的對比見 #11。
- **原因不能歸一。** 年報把 2007–08 弱勢寫成產品／促銷、業務挑戰和消費環境；2010 改善是配方、廣告、營運與加盟等一起。若口播「新披薩帶動客流」須標明是公司歸因，不能稱已用獨立方法驗證每個因素。
- **不要演出未被原件證實的廣告鏡頭。** SEC 證明活動名和回饋網站，但沒有逐字記錄廣告裡是否播出顧客痛批、員工道歉、某個產品試吃反應。旁白、縮圖和提示詞不得把那些傳聞當確立事實。插圖只畫匿名、無招牌的製作及門市示意；品牌文字和年份留給後製卡片。
- **六章建議順序：** ① 2010 Q1 +14.3% 對 2008 −4.9% 的 30–60 秒濃縮反差；② 2007–09 弱勢和多重原因；③ 2009 Q4 動到核心配方、同時仍有其他新品；④ 宣傳活動、網站回饋和行銷資源；⑤ Q1 的跳升、公司自己不預期同速持續；⑥ FY2010 +9.9%、FY2011 +3.5% 與多因素收束。章末問題要等後續新事實回答，不能每一兩句假問答；結尾三句依序回答／留言／訂閱。
