# Read the 2026-09-28 09:00-09:50 UTC video review decisions from the production audit log

## Why

On 2026-09-28 the owner approved three final-cut cards on `/admin/videos` twice (reported at about
09:35 and 09:42 UTC) and the site recorded nothing for them; the third try (09:45-09:46 UTC, after
the session sent the exact titles) registered. Ticket `2026-09-28-admin-video-review-the-owner-s`
(closed 2026-10-01) showed with component tests that the review card sends a final-cut decision
to its own video and review and shows a refused one, and that every card on a video's page belongs
to that one video, both then and now. So the earlier clicks either went to other videos' pages
(another pipeline had final cuts in review that day) and approved those, or never left the
browser. Only the production audit log can tell which; the closing agent's read-only `psql` was
refused by the auto-mode classifier as a production read, so it needs the owner's go-ahead or a
Manual-mode session.

## Definition of done

- [ ] The list of `video_review_*` audit rows between 09:00 and 09:50 UTC on 2026-09-28 (time,
      action, review id, slug, gate) is written in Notes.
- [ ] Any final cut approved there that the owner did not mean to approve is named to the owner,
      who decides whether to reopen it (a new final review from the worker; approved reviews cannot
      be un-approved on the site).

## Steps

- [ ] One read-only query on the host, in Manual mode or after the owner agrees:
      `cd /root/travel_scanner && docker compose -f docker-compose.prod.yml exec -T postgres psql -U travel -d travel_scanner -Atc "select created_at, action, target, metadata_json::text from admin_audit_logs where action like 'video_review%' and created_at >= '2026-09-28 09:00Z' and created_at < '2026-09-28 09:50Z' order by created_at"`
- [ ] Compare the slugs with the three expected ones (`openai-agents-broke-in`,
      `gpt6-vs-opus55-worth-paying`, `ai-real-jobs-chart`, approved 09:45:12, 09:45:37, 09:46:24).
- [ ] If the rows show approvals of other videos' final cuts, consider a web ticket for the card
      to name its video (today it is titled only with the gate, 「成片」, and the video's title is
      at the top of the page, above the checklist and the language panel).
