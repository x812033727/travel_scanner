# Refresh the multi-vendor AI price pages and the 2026 release timeline for the late-September line-up

## Why

Split from `2026-10-05-review-the-openai-and-cross-vendor`, which updated the OpenAI, ChatGPT and
cross-vendor explainer pages for GPT-6 Sol/Luna, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. Four
zh-TW life pages were left because each one compares eight to ten vendors in one table, and a
correct update means re-reading every vendor's pricing page on the same day, not only OpenAI's and
Anthropic's:

- `ai-api-pricing-comparison-2026` (9/15) still calls Claude Opus 5 at $5/$25 the Anthropic flagship
  and `gpt-5.6-terra` the OpenAI mid-tier; its three price bands, the diagram and the
  10,000-conversation worked example are computed from those rows.
- `ai-model-comparison-table-2026` (9/16) lists Opus 5, Sonnet 5 and gpt-5.6-sol/terra/luna in its
  three tier tables. It is generated by `docs/content-research/ai-model-comparison-table-2026/build_pack.py`
  (edit the script and `notes.md`, then `reingest.sh`), not by hand.
- `ai-pricing-beyond-list-price` (9/17) has Opus 5 at a 10% cache hit (Opus 5.5 is 5%) and says
  OpenAI publishes no long-context threshold; the OpenAI pricing page now says short context is
  ≤272K input tokens. It is also built from `docs/content-research/ai-pricing-beyond-list-price/build_pack.py`.
- `ai-model-release-timeline-2026` covers 1 January to 15 September and says so; it is correct as a
  dated snapshot, but an evergreen "this year so far" timeline is missing Claude Opus 5.5 (09-22),
  GPT-6 Sol and Luna (09-22 US / 09-23 Taipei), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (09-28), GPT-6.1 Sol (09-29) and
  whatever the other seven vendors announced after 9/15.

## Definition of done

- [ ] The three price pages show each vendor's current line-up and prices from that vendor's own
      pricing page, read on the day of the edit, with every derived range, ratio, percentage and
      worked example recomputed and each source's `checked_on` bumped only where it was re-read.
- [ ] The timeline either extends past 2026-09-15 for all nine vendors it covers (title,
      description, table, diagram) or keeps its 9/15 cut-off; the choice is written in the notes.
- [ ] Diagrams in scope that show an old line-up are redrawn, `image.description` stays a verbatim
      copy of the SVG `<desc>`, and every diagram number is in the text.
- [ ] `pack_cli lint --kind life` and `intake_check.py --from-content` pass for each edited slug
      (pre-existing intake FAILs noted, not added to).

## Steps

- [ ] Re-read the pricing pages: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini API, xAI, DeepSeek, Mistral,
      Alibaba Model Studio, MiniMax pay-as-you-go, Moonshot Kimi, Z.ai, plus the model cards the
      comparison table cites (Hugging Face, Google DeepMind, Qwen, xAI, OpenAI).
- [ ] Update `build_pack.py` and `notes.md` for the two generated packs and run `reingest.sh`; edit
      `ai-api-pricing-comparison-2026` and its diagram by hand.
- [ ] Decide the timeline's cut-off; if extending, read each vendor's official announcement feed
      for 9/16 to the new cut-off and add the entries (sources are at the 20-item limit, so trim).
- [ ] Render the diagrams (`pack_cli lint --render-dir`, `CHROMIUM_BIN` = Edge) and look at them.
