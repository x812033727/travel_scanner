# Curio S02 S03 S04 S05 S23 S26 S28 S30 S34 S40: make the eight ticked languages from the original workdirs

## Why

The owner ticked all eight languages (en ja ko es pt id vi th: title and description, CC and dub) for these ten
奇聞檔案局 episodes. On the site every part has sat at "working" since 2026-10-19/20 and no languages batch exists.
They were produced outside the host worker (Codex, PR #1598, workdirs under `/workspace/.video-work/<slug>/`), so
the worker cannot make them: the per-line narration audio, timeline.json and final.mp4 the dub fits into exist
only in those workdirs. S03 S04 S05 S23 S26 S34 S40 are already on YouTube, so the dubs must fit that exact cut;
remaking narration or images elsewhere is not an option for them.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Each of the ten has a `languages` review on /admin/videos with the eight locales: description, captions and dub track (or a skip with its reason).

## Steps (per episode, in its original workdir)

- [ ] Write `languages.json` from the site's choice (`writeLanguages` in `tools/video/core/stages.mjs`).
- [ ] `i18n-sheet --locale en,ja,ko,es,pt,id,vi,th`, translate, `i18n-merge`. Merge rejects Han characters in
      non-ja descriptions; read every locale's merge line (a rejected description is silently left out).
- [ ] `lint --slug` must show no "bytes once composed" warning (Thai is 3 bytes a character; trim descriptions).
- [ ] `dub --locale <all eight> --line-by-line`; shorten the lines `dub` names until every window fits.
- [ ] `check-audio --locale <l>` per locale, then triage flags: most are transcriber noise on names. Real ones seen
      in the 2026-10-21 remake (task 2026-10-20-remake-ten-curio-episodes-in-the): Japanese kanji readings
      (甫庵→ふあん, 流馬→りゅうま, 降って→こうて; write them in hiragana), a stray "thought" spoken before a line
      (redo with `dub --redo <file> --keep-fitting`), mistranslations, and readings that sound crude.
- [ ] `captions`, `package`, `review-push --gate publish` (if none), `review-push --gate languages`.
