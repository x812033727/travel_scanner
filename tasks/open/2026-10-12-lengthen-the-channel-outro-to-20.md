# Lengthen the channel outro to 20 s with an end-screen canvas and music

## Why

The owner (2026-10-12) wants the end of every long video to look like the reference
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6FBmhdp_YA: about 20 s of a quiet moving background with
music, over which YouTube shows end-screen elements (other videos, subscribe). Today the channel
outro (`mokaair-brand-package-v2-cc`) is 3 s / 90 frames of like/share/bell, with no room for
end-screen elements and no music.

End-screen elements are YouTube Studio features: a video cannot draw clickable links, and the
YouTube Data API cannot set end screens. The video's job is the 20-second canvas and the music;
the elements are set once in Studio and copied to later uploads with "import from video".

The branding code already accepts an outro of 1-900 frames and offsets captions, chapters,
dubs and compilations by `checks.branding.outro_frames`, so no change to `core/branding.mjs` or
`assemble/branding.mjs` is needed (those belong to task 2026-10-12-play-the-channel-intro-bumper-after).

Owner decisions (2026-10-12):
- The picture is generated by a script in this repository.
- The music is a YouTube Audio Library track the owner picks from candidates Claude lists.
- End-screen elements: latest upload + best for viewer + subscribe.
- Applies to new long videos and to long videos not yet approved (rebuilt with --adopt-branding
  and resubmitted for manual review). Shorts unchanged.
