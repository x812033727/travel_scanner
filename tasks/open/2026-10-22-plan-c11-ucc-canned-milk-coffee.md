# Plan C11 UCC canned milk coffee with first-claim boundary

## Why

The twenty-episode everyday-reversals slate names UCC as C11 but its starting question can imply that the 1969 can opened like a modern pull-tab can. UCC says that original needed a two-hole opener and the pull tab arrived on its 1981 version. Municipal records also name a 1965 Mira canned coffee, so the unqualified world's-first claim needs a narrow boundary before any script is written.
