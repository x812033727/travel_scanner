# Clarify Area 51 title and body declassification scope

## Why

Independent review of the five-video opening remaster found that the existing
U02 title and some body wording can suggest that Area 51 first appeared in any
CIA document in 2013. The source material is narrower: a 2013 release removed
redactions from a particular U-2 program history, and the episode also cites a
1967 document. The new opening explicitly preserves that distinction, but this
remaster retains the existing title and chapter 2 onward.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Title, description and body consistently identify the particular 2013
      declassification without implying that no earlier document used the name.
- [ ] Any affected language package and published copy is reviewed and separately
      authorized before replacement; original media remains preserved.

## Steps

- [ ] Review exact assertions against the National Security Archive source and
      the episode's cited 1967 document; identify the smallest wording correction.
- [ ] Propose source-bound wording and affected lines before synthesizing or
      changing a live title/description.

## How to verify

Compare the four-language wording with the primary documents. If media changes,
run the usual source-bound audio, captions, language-package and final-media QA.
Do not treat the 2026-10-12 opening review as a reapproval of the unchanged body.
