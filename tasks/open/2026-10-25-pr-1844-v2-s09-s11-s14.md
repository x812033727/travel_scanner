### 13:20 disk note

`package` copies `final.mp4` into `upload/final.mp4` (about 250 MB per episode). When the two are byte-identical (`cmp -s`), `ln -f final.mp4 upload/final.mp4` frees that copy without changing either file; doing it for 15 packaged episodes took free space from 2.6 GB to 6.0 GB. `upload/dubs/*.m4a` is not the same file as `dubs/<loc>/body.m4a`, so leave those alone.

### 21:30 progress and lessons

- Gemini voice quota ran out again 14:47-21:00 UTC. The 4-tries-then-skip lane would have skipped every remaining locale, so the dub lanes were restarted with a 15-minute wait for up to 15 hours (`lane_dubs_wait.sh`); they resumed by themselves.

### 06:05 background lanes die when the session goes idle

Three times overnight (20:09, 01:34, 04:03 UTC) every background lane stopped a few minutes after the last Monitor was stopped and the session went quiet: the cloud container is reclaimed when the session is idle, and that kills all background processes. Lanes kept running for days while a lanes.log Monitor was armed and re-armed on each expiry. So keep a Monitor armed for as long as lanes should run, and relaunch with `restart_all.sh <slug groups>` (scratchpad) after any restart: it clears stale LEASE files, restarts the lanes, the rechecks and the disk cleaner.
