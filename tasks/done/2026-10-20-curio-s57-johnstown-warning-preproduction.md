**Disposition: reject for S57.** Parke's quoted account in Fox says he sent two men to the telegraph tower at noon; surrounding reporter prose says Parke personally telegraphed, and Fox says Johnstown was two miles below South Fork. Johnson names freight agent Dechert as sender and also prints Parke saying everyone *should* have known by noon. Johnson and Walker reproduce some Dechert/Davis and Ogle passages nearly verbatim, so their agreement is not independent corroboration. Ogle's reported 8:00/11:00/12:33 river-gauge telegrams document flooding but do not prove who in town received the dam warning. The popular explanation that residents ignored a known warning rests on reporter generalization and cannot be assigned to individual victims. `www.nps.gov` was attempted but blocked by the network proxy (`CONNECT 403`). A future attempt would need an accessible original dispatch log or museum/NPS source before scripting this causal question. No S57 creative file or paid-media call was made for Johnstown.
