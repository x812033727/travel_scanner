## Why

Split from `2026-10-05-review-the-openai-and-cross-vendor`, which updated the OpenAI, ChatGPT and
cross-vendor explainer pages for GPT-6 Sol/Luna, GPT-6.1 Sol, Claude Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5. Four
zh-TW life pages were left because each one compares eight to ten vendors in one table, and a
correct update means re-reading every vendor's pricing page on the same day, not only OpenAI's and

  and `gpt-5.6-terra` the OpenAI mid-tier; its three price bands, the diagram and the
  10,000-conversation worked example are computed from those rows.

  three tier tables. It is generated by `docs/content-research/ai-model-comparison-table-2026/build_pack.py`

  OpenAI publishes no long-context threshold; the OpenAI pricing page now says short context is
  ≤272K input tokens. It is also built from `docs/content-research/ai-pricing-beyond-list-price/build_pack.py`.

  GPT-6 Sol and Luna (09-22 US / 09-23 Taipei), Claude Sonnet 5.5 (09-28), GPT-6.1 Sol (09-29) and
