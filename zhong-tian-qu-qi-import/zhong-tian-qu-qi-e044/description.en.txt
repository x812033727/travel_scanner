Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 44 | CC

In the sealing room, Examiner Feng locks the wooden box and pinches out the candle. The next day, at a street corner in the prefectural city, Qin Shuang pushes up her bamboo hat to look over a stall of hunting bows, and the crutch of a lame old soldier mending shoes clatters to the ground: "Young General?" Propping himself against the wall on his one good leg, he bows to her in a military salute. Qin Shuang turns and walks away. Prefecture patrolmen hear the shout and close in, and Xu Yan throws an arm around the old soldier: "That's my wife. Looks like her, huh?" In a tavern, the old soldier, Du Tie, downs two bowls of wine and tells the story of the Qin family: her father is Qin Lieshan, the General Guardian of the North; the court says he sold the border defense maps to Shuomo, the Qin house was seized, and everyone scattered. Du Tie promises to find out for Xu Yan where the General is. That night, Qin Shuang sees the white jade pendant on the table, goes deathly pale and snatches it up: "Where did you get this?" Then she sets it down: "It's nothing." Standing at the window with her back to him, she asks: "If I'm a convict's daughter, would you still want me?" "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 44.
