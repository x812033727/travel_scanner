A-Ling points at the vermilion seal on the copy of the warrant: "On the real seal, the character 'Zhou' — this stroke is broken." Tang Xiaotang starts to ask how she knows, but Xu Yan raises a hand to stop her: "No need to ask. I trust her." To prove the warrant is forged, they need a prefecture document that is certainly genuine to compare it with. Du Tie, a veteran of the Qin army, comes hobbling up on his crutch, and Xu Yan asks him to keep watch on the prefecture jail; Tang Xiaotang goes to plead with the prefect's wife. In the prefecture's court hall the prefect is away, and the vice-prefect presiding insists he watched the great seal stamped with his own eyes, then turns nasty and orders them thrown out. Xu Yan pulls his own Jieyuan results notice from his robe — stamped with the prefecture's genuine great seal. Held together before the candle flame, the two red seals line up in the light, all but one stroke that is off, like a crack. The prefect's wife sends for Prefect Chu to come back, and the prefect signs a recall order on the spot. Xu Yan says: "It was my wife who spotted it." And to Tang Xiaotang: "Without you, we'd have lost today." The words are barely out when Du Tie bursts into the hall: the prison cart went out the North Gate before dawn, and the men escorting it aren't the prefecture's. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 52.
