The night the results are posted, before Qin Shuang can get her words out, a row of people is already kneeling at the inn door: Squire Zhou, who once broke off the original Xu Yan's engagement, with his daughter beside him and ginseng, silver and a jade ruyi held high: "Honored Jieyuan, we were blind!" Tang Xiaotang's eyes light up: three hundred taels, at least. Xu Yan helps Squire Zhou to his feet: "That old debt isn't mine to settle. The gifts, please take them back." Headmaster Cheng brings a gift that Prefect Chu asked him to deliver, and leaves just one piece of advice: "The tallest tree catches the wind." At the celebration feast, his clan cousin Xu Dalang raises a smiling toast, but his eyes keep sliding over the rim of his cup toward Qin Shuang. In the guest room upstairs, Qin Shuang asks for the jade and, under the lamp, lays it in Xu Yan's palm: "This was my father's. My father is Qin Lieshan." He wore it close for twenty years and never took it off — so how it ended up with the original Xu Yan, she doesn't know either. Xu Yan takes her hand: "When we find him, I'll give it back to him myself." The words are barely out when, downstairs, the wine cups stop one by one, yamen runners with torches pack the stairs, and the door is kicked in: "Qin Shuang, a disgraced official's daughter, deserting the military register — seize her!" At the very back of the crowd stands Xu Dalang. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 50, end of Season 1, Chapter 5.
