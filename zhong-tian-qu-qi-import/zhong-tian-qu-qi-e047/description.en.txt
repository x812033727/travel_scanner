Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 47 | CC

In the prefectural hall, Wei Xun has written out Xu Yan's essay from memory without a single word wrong, Examiner Feng has sneered, "False accusation against an examiner: thirty strokes," and the rods are already raised. Xu Yan sets down his brush: "The waterwheel in part three — you draw it." Prefect Chu allows it. In a few quick strokes Xu Yan draws twelve blades while the candidates below crane their necks on tiptoe to watch; Wei Xun grips his brush and manages only a lopsided circle on the blank paper. When the prefect asks why the blades are mounted backward, Wei Xun blurts out, "What I memorized…" Xu Yan can explain it: the first time he put them in backward, and only when his little sister held up a paper pinwheel did he flip them around. Headmaster Cheng rises to testify that before the exam Xu Yan drew this very waterwheel in his study. Examiner Feng tries to bluster on, and Tang Xiaotang walks in from outside the hall holding up a folded paper packet: she remembered the face of the Wei servant who handed Feng a folded note before the exam; the man came back with a second packet, and she intercepted it. Inside is a money draft stamped with a private seal: five hundred taels, paid in full when the results are posted. And that seal Tang Xiaotang knows: it belongs to Wei Shaoqing's steward. Examiner Feng is seized on the spot. In the back hall, Prefect Chu sends everyone out and pushes the money draft across to Xu Yan: go after the Wei family, and the case goes to the capital and drags on for three years; charge only Examiner Feng, and tomorrow you are Jieyuan, first in the provincial exam.
