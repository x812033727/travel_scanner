Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 46 | CC

Blow after blow, the great drum at the prefecture gate booms: "I, Xu Yan, a student—my exam paper was switched!" In the hall, Examiner Feng insists that the handwriting on the top paper on the list is Wei Xun's: "Slandering an examiner: thirty strokes, and stripped of your title!" Xu Yan looks Prefect Chu straight in the eye: "Handwriting can be copied, but an essay can't be. Your Honor, give us paper and brush, and he and I will write it from memory, right here." Examiner Feng wants him thrown out, and the prefect stops him with one sentence: "Whoever beats the drum, this court will hear him." Tang Xiaotang and Qin Shuang push through the crowd and bring Headmaster Cheng, who stood as Xu Yan's guarantor, to listen. Two desks stand side by side; Wei Xun takes up his brush first, and his very first line matches Xu Yan's essay word for word. Qin Shuang sees at a glance the dark circles under his eyes: he didn't sleep all night. Wei Xun writes the last character and slaps his brush down on the desk: "Word for word." Examiner Feng sneers: "Thirty strokes!" The yamen runners raise their staffs. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 46.
