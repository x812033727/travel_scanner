Early on results day, the red list rolls down from the top of the prefectural city gate in one go, and the herald starts reading from the last name: "Eightieth place—" Squire Zhou pushes through the crowd with a sneer: "If he passes, I'll walk backwards!" Tang Xiaotang gives a sweet smile: "It's a deal: backwards down a whole street." Halfway through the reading, prefecture constables strike Examiner Feng's name off the examiners' notice with one stroke, in front of everyone. But whether the list tampered with the night before was ever changed back, no one knows — tenth place, third place, second place, and still no Xu Yan. Squire Zhou laughs out loud, and Tang Xiaotang all but bites through her fingernail. The last one: "Jieyuan — Xu Yan, of Liuxi, Qingshui County!" The whole street goes silent for a beat, then erupts. The System: provincial exam mission complete, reward: movable-type printing — but it can't be made until he finds a type carver. Squire Zhou walks backwards through the laughter of the whole street, and he is only halfway when Qin Shuang folds her arms: "The whole street, not one step short." At the Jieyuan's parade, Xu Yan rides a white horse wearing a red silk flower; Qin Shuang walks up beside the horse, takes the reins and looks up at him, her eyes wet: "Tonight, I have something to tell you." "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 49.
