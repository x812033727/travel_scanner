Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 51 | CC

On the night of the Jieyuan, prefecture yamen runners kick in the door of the inn room: "Qin Shuang, a disgraced official's daughter, deserting the military register — seize her!" Qin Shuang snatches up the red-tasseled spear by the door, and the lead runner already has his blade half out of its sheath. Xu Yan presses his hand over hers on the spear: "Go with them. I'll come for you." Fight here, and she really becomes a fugitive. Qin Shuang looks at him for three seconds, leans the spear back against the table, and holds out her own wrists. The System pops up a mandatory mission: rescue Qin Shuang within three days; on failure, Qin Shuang's affection resets to zero. That night, Tang Xiaotang uses Tang family silver to buy a copy of the arrest warrant. At first light, a galloping horse tears down the long street of the prefectural city: A-Ling has ridden all night from Liuxi. Tang Xiaotang also brings news from the capital: a month ago, Wei Shaoqing went back to the capital with a painter. A-Ling takes the copy of the warrant and gives the vermilion seal on it a single glance: "This seal is fake." "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 51.
