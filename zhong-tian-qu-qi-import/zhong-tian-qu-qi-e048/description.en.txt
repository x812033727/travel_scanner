In the prefect's back hall, Prefect Chu has offered Xu Yan two roads. Xu Yan folds the money draft stamped with the Wei family's private seal and tucks it into his sleeve: "Only Examiner Feng. This one, I'll keep." The prefect gives a rare smile: "Young man, you keep a cool head." But the results list has already gone out, and whether it can be changed back, they will only know tomorrow. Back in the inn courtyard, Tang Xiaotang demands to know why he didn't accuse the Wei family; Xu Yan says that suing now would only drag them down, and this paper waits until it's worth the most. Tang Xiaotang's eyes light up: "No capital spent, interest on interest." Then she holds up three fingers: thirty percent of the interest is hers, and the gold abacus at her waist clicks a bead across. Late at night, the old soldier Du Tie knocks at the back gate: "The General is alive, in the salt mines of the northern frontier." It was Xu Yan who asked him to find out. Qin Shuang spins around, runs to the veranda and punches the red pillar, leaving a fist mark in the wood; Xu Yan sits down beside her, says not a word, and just hands her a piece of white sugar. That same night, under a red lantern in a dark alley, Xu Yan's clan cousin Xu Dalang bows low to the Wei steward: "That Qin girl is Qin Lieshan's daughter." The steward presses a pouch of silver into his hand. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 48.
