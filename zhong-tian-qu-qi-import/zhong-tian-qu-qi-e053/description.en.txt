Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 53 | CC

The prison cart went out the North Gate before dawn, and the men escorting it aren't the prefecture's. A-Ling wheels her horse around and hauls Xu Yan up onto its back in one pull: "Hold on." Xu Yan can't ride a horse; all he can do is grip the saddle and shout: "Charge!" Erniu and the boys from Liuxi come catching up after two days on muleback. On the shallows of the northern ford, the prison cart stands in the river; the escort's leader, Sergeant Deng, draws his saber, and a dozen armed men surround the cage. Xu Yan isn't trying to win the fight, only to get the spear into her hands — and a red-tasseled spear goes flying into the cage. Qin Shuang catches it and pries the cage door open; water explodes across the shallows, and two sabers fly into the air at once and drop into the river. Xu Yan raises the recall order, and the other side hesitates. Sergeant Deng grabs A-Ling's reins: "I know who she is." Xu Yan cuts him off: "Who she is, is hers to say." Sergeant Deng backs away to the water's edge, pulls a letter from his robe and waves it at Qin Shuang: "Let me go, and you get the letter to your father." "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 53.
