Standing at the window with her back to him, Qin Shuang asks: "If I'm a convict's daughter, would you still want me?" Xu Yan steps up behind her: "You're Qin Shuang, that's all that matters to me." Her affection meter dings up to fifty-two, and she turns around, smiling through red eyes: "Idiot." She tells him that calling herself a soldier's orphan was a lie, and asks him not to let a third person know. The door wasn't shut tight: at the turn of the inn's staircase, Xu Dalang presses against the wall and takes in every word Qin Shuang says. The next day Tang Xiaotang can't wait for the results to be posted. With a box of white sugar she buys over the wife of the results-office clerk, and that night she sneaks a look at the draft list. The candlelight runs all the way down, and the name "Xu Yan" is nowhere to be found. She rushes back to the inn, pale as a sheet: "Your name isn't on the list. The top of the list is Wei Xun, and his policy essay is about waterwheels." "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 45.
