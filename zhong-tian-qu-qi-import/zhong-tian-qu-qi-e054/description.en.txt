Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 54 | CC

Sergeant Deng waves "a letter to your father" and demands that Qin Shuang let him go. Qin Shuang says only: "The letter — I'll take it myself." One sweep of her spear shaft and Sergeant Deng goes down in the shallows; the letter flies high into the air, and A-Ling reaches out and catches it. Xu Yan binds Sergeant Deng with the iron chain from his own belt. The letter is opened: inside is a blank sheet of paper — kneeling in the river, Qin Shuang finally sobs out loud. That night, by a campfire on the riverbank, sparks drifting up into a sky full of stars, she tells Xu Yan everything for the first time: on the day her father was convicted, he gave the jade pendant he always wore to his most trusted guard to carry away; that man never came back, and his last letter said he had reached Liuxi in Qingzhou. Her mother, never hearing any news, died of grief — she lies in the nameless grave in the bamboo grove. Xu Yan says: "Your father's name — we'll clear it together." For the first time, she rests her head on his shoulder. Then she wipes away her tears and points at the empty air in front of him: "You keep smiling at that spot. What's over there?" A-Ling turns her head too, waiting for his answer. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 54.
