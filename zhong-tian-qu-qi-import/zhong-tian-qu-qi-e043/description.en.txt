Farm, Marry, Conquer Ep. 43 | CC

Two days before the exam, someone has withdrawn Xu Yan's guarantee bond. Tang Xiaotang slaps a stack of Liuxi white paper down on the desk of Headmaster Cheng of the prefectural academy: "This paper, he made it." The headmaster tests him on beating a drought, and Xu Yan picks up a brush and draws a waterwheel: the river is down low, the fields are up high, so let the water climb up on its own. Liuxi tore down the shrine's beams to build that wheel. The headmaster vouches for him on the spot, then dresses down the registrar who pulled the bond, right at the registration desk. On exam day, at the examination hall gate, Tang Xiaotang sees Examiner Feng take a folded note from a servant in Wei household clothes and tuck it into his sleeve. Xu Yan's exam cell leaks, and he shields his paper with his own body through all three sessions. Late that night in the sealing room, Examiner Feng pulls Xu Yan's paper from the pile.
