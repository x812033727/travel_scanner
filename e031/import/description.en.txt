A gust of wind tears A-Ling's veil away, and Wei Shaoqing freezes in the doorway: "You're… you're…" She pulls the veil back over her face, her voice as cold as well water: "You've got the wrong person." He backs up two steps, mounts his horse and rides off. That night she sits on the rim of the well, the moonlight turning her face almost translucent, and says only, "He saw me once, inside very high walls," and "Don't ask again." Xu Yan nods: "Fine. I won't." And her affection goes up. Not one drop of rain in three months. Xu Yan wraps the rice seed from a reward chest in damp cloth, and the very day the white sprouts show, three days later, Liuxi Creek runs dry; the creek bed cracks all the way to the horizon, while Squire Qian shuts his gates, hoards grain and raises prices. The System issues a drought mission: get water to Liuxi's fields within ten days. Fail, and the seedlings die. The villagers follow the creek upstream and find the Qian household building a dam to divert the last of the water into Qian fields. Erniu charges with his hoe raised, and a dozen staves rise to meet him. "Farm, Marry, Conquer" — Episode 31, the start of the fourth arc of Season 1.
